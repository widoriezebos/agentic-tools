## 2. Findings

**A1; MATERIAL; Part 2; Gate 1, “What the code already offers” and `g1-s6`: the proposed health read changes engine behavior.**

The plan lists `steward.ObserveHealth` as an available Fleet read. It acquires a write lock, evaluates health, advances the observation, and saves the record ([metasystem/internal/steward/health.go:285](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/steward/health.go:285)). A dead-role observation increments its persistent failure count; enough observations mark automatic healing ended ([health.go:692](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/steward/health.go:692)). This is consumed operationally: `AutoHealingEnded` reads that count, and runner repair returns without repairing when it is set ([health.go:1738](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/steward/health.go:1738), [runner.go:554](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/steward/runner.go:554)).

Inference: implementing Fleet refresh with the named function would let browser observation frequency advance the engine's repair breaker. This violates Gate 1's read-only contract, with an effect beyond the interface. **Fix direction:** add an owner-exported read of the retained health verdict and its original observation time, reporting absence explicitly; keep periodic observation and escalation with their existing owner.

**A2; MATERIAL; Part 3, S2 proposal; “confined to humanauthority” and unchanged predicate consumers: admission is only half the authority contract.**

The ordinary `requireHuman` gate can accept a full-standing session proof through the existing predicates ([metasystem/internal/goal/verbs.go:305](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/verbs.go:305)). Downstream consumers still require terminal facts. `Carry` admits through `ValidFor`, passes `TerminalGeneration` into its transaction, and records it in History; consumption later requires a positive generation, including independently in landing ([verbs.go:4512](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/verbs.go:4512), [verbs.go:4624](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/verbs.go:4624), [verbs.go:4161](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/verbs.go:4161), [verbs.go:4720](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/verbs.go:4720), [metasystem/internal/landing/carried.go:273](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/landing/carried.go:273)). A browser carry could therefore publish successfully and be unusable.

There are also actual process-dependent consumers. `WriteSessionStop` copies `proof.InvokerRef` into the marker, validates that process identity, and later requires the attending process to remain alive ([metasystem/internal/goal/sessionstop.go:258](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/sessionstop.go:258), [sessionstop.go:150](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/sessionstop.go:150), [sessionstop.go:506](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/sessionstop.go:506)). Human handoff cancellation also requires and records `InvokerRef` after accepting `TerminalValidFor` ([metasystem/internal/steward/handoff_capture.go:1213](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/steward/handoff_capture.go:1213), [handoff_capture.go:1236](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/steward/handoff_capture.go:1236)). Substituting the interface server's PID would falsely identify its lifetime as human attendance.

**Fix direction:** preserve full human standing, but extend these owners to consume source-specific authority evidence and, where required, session presence; do not manufacture terminal generations or human process identities.

**A3; MATERIAL; Part 3, S2 proposal; session provenance in History: the promised record requires changes outside the proof package and a fleet compatibility decision.**

Existing approval recording has a branch for channel provenance but no session branch ([metasystem/internal/goal/approval.go:408](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/approval.go:408)). `HistoryLine` has channel fields and terminal generation, with no session source or sign-in issuer ([metasystem/internal/goal/file.go:438](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/file.go:438)). The rejection reaches fleet synchronization: History parse errors become file problems, `ValidateCommit` rejects the tree, and `FetchAdvance` validates before advancing the accepted ref ([file.go:570](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/file.go:570), [metasystem/internal/goal/validate.go:672](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/validate.go:672), [metasystem/internal/goal/fetchadvance.go:65](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/fetchadvance.go:65)). **A4; MATERIAL; Parts 2 and 3; `g2-s3`, master “Clone, publication, and freshness,” and S1's claim that Gate 2 is unaffected: two processes need one publication admission boundary.**

With the interface and brain both operating on the declared checkout, a mutex inside either process cannot serialize their publications and recovery. The existing journal does not supply that whole-operation exclusion. `Publish` checks `PushedBlocking` at admission, creates an entry, and starts the transaction ([metasystem/internal/goal/txn.go:549](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/txn.go:549)). `CreateEntry` and `MarkPushed` lock only their individual journal updates ([metasystem/internal/goal/journal.go:231](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/journal.go:231), [journal.go:370](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/journal.go:370)). The later push proceeds without repeating the admission check, and an unknown transport outcome leaves its entry pushed ([txn.go:743](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/txn.go:743), [txn.go:812](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/txn.go:812)).

Inference: both processes can pass admission before either is pushed; one can then continue publishing after the other becomes pushed-unknown. Remote CAS does not enforce the promised local admission block. **Fix direction:** make Gate 2's shared command owner serialize admission, publication, and relevant recovery across processes for a checkout, with the same boundary used by HTTP, brain tools, and CLI; use a separate operation lock rather than recursively holding the journal's existing lock.

**A5; MATERIAL; Part 3, S1 proposal; “one brain seat per fleet”: the existing declaration enforces a smaller scope.**

The registry defaults to the local user's home and stores the fleet pointer there ([metasystem/internal/brain/brain.go:228](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/brain/brain.go:228)). `Declare` locks that local pointer and explicitly enforces “one brain per host per fleet” ([brain.go:272](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/brain/brain.go:272)). MAIN announcements and their lease belong to a checkout ([metasystem/internal/lease/verbs.go:111](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/lease/verbs.go:111), [verbs.go:446](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/lease/verbs.go:446)).

Inference: two hosts can each satisfy the existing declaration and occupancy rules while running different brains for the same fleet. The implementer must otherwise guess whether “one” is a convention or an enforced fleet contract. **Fix direction:** designate one canonical fleet brain home that both terminal and hosted startup validate, retain the checkout lease for occupancy there, and defer relocation and automatic failover.

**A6; MATERIAL; Part 2; `g1-s2`–`g1-s7` freshness contract: an accepted-tip change is insufficient to invalidate all read models.**

The master names local publication and remote tip changes as invalidation triggers (`plans/designs/user-interface-design.md:250`); the plan adds snapshots, local readers, and an invalidation-only event stream without assigning refresh responsibility for non-ledger changes. Questions are read from local JSON files under `artifacts/agents/channel/questions`, independently of the accepted ref ([metasystem/internal/channel/question.go:80](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/channel/question.go:80), [question.go:113](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/channel/question.go:113)). Health changes independently too, as A1 establishes.

Even ledger-derived answers can change with an unchanged tip. `Project` captures an approval horizon using the supplied time; expiry compares that time with the review date, and the next-work projection excludes expired approval from readiness ([metasystem/internal/goal/project.go:92](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/project.go:92), [metasystem/internal/goal/approval.go:253](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/approval.go:253), [metasystem/internal/goal/file.go:290](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/file.go:290), [project.go:692](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/project.go:692)). Inference: caching the entire projection until its tip changes can keep yesterday's authority state; tip-only events can leave an open Decisions or Fleet view unchanged indefinitely.

**Fix direction:** assign the existing refresh tick responsibility for local read changes and time-dependent reevaluation, cache immutable tree data separately from observation-time projections, and invalidate affected views even when the ledger tip is unchanged.

**A7; MATERIAL; Part 3; S10's proposed recovery explanation: a foreign live owner's deadline does not unblock the checkout.**

S10 says the clone remains blocked “until that owner's deadline” (`plans/user-interface-design-critique-r2.md:91`). With no decisive postcondition, `ClassifyRecovery` returns `leave-to-owner` for a foreign live owner before it examines the deadline; only the caller's own entry reaches `expire-own` ([metasystem/internal/goal/journal.go:556](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/journal.go:556)). Recovery leaves that foreign entry untouched, and `PushedBlocking` has no deadline exemption ([metasystem/internal/goal/recover.go:123](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/recover.go:123), [journal.go:508](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/journal.go:508)).

Inference: a deadline countdown that promises release, or a test expecting another process's Recover to expire that entry, would be wrong. **Fix direction:** keep S10's additional dispositions, but say that a foreign live owner's unresolved entry remains blocked until evidence resolves it or ownership/liveness permits recovery; passing its deadline alone is insufficient.

**A8; MATERIAL; Part 2; coverage mapping: two assignments can certify work before the required behavior exists.**

`UID-R1-05` is assigned solely to `g2-s4` (`plans/user-interface-implementation-plan.md:182`), whose scope is preparation for goal edit. The actual obligation requires withdrawal preview to name the standing claim that will park (`plans/designs/user-interface-design.md:861`); withdrawal belongs to Gate 5 (`plans/designs/user-interface-design.md:792`). Acceptance scenario 1 is assigned to Gate 3 (`plans/user-interface-implementation-plan.md:202`), but includes editing and approving work through controls (`plans/designs/user-interface-design.md:806`); those browser paths arrive in Gates 4 and 5.

These are document-contract mismatches, requiring no inference about engine behavior. Following the mapping would permit a generic preview test or sign-in test to discharge an unbuilt domain/browser outcome. **Fix direction:** distinguish foundation evidence from final discharge, assign the withdrawal obligation to Gate 5, and retain scenario 1 through the gate that actually supplies its approval flow.

## 3. S1 and S2

**S1 — what holds.** A brain process independent of the optional interface is a coherent owner for the agent session, sitting store, budgets, and application tools. One logical seat with many conversation clients is a reasonable product choice; the existing code does not establish fleet-wide uniqueness, as A5 shows.

The lease can represent a hosted process: announcement accepts a live readable process identity, and holder authorization checks the resulting MAIN against the checkout lease ([metasystem/internal/lease/verbs.go:101](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/lease/verbs.go:101), [verbs.go:465](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/lease/verbs.go:465)). However, announcement success alone does not prove occupancy: a competing live holder can make the claim return without transferring the lease, while announcement still succeeds ([metasystem/internal/lease/claim.go:117](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/lease/claim.go:117), [verbs.go:217](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/lease/verbs.go:217)). Hosted startup must verify that it actually holds the lease before enabling tools.

The brain context is reusable. `PhaseOne` reads declaration and role instructions independently of the interface, and the existing boot path has a delivery acknowledgment that checks the declaration and advances the delivered digest ([metasystem/internal/brain/brain.go:371](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/brain/brain.go:371), [metasystem/cmd/metasystem/brain_boot.go:563](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/cmd/metasystem/brain_boot.go:563)). Removing the server-owned clone is supportable for ledger work: reads load the accepted tree, and transactions build through an isolated index containing explicit changes ([metasystem/internal/goal/project.go:83](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/project.go:83), [metasystem/internal/goal/txn.go:252](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/txn.go:252)). This justification does not require assuming the declaration guarantees a permanently untouched worktree.

**S1 — what does not hold, and my alternative.** Gate 2 is affected by the second process, and the fleet home needs the explicit contract in A5. I would make the stable brain host process the single MAIN occupant, with provider sessions subordinate to it. Put the application tool endpoint in that process so the brain works with the interface stopped; have both processes call the shared Go command/read owners. The interface owns browser authentication and connects its dock to the brain. Human credentials never become tool credentials. This is a design recommendation, not a claim about an existing hosted service.

Gate 1 can proceed with its current checkout scope. Gate 2 must establish the shared process-safe publication and principal contracts. Gate 3 should deliver the independently operable brain service and then its authenticated browser connection, retaining its isolation and budget proofs. Moving all of Gate 3 into the brain would incorrectly move browser sign-in and human-session ownership with it. I assume “every fleet must have a brain” requires a durable declared home and an explicitly started occupant; unattended event-driven turns remain an unresolved requirement.

**S2 — what holds.** A server-verified human session can have full human standing without a new grade or a browser-specific verb permission table. The existing root-bound, unexported observation state supports refusing parsed proof JSON; the ordinary human gates can retain their existing decision structure ([metasystem/internal/humanauthority/authority.go:102](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/humanauthority/authority.go:102), [authority.go:196](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/humanauthority/authority.go:196), [metasystem/internal/goal/verbs.go:305](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/verbs.go:305)). The new constructor must receive the server's verified principal and bind the observation to its root and current authenticated request.

The channel precedent is broader than approval-only: verified channel answers also reach grant and revoke, while the authority package admits channel evidence for set-obligation and resume ([verbs.go:1530](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/verbs.go:1530), [verbs.go:1592](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/verbs.go:1592), [metasystem/internal/humanauthority/authority.go:221](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/humanauthority/authority.go:221), [authority.go:233](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/humanauthority/authority.go:233)). `refuseRelayedAfterFleetEnrollment` excludes temporary relay, rather than every nonterminal source ([metasystem/internal/goal/approval.go:424](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/goal/approval.go:424)). A verified full-standing session therefore need not bypass that relay restriction.

**S2 — what does not hold, and my alternative.** The package-only implementation promise fails at A2 and A3. I would separate authority standing from evidence source, retain the existing standing for a verified session, and extend the few owners that consume terminal evidence directly. Carry needs durable proof of the human's authorization that its consumers understand. Session-stop needs an authenticated-session presence binding while preserving its holder, expiry, and lifecycle checks. Actual terminal enrollment still needs terminal observation: its implementation establishes a terminal identity and generation ([metasystem/internal/humanauthority/authority.go:780](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/metasystem/internal/humanauthority/authority.go:780)). Browser authentication cannot supply those process facts merely by granting human standing. Record session source, user, and issuer through an explicit compatible history design; keep issuance confined to the human request boundary and brain requests bound to the brain principal.
