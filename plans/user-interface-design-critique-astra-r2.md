## 2. Findings

**B1; MATERIAL; Part 2; master “The brain,” continuity, and gate 3: attendance has no end condition.**

The master requires the occupant to exist only while a human sits, but also makes the brain independent of the interface and says closing the browser does not cancel authorized work ([plans/designs/user-interface-design.md:65](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/plans/designs/user-interface-design.md:65), [:386](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools-ui/plans/designs/user-interface-design.md:386)). Neither it nor gate 3 distinguishes retained conversation state, a live process, and current human attendance.

Inference: an implementer can legitimately keep starting agent turns after the last human connection disappears, or kill the brain whenever one of several tabs closes. Explicit startup, an unexpired login, the checkout lease, and remaining budget do not answer when the human has left. **Fix direction:** define the sitting’s attendance lifecycle across browser and terminal connections, including disconnect, sign-out, interface shutdown, and reconnection, and enforce it at agent-turn/tool admission while preserving already accepted domain operations.

## 4. Consequences of settled decisions

- **Human authority is shared, including revocation.** Existing powers of attorney have a grantor, tiers, verbs, and lifetime, but no recipient-seat or recipient-human field.
