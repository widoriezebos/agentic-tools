**Proof and red.** New `plain.PolicyDecision` is the small decision function used at batch selection, full proof start, red continuation, return and trunk-red admission. It does not classify tests. `landing.proof=auto` uses 1a's gate/full/trunk ladder and retained evidence. `person` asks only before each full proof execution, including a repeat, replay full proof or explicitly requested trunk full proof. Each merge gate remains automatic within the admitted batch and does not ask separately. A lane helm suppresses the periodic trunk timer; an explicit trunk proof remains available. Reusing a recorded green spends no proof act. Extend the existing `Running` proof record with actor provenance and the policy subject admitted for its attempt/tree/rung. The parent proof verb writes these; the detached child consumes them only for that exact attempt and never asks a second time for the same execution. A caller-supplied attempt id without the matching recorded start grants no permission.

`landing.on-red=person` records the initial red and its observed facts, then holds before any extra classification execution, retry or automatic return. Split 1a's proof continuation at that point. Existing evidence may identify a cause without another execution; recording it is allowed. Add `landing prove --classify <attempt>` for a person to request 1a's bounded classification of that recorded result. When proof itself is also `person`, this act admits the next required full proof only; a second full proof requires its own act. The intervening merge gates do not ask. `auto` runs the existing classifier and stop rules. A person can instead use the existing `landing return G --cause CAUSE --reason TEXT`; the reason and actor remain in the result, without mislabeling the cause as `own`.

`landing.trunk-red=auto` keeps 1a's hold except incident fix goals and an existing scoped human exception. `person` asks before allowing even a fix through a standing red. The command is `metasystem work land G --exception GOAL_LAND_TRUNK_RED --reason TEXT --by NAME`, with G and the known person's name filled and TEXT supplied as the person's reason. Both reason and by are required before lane routing (`cmd/metasystem/intent_exception.go:51`; `intent_delivery.go:1747`); the hand-in records `Exception{Code, Reason, By}` (`cmd/metasystem/landing_plain.go:162`), as mechanism 4 requires. A queued goal obtains the exception by the identical hand-in update already supported by `HandIn`; push rereads the incidents and exception. No policy makes a gate's green count as a full proof or permits an unproven push. Clearing an incident by evidence uses `landing prove --trunk`; `incident list` is never printed as a clearing act.

**Questions and human acts.** Extend 1a's lane question sync with a subject key `(policy, batch or goal/commit, rung or attempt, incident set)`. Store the required argv, evidence and this identity in the existing question's facts. No new question store. New `plain.SyncPolicyQuestion` is called by the decision owner and by the keeper's observer. `question show` displays the same executable command as status. Plain text “yes”, withdrawal and a general `landing run` cannot authorize a proof, different batch or different incident exception. Only the successful named act for that subject closes it. A policy changed to `auto` supersedes its request after reevaluation; the record says superseded, not answered.

`landing run --goals` records a person's bounded batch choice. It can launch the lane's agent while the helm is held to execute that recorded choice. It does not lend the agent the person's identity or permission for later proof/red decisions. Direct human proof and return commands likewise bypass policy holds for their own act. The gate at `landing run:791`, the paused guard at `intent_landing_prove.go:104`, the keeper and question hold all need this distinction; changing only the skill leaves the remedy blocked. A human act under an immediate pause may perform its named operation, without silently resuming automatic work.

**The return stop carried from 1a.** Automatic returns are bounded to two per goal, across tips and batches. The existing queue lines supply the count, cause class, test identities and conflict evidence; no new counter file. Before a further automatic return, compare the red set and class to the previous return. A repeated test/conflict class or a non-shrinking red set stops immediately; otherwise two is the maximum. The handoff is one question offering the specific person's `landing return` act and its evidence. A new hand-in, a renamed batch or a generic wake cannot reset this history. Human returns are recorded as such and do not silently reset the automatic allowance. Environment failures consume their existing one-retry allowance, never a goal correction.
