Working Mode: Implement. Goal lane-steward, design `plans/designs/lane-steward.md` (read it whole; U1 and U2 landed `health.go` and `steward.go`). Worktree = this checkout. At most 250 production lines. Never commit, rebase or push; never touch memory/receipts.log, plans/, testing.json, go.mod or any metasystem.conf*. Tests never read a live ledger or install.

## Build
1. `internal/landing/plain/steward_report.go` (new). Once an hour (a `steward-report.json` record holds the last report time; the steward step of U2 calls `StewardReport` and it returns at once when the hour is not up) the steward writes `artifacts/agents/landing/steward-report.md`, one section per hour appended at the top, from the records of that hour: Quality — every red proof with its attributed cause (goal / main / flake / unknown) and the incidents opened and closed; Speed — per goal that was in the lane, its minutes from hand-in to push or return against 60, the pushes of the hour, the rows that fired and the acts taken (U2's interventions); Tokens — the landing agent's and the critics' spend per landing where the session usage record exists (find the usage record the engine writes per session; when it does not exist for the lane, the line says "unmeasured: <the record that is missing>" — never a guess). The last section's one-line summary (`hour to HH:MM: N landed, N returned, N red (causes), oldest goal M min, K acts`) is `Health.Report` and `Line()` appends it; `landing health` prints the report path and that line.
2. `skills/landing-agent/SKILL.md`: a section "The lane steward" (≤ 25 lines): what it watches (the rows), what it may do (the U2 acts), what it never does (end a person's process, refuse a person's verb, push code), that the agent reads `artifacts/agents/landing/interventions.jsonl` at the start of each turn and does not re-ask a question the steward withdrew until a fresh proof of main exists, and that `landing health` is the one place to look before asking a person anything. Keep the skill's word budget if the repository audits enforce one (`TestAudit`).

## Tests
Fixture records with an injected clock: (a) the report is written once per hour and not twice; (b) a red proof with Cause main and an incident → Quality names both; (c) two goals landed in 45 and 95 min → Speed names the second as over 60; (d) no usage record → Tokens says unmeasured and names the record; with a fixture usage record → the sum; (e) `Health.Report` and `Line()` carry the summary; (f) the skill section exists and the skill audit passes. Mutation: report every tick, or count a returned goal as landed → a test fails.
