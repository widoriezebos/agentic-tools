# questions-reach-the-person-only-when-his

- State: approved
- Priority: 1
- Sequence: 24
- Risk: severity=2 novelty=2 exposure=2 accumulation=1 basis="Decides who answers a seat; a wrong rule lets an agent decide what is the person's, or floods the person again; builds on the power-of-attorney grant and the question records that exist"
- Tier: 2
- Intent: A seat's question reaches the person only when it is his to decide, and then with everything he needs: the seat's steward first classifies the question (a machinery wall to route, a call within the partner's recorded authority, or the person's own choice), the partner answers what the person's standing word covers and escalates the rest with the impact in plain words (R-143), and a question that does reach the person names the decision, carries its material (a picture or a page is shown, never a local path) and offers the recommended answer. Measured 2026-10-03: seven questions reached Wido's phone that he could not or need not answer (run a close, accept a trivial risk, accept a design whose nine reserved decisions the question did not show, 'build as drawn?' with a mock he could not see); the supervisor answered or withdrew all seven (Wido: 'typically your interventions should have been steward interventions').
- Origin: human
- Next step: Design first, tier 2, with steward-acts-on-behaviour-patterns (the classification at the steward), agent-works-as-project-partner (the partner as the first addressee and its recorded authority), questions-carry-what-they-ask-about and channel-questions-stand-alone (the content rules): who may answer which class, how an answer by the partner is recorded as the person's standing word, how a question with an image or a page is shown on the phone and in the UI, and the measure (questions to the person per day, answered by the partner per day). Done when: for a week no question reaches the person that the partner could have answered under his recorded word, and every question that does reach him carries its material and the recommended answer.
