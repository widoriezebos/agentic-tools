# design-rounds-read-less-and-repeat-less

- State: approved
- Priority: 1
- Sequence: 10
- Risk: severity=2 novelty=2 exposure=3 accumulation=2 basis="severity 2: a wrong context-pack check can refuse a design launch or admit an unfilled brief, and a wrong receipt field misstates spend; no process is signalled and no ledger or landing path changes; novelty 2: the context pack, call budget, page ceiling and receipt token fields exist (fb6839a44, e700328e1), while the excerpt check, the batching instruction, the per-request usage fields and the recurring-findings section are new; exposure 3: every design and code-read round on every seat; accumulation 2: re-read context adds up per round and per day but leaves no state behind"
- Tier: 2
- Intent: A design delegate's tokens are mostly context re-read, not thinking. Measured by m1e on 2026-09-15 on the coordinator-context 8d design r1 (one fresh Claude Fable 5.1 delegate): 2,432,374 tokens over 28 requests, 91.4% cached context re-read, 6.7% new context, 1.9% output including thinking, 163K context at the last request. Raw tokens are about requests times average context, so three levers cut them without lowering effort or any gate. (1) The seat fills the design brief's context pack (fb6839a44) with cited line-range excerpts before launch instead of letting the delegate find and read whole pages; nothing checks today that the pack is filled, and the 8d r1 prompt was find-first. (2) Delegates batch independent reads into one request; nothing asks it today (docs/orchestration.md:8 covers parallel workers, not batched calls). (3) A first-round brief carries the finding classes that recurred in earlier critiques of the area it touches, so fewer rounds follow; retro-flags-repeated-critique-findings detects recurrence after the fact, and no brief consumes it. Effort levels per hazard class were weighed and held by Wido on 2026-09-15 (a small share for Fable). Part of the efficiency program led by seats-spend-tokens-in-bounded-sessions (Wido, 2026-09-15). R-115-m1e item 6 binds: no unit lowers a proof floor, removes a witness or a gate, narrows a DONE, or cuts a call budget, page ceiling or critique round to save tokens, and each unit's read checks it. DONE: (a) a validator that the one launcher (units-run-through-one-launcher) runs refuses a design or code-read brief whose context pack keeps a template placeholder or cites an excerpt whose bytes differ from the checkout at its file:start-end, proven by a fixture that refuses an unfilled and a drifted brief and admits a filled one; (b) the design-brief and review-brief templates tell the delegate to batch independent reads into one request, and every design and read receipt records requests, tool calls, peak context tokens, cache-read tokens and output tokens next to design_tokens and design_calls; (c) the design-brief template carries a recurring-findings section filled from retro-flags-repeated-critique-findings for the goals the design touches, or 'none recorded'; (d) a report over the first three design rounds after the landing, against the 8d r1 baseline, shows tokens per round, peak context and rounds to acceptance, with material findings per round no higher than in the three rounds before the landing.
