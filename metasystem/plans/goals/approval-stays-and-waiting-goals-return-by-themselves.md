# approval-stays-and-waiting-goals-return-by-themselves

- State: approved
- Risk: severity=3 novelty=2 exposure=3 accumulation=3 basis="Changes what approval, waiting and budget mean for every goal, every seat and the person; a wrong rule starts work nobody agreed to or strands agreed work, and a changed ledger form can make the goal ledger unreadable to a running engine; the picker's condition and the dependency edges exist, the split of the records is new; it shapes every future goal"
- Tier: 3
- Intent: A goal the person agreed to stays agreed until its intent changes, and a goal that waits comes back by itself (Wido 2026-10-03: 'why would these need approve? ... Surely approval is only needed if the design/build has changed to no longer match intent? The rest is budget?'; 'equal priority to a todo goal? Then waiting goes first'; 'you don't really need a trigger'; 'Just a condition that says can continue and then the next picks up from todo and waiting with this order'). Measured 2026-10-03: one approve act binds intent, tier, risk and budget together, so a budget change, a risk edit, a text edit and a retry after seats made no progress each demand a new approval; 28 of 67 approve acts in a week were repeats; 30 open goals had their approval withdrawn only as a brake and none because its intent changed; all 13 returns from waiting were a person's act. Wanted: (1) three separate facts with their own records and acts: agreement with the intent, the rigor (tier and risk), and the budget; the person is asked to agree again only when the intent text changes, when a reviewer finds that the design or the build no longer delivers the intent, or when he withdraws. (2) Waiting is a condition, not a state: a goal can continue when the goals it waits for are done, no question to a person stands open on it, its budget has room and no person paused it; the picker checks this when it picks, nothing is parked for a dependency and nothing needs lifting. (3) One order for todo and waiting together: priority, with a blocker taking the place of the best goal that waits for it, and at equal priority a goal that waited goes first. (4) Budget is its own act: giving, changing or extending a budget never touches the agreement or another seat's claim, and giving budget to a stopped goal lets it continue. (5) Today's ledger moves over without anyone's agreement or allowance changing, and withdrawn approvals are restored on a listing the person sees first.
