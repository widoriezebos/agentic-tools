# fleet-steward-reasons-over-all-seats

- State: approved
- Priority: 1
- Sequence: 14
- Risk: severity=3 novelty=3 exposure=3 accumulation=2 basis="An agent that acts across the whole fleet under the person's word; a wrong rule overrules a person or burns tokens unbounded; the signals and verbs exist, the reasoning role is new; it shapes every seat"
- Tier: 3
- Intent: One fleet steward per host observes every seat, the lane and the backlog together, reasons about what it sees, and acts under the person's recorded word where the seat stewards' rules end: cross-seat patterns (the same wall on three seats, a landing that starves a seat, a budget trend), the lane and the backlog as a whole, and the person's channel; it never overrules a person (R-142), spends only on a signal the rules cannot resolve and within a bound, and turns what it learns into goals (Wido 2026-10-03: 'one overall metasystem consciousness at top level observing / overseeing all and reasoning about that? ... Can we have this with existing machinery and agents / roles? Or should we layer something on top?'). Today that role is a person's session (m1e): on 2026-10-03 it answered seven questions, set four budgets, ran nine acts for seats, pushed three records and watched the fleet with a script; the measure of this goal is that count going to zero.
- Origin: human
- Next step: Design first, tier 3, after steward-acts-on-behaviour-patterns has its design: the seat stewards own their seat (rules R0-R9 of agentic-tools-evidence/steward-seat-rules-20261003/rules.md); the fleet steward consumes their signals plus the lane's and the ledger's, and owns what crosses seats. Built from existing parts, not a new layer: the Go steward runner as the collector (the Fleet page already aggregates), the bounded diagnosis worker of the seat steward design generalised to the fleet (an agent session on the partner's runtime, started only on a signal the rules cannot resolve, with a cap per hour and a budget), acting through the public verbs under the recorded grant, and the retro agent for the weekly look. Decide in the design: what stays Go rules and what needs reasoning; the spend bound; what it may do alone and what goes to the person with the impact; how it writes goals; and the measure (interventions by a person's session per day, target zero).
