# backlog-holds-only-work-that-can-start

- State: queued
- Priority: 1
- Sequence: 3
- Risk: severity=3 novelty=3 exposure=3 accumulation=3 basis="Changes what a goal, an approval and a park mean for every seat and for the person; a wrong model loses work or floods the queue, and it shapes every future goal"
- Tier: 3
- Intent: The number of waiting goals stays at a minimum, ideally zero, because a goal is work that can start now and everything else is kept somewhere else (Wido 2026-10-03: 'We should really get into the situation/habit/proper prioritisation that tries to keep the number of waiting goals TO A MINIMUM, IDEALLY 0 ... 14 goals waiting is an indicator of something really wrong'). Measured 2026-10-03: 110 open goals, 7 underway, 24 ready, 65 unapproved (median 23 days), 14 parked. The rules for agreement, waiting, order and budget are goal approval-stays-and-waiting-goals-return-by-themselves; this goal builds on them and does not restate them. Wanted: (1) Work that cannot start now is not a goal: it is an idea on a plain list, a follow-up stub on the goal it follows (opened by the machinery when that goal lands), or a watch entry the steward's patterns own ('if X is seen, propose goal Y'). (2) Opening a goal that cannot start now is refused with the plain alternatives. (3) What starts next is decided by scheduling: a Ready queue with a cap tied to the seats, with a way past the cap for a red main and for machinery defects. (4) Waiting with no end in sight, Ready against its cap, the oldest Ready goal and goals opened against goals landed are measured, shown and watched; a triage the partner prepares and the person decides in one sitting keeps them there, starting with the goals that were never approved.
- Origin: main
- Next step: Proper design first, at this goal's tier, after the design of approval-stays-and-waiting-goals-return-by-themselves is accepted, because this goal builds on its model (the three records, the can-continue condition, the order).
