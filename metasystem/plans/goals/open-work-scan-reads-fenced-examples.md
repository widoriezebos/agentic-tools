The two available responses today are both bad: corrupt accurate documentation to satisfy the scanner, or let the refusal ride to the third strike where the steward overrides. DONE means a plan field is read only from the document's own body and never from inside a fenced block, every planField reader gets the fix rather than one call site, and a test whose only candidate field sits inside a fence reports no open work and no unfilled template.
