# small-change-lane

- State: queued
- Priority: 1
- Sequence: 12
- Intent: The 'change this little thing' case has a supported path: a dispatch lane cheap enough that a certified one-line fix is not ceremony. Wido's three decisions of 2026-09-10 stand (recorded in the next step). DONE means: (1) the lane admits a change of tier 1 or 2 (after tier-from-severity-and-novelty) within recorded bounds: at most four files and eighty changed lines, no protected surface; (2) its stages are exactly: build in a worktree, the focused tests named in the brief, one independent read at the tier's depth (critique-always), a receipt in standard mode, land; no design page, no register, no conformance round; (3) a change that outgrows the bounds mid-lane overflows to the ordinary pipeline with its work carried, never silently; (4) an end-to-end one-line fix goes from claim to landed in under 45 minutes of seat time and under 3 delegate rounds, measured on a real fix and recorded in the receipt. Goal 19 of plans/delivery-efficiency-plan.md. Design revision 1 (scl-design1, Fable) is a patch in m1b's scratchpad. RUNTIME INDEPENDENCE (plan principle 0; architecture doctrine of 2026-08-16: correctness never depends on an accelerator): this holds for every runtime the roster can host today (claude, codex, devin) and for future adapters such as opencode; the mechanism lives in the Go engine, the ledger verbs and the adapter contract, never in one runtime's hook, harness, CLI or transcript format; a native facility may accelerate it through its adapter, and an agent on any runtime with no accelerator gets the same guarantee from the records and the verb alone; where the goal touches a runtime path, DONE is proven on at least two runtimes.
