- Intent: A review sitting in the web UI is one guided path a person can follow from "what am I looking at" to a verdict, where a nod is one press whenever nothing blocks: the sitting first says in plain English what is under review (the goal's intent, what this version adds, whether it is the version that would land), then shows the reviewer's findings as decisions (each with a severity word, what the problem is, why it matters, and the reviewer's recommendation; the person's choices are named acts with their consequence stated before the press: must fix before landing, fix after landing, not a problem with a reason, accept this risk with a reason; untouched findings follow the recommendation), lets the person try the reviewed version, and ends with one verdict (looks good and land it, send it back, or ask for a new review) whose consequence is stated; the person never meets a refusal without the one act that resolves it; the critic's ids, paths and timestamps are evidence behind a fold (Wido 2026-10-03 16:28-16:40 CEST: 'the texts are totally useless: they have no meaning at all for a human.
