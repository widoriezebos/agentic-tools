# review-findings-read-as-decisions

- State: claimed
- Priority: 1
- Sequence: 8
- Risk: severity=2 novelty=2 exposure=2 accumulation=1 basis="The surface where a person decides what lands; a wrong consequence text makes him record what he did not mean, so severity 2; new fields in the critic's return and new decision acts, novelty 2; every review sitting, exposure 2; nothing accumulates beyond the review records"
- Tier: 2
- Intent: A review sitting in the web UI is one guided path a person can follow from "what am I looking at" to a verdict, where a nod is one press whenever nothing blocks: the sitting first says in plain English what is under review (the goal's intent, what this version adds, whether it is the version that would land), then shows the reviewer's findings as decisions (each with a severity word, what the problem is, why it matters, and the reviewer's recommendation; the person's choices are named acts with their consequence stated before the press: must fix before landing, fix after landing, not a problem with a reason, accept this risk with a reason; untouched findings follow the recommendation), lets the person try the reviewed version, and ends with one verdict (looks good and land it, send it back, or ask for a new review) whose consequence is stated; the person never meets a refusal without the one act that resolves it; the critic's ids, paths and timestamps are evidence behind a fold (Wido 2026-10-03 16:28-16:40 CEST: 'the texts are totally useless: they have no meaning at all for a human. All should be in plain easy to understand English, listing the impact (pros and cons) and a recommendation'; 'it is totally unclear what these do'; 'HELP from a UX perspective on how to even review. Not a bunch of buttons and cards all over the place ... even refusing to end the review with a nod, although I have no idea how to do anything that is not a nod. This is just really bad UX'). Today the room is a desk of parts with no path: header chips (tip, candidate, anchor), finding cards as generic Partner deposits with Record it and Dismiss, a Partner box, End and Step out; and a nod on a sitting of an old version was refused without words.
