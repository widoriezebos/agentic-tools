# never-idle-ironclad

- State: queued
- Priority: 2
- Sequence: 19
- Intent: What: an umbrella that proves one promise: a seat never sits idle while approved work waits and no person has told it to stop. It builds nothing itself; it collects the evidence from its member goals. Several parts exist: turn-ending checks are hardened, a stop command exists, host setup is done, and the steward now starts a seat when work waits and none is live (seat-works-without-a-person, which also covers restarting a dead or absent session). Left: raising an outside alarm when a seat stays idle anyway, a watch command, recovery to a good state, and the two unattended-delivery proofs. Why: Wido ordered that seats must never stop while work remains; there were nights where seats sat idle for hours with 84 goals queued and nobody knew. Pros: one place that says whether the promise is really kept, with evidence. Cons: it cannot close until many other goals are done, so it stays open a long time.
- Origin: main
- Next step: Next: record the steward-started seat (seat-works-without-a-person, on main since batch 3) as the part that restarts a dead or absent session, and keep waiting on the remaining members (watch command, alarm, recovery, the two proofs). Done when: every part of the promise has a landed member with a passing test, and both unattended-delivery proofs are done.
