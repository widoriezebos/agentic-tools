The Round 1 rescope is directionally sound, but Round 2 is not implementation-ready. The empty act-allowlist, exclusion of the flight recorder from authority, and deferral of the coach are correct. Eight structural gaps remain.

1. **STRUCTURAL — The typed-owner aggregation boundary does not hold for the shipped invariant.** `supervise.ArmedNow` is a Boolean function, not an emitted tri-state verdict. It reads state, census, heartbeat, and process evidence and collapses missing, unreadable, stale, dead, and mismatched cases into `false` ([verifyarmed.go](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/internal/supervise/verifyarmed.go:36)). Therefore the steward cannot produce `unknown` versus `breach` without re-reading raw evidence. Moreover, `steward scan --root` lacks the owner PID, start time, tag, and interval required by `ArmedNow` ([verifyarmed.go](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/internal/supervise/verifyarmed.go:42)). A supervise-owned typed liveness result, including reason and evidence identity, is a prerequisite—not something existing owners already expose.

2. **STRUCTURAL — `ArmedNow` is canonical only for arming success, not universal supervision health.** The recorded decision calls it “the arming success criterion” ([delegated decision D21](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/docs/reviews/2026-08-13-delegated-decisions.md:462)). Other readers still differ:

   - `ArmedNow`: census age at most one interval; heartbeat age at most `2×+2`.
   - Dispatch: census age below `min(2×interval, 180s)`, with stricter schema and state-digest validation ([attest.go](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/internal/dispatch/attest.go:39)).
   - Stop watchdog: the same capped `2×` census window, without heartbeat freshness ([watchdog.go](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/internal/supervise/watchdog.go:32)).
   - Run-pass attestation: `2×` with a future-time bound ([scan.go](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/internal/report/scan.go:242)).

   With a 60-second interval, a 61–119-second-old census becomes a steward breach while watchdog and dispatch still accept it. Reuse avoids inventing new arithmetic, but it does not establish one owner contract or prevent contradictory Stop output.

3. **STRUCTURAL — The decision table is incomplete and internally contradictory.** “A session is active” has no named source or outcome for inactive or unreadable session evidence ([design table](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/plans/process-steward-design.md:62)). An implementer must guess between the current Stop caller, any live announcement, the lease holder, or any active work. Main announcements also require kernel revalidation; file presence is not activity ([census announcement reader](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/internal/census/run.go:536)). Separately, the table maps unreadable attestation to `unknown`, while the next paragraph declares a missing attestation an immediate `breach` ([design](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/plans/process-steward-design.md:65)). Missing evidence must have one unambiguous fail-unknown outcome.

4. **STRUCTURAL — The durable incident record lacks an incident lifecycle.** Scan identity and completion time change every scan, but the design defines no stable incident identity or semantic digest, revision, concurrency ordering, reopen/resolve transition, or rule for whether `unknown` preserves an existing breach. Atomic rename alone allows an older concurrent scan to overwrite a newer one. Digesting the whole record would also re-deliver an unchanged incident every scan. Evidence references point at mutable supervision files unless bound to their generation or digest. Existing run state demonstrates the missing disciplines: directory locking, compare-and-swap, lifecycle identity, and terminal ordering ([run record contract](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/internal/run/run.go:1)).

   Placement under `artifacts/agents/steward/` is correct, as is rejecting the flight recorder as authority. But “durable, atomic like every record” is not a sufficient write contract: durable publication has committed-but-durability-unknown as a distinct outcome and requires an anchor ([atomicfile contract](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/internal/atomicfile/atomicfile.go:8)).

5. **STRUCTURAL — Stop-hook precedence and composition are asserted, not specified.** Round 2 gives no input/result fields, precedence table, all-clear veto, or rule for `shouldBlock`. A steward-only finding must not silently acquire blocking authority when the act-allowlist is empty. Ordinary timing already permits steward-breach plus watchdog-clear; races permit an old steward breach after rearming or an old steward ok while the watchdog sees failure. Appending a breach to `NOTHING LEFT TO WORK ON` is contradictory, while suppressing watchdog output can lose its independent `UNTRACKED` signal. The current turn ladder has concrete precedence that the design must extend explicitly ([turn verdict ladder](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/internal/goal/turnverdict.go:324)).

6. **STRUCTURAL — Deduplication, clearing, and retry are not inherited from the existing digest slot.** The current contract defines new/same/empty-clear and concurrent-winner behavior for one watchdog digest ([implementation](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/internal/goal/turnverdict.go:478), [tests](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/internal/goal/turnverdict_test.go:244)). It persists “surfaced” before hook transport. On a blocking turn, the hook discards watchdog extras even though their digest has already been consumed ([hook](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/scripts/agents/supervision-hook.sh:137)). A fresh steward incident concurrent with an open-work or goal block can therefore be lost permanently; post-state-write transport failure has the same retry hole. Round 2 needs an acknowledgement-safe transition or composition into the one authoritative display decision.

7. **STRUCTURAL — Who-watches-the-steward is topologically plausible but operationally undefined.** Stop and SessionStart/arming are outside watcher+reaper, so the topology can be non-circular. But no caller or cadence for `steward scan`, freshness threshold, producer identity, future-clock rule, first-attestation bootstrap, or ordering between incident publication and pass publication is defined ([design](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/plans/process-steward-design.md:89)). Nor does current arming read a steward attestation. The existing run-pass attestation works because the watcher owns its cadence and the reader binds freshness to the armed live watcher ([run-pass writer](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/cmd/metasystem/supervise_component.go:167)). No equivalent freshness owner exists here.

8. **STRUCTURAL — Three deferred invariants still name the wrong or incomplete owners.** They are not smuggled into the steward, which is good, but their prerequisites are not ready:

   - Temp namespace inventory and orphan judgment belong to `internal/janitor`; `internal/run` owns only its delegated launch-log lifecycle ([disk-hygiene ownership](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/plans/disk-hygiene-design.md:22)).
   - Plan/work correlation does not yet have one decision owner. `internal/report` scans facts while `internal/goal` owns the typed turn decision; `Run.GoalId` exists but the public launch/register verbs do not populate it ([run CLI](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/cmd/metasystem/run.go:68)).
   - `gaterun` owns live gate markers, not green gate results, and `commit.sh` only enforces lease authority before `git commit` ([gaterun](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/internal/gaterun/gaterun.go:1), [commit wrapper](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/scripts/agents/commit.sh:8)). Exact-tree critique proof currently lives in validation, so ship certification needs a real domain owner.

   Ack history is correctly assigned to `internal/run`; its current transition records only `Acked=true`, confirming the timestamp/history prerequisite ([Ack](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/internal/run/verbs.go:301)).

9. **MECHANICAL-GRAIN — The coach seam is correct with two naming corrections.** A deferred rostered, read-only role producing untrusted advice is the right shape and does not weaken the first slice. Runtime/model binding belongs to `metasystem.conf`, not adapter configuration ([roster resolution](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/internal/dispatch/roster.go:111)). “No repository permissions” must also be clarified: the existing `none` preset permits repository reads but no writes ([preset](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/scripts/agents/permissions/none.json:1)); literal no-read access would require embedding all evidence in the brief.

The one-invariant scope is small in policy count, but not yet a small implementation slice: it presently requires a new supervise verdict boundary, incident lifecycle, scan cadence/attestation protocol, turn-verdict state-machine extension, and arming integration. It becomes worth shipping once the durable typed record is genuinely reusable; without that, it largely duplicates the existing Stop watchdog warning.
