`Batch *BatchHealth` (id, open, sealed_at, members, minutes, and per waiting goal: handed in before or after `sealed_at`, and the admission predicate's answer against the recorded batch state — admissible / not admissible with its reason / unknown when the records cannot establish eligibility at the time); `Agent AgentHealth` (alive, last step verb and its age in minutes from the `agent_step.go` record, `WaitingOn string` naming the lane record that explains an idle agent — a running proof, the running fix/resolution, a regeneration, a review in progress — with its start time, `Silent bool` only when alive, the last step older than 20 min and `WaitingOn` is empty: unexplained inactivity, with work waiting or with an empty batch alike (finding 134's 55-minute stall was an empty batch; reuse `agentIdleFinding` in status.go as the seed and keep its finding); this unit also makes `cmd/metasystem/landing_agent_step.go` record the agent's `work build`, `work review`, `work wait` and `work land` steps, which it records for selected landing commands only today); `Paused *PauseHealth` (since, reason, minutes) from the pause/stop records; `Questions []QuestionHealth` (id, kind, minutes open, `Premise string` = current / gone / unknown: gone only on the evidence the question carries — the "main is at commit X" fact with main moved past X, the checkout-not-on-main fact with the checkout on main, exactly as `WithdrawStaleLaneQuestion` decides (extract that predicate into a function both call), or a policy request that `PolicyQuestionSatisfied` says is satisfied; current when the evidence exists and still holds; unknown when the question carries none); `Incidents []IncidentHealth` (id, commit, test, the recorded attribution/class, `Repeat string` = green / red / none from the repeat records (`Result.FlakeRepeats`, `Flakes`, `RepeatComplete`) of the proof that recorded it, `MainMoved bool` when main is not the incident's commit, `Unproven bool` when main moved and no trunk proof of the current main exists); `Load LoadHealth` (hostload.Read now: one-minute load, saturated; and for the last proof `AtStart`, `AtEnd *float64` — nil prints as "unmeasured" — read from the result; this unit also makes every finished result carry `LoadStart`/`LoadEnd` samples taken in the prove path at launch and at completion, green or red, so the fact exists from now on; the existing red-only `Load` stays); `Engine EngineHealth` (the LANE INSTALL's engine identity read from its binary stamp with `internal/enginebuild`'s stamp reader (record.go), never the observing process's `debug.ReadBuildInfo` — the observer may be another install's engine, finding 162; empty when the stamp is missing; `Stale bool` when main's lane code — `internal/landing/`, `internal/gaterun/`, `cmd/metasystem/intent_landing*.go` — changed between that revision and the checkout's main, read with one `git diff --quiet`); `Proofs ProofHealth` (the last five COMPARABLE executions' durations in minutes — same scope as the running proof (full with full, impact with impact; `Result` records the scope), each attempt counted once; reuse the full-proof estimator's selection in restart.go — their median, the running proof's minutes, `Drift bool` when running > 1.5 × median, and the load samples where recorded); `LastPush string`.
2. `func (h Health) Rows() []Fired` evaluates the noticing table and returns every row whose signal is present, in this order and with these names and one-line prescriptions (the words the design gives): `stale-question` (Premise == gone only → "withdraw it, prove main, re-ask only if red"; unknown never fires this row), `unproven-incidents` (→ "prove main before asking anything"), `suspected-contention` (last proof red, its recorded load saturated, no repeat record yet → "repeat by name before classifying; the recorded attribution stands"), `confirmed-flake` (the named repeat of the failed tests is green → "a flake: register it, no incident"; the attribution text is the record's, never the row's), `silent-agent` (fires only on unexplained inactivity, never while `WaitingOn` names a record → read `stopLane` and `settleLandingAgent` in cmd/metasystem/intent_landing.go and print the verb that actually ends the agent's session; `landing stop` pauses admission and does not end it, so when no verb does, the prescription is "no verb ends the agent session today; the steward's loop (U2) ends it through the settle path; until then: metasystem landing stop, then start"), `stale-engine` (→ "redeploy the engine from main"), `batch-of-one` (a sealed batch of one member whose `sealed_at` is later than the hand-in of a waiting goal that the batch's own admission predicate — reuse the function `checkBatchLocked`'s admission uses: eligible, cap above one, automatic policy, no person's seal; a different proof depth is NOT an exclusion, depth.go admits every entry and the batch takes the highest depth — says was admissible against the recorded batch state → "a goal admissible before the seal was left out"; handed in after `sealed_at` → a fact (arrived after the boundary), no row; eligibility unknown from the records → the fact says unknown, no row; predicate says no → the fact shows its reason, no row), `proof-drift` (→ "<minutes> min against a median of <m>; load at start <x>/end <y> or unmeasured" — the load is shown, never named the cause), `abandoned-resolution` (the abandoned observation `readStatus` makes before it sets `RunningFix = nil` — capture it there into a local the health builder receives → "rebuild the merge on the current main"), `overdue-goal` (one per goal over 60 min → the goal, its minutes and the reason), `paused` (> 30 min → since and reason), `question-open` (any open lane question > 30 min → what it waits for). Each `Fired{Row, Subject, Minutes, Act string}`.

## Tests
Fixture installs shaped like 2026-10-11/12 (build them with the package's helpers, an injected clock, no live records): (a) an open agent question naming "main is at commit X" while the fixture main contains X and moved → `stale-question` fires and Premise gone; the same question with main at X → Premise current, nothing fires; a question with prose facts and no commit → Premise unknown, only `question-open` after 30 min; (b) an open incident on commit X, main moved, no trunk proof of main → `unproven-incidents`; with a trunk proof of main → not; (c) agent alive, last step 55 min old, one waiting goal, no explaining record → `silent-agent` "with 1 goal waiting"; the same with an empty queue → `silent-agent` "with nothing to do"; the same with a running fix/resolution record (or a running proof, or a review in progress) → not, and `WaitingOn` names it; a `work review` run through the step recorder leaves a fresh step; (d) a sealed batch of one member and an admissible goal handed in two minutes before `sealed_at` → `batch-of-one`; handed in after `sealed_at` → not (fact: arrived after the boundary); under a cap of one, or sealed by a person, or ineligible → not, and the batch fact names the predicate's reason; a newcomer of another proof depth handed in before the seal → fires (depth is not an exclusion); records that cannot establish eligibility → fact unknown, no row; (d2) a red proof with recorded saturated load and no repeat → `suspected-contention`, not `confirmed-flake`; with a green named repeat → `confirmed-flake`; a green proof's result carries LoadStart/LoadEnd after the prove path ran in the fixture; an old result without them prints unmeasured; (e) five full proofs of 40 min and a running full proof at 70 min → `proof-drift`; at 50 → not; five impact proofs of 4 min and a running FULL proof at 40 → not (not comparable); duplicate result lines for one attempt count once; (e2) an abandoned resolution fixture driven THROUGH `readStatus` (not a hand-built Health) → `abandoned-resolution` fires although `RunningFix` is nil in the status; (f) a goal waiting 95 min → `overdue-goal` with minutes 95; (g) `Line()` for a fixture with two rows and for one with none; (h) `landing health` text and `--json` through the command's test harness; (i) engine identity comes from a fixture install's stamped binary, not from the test process; engine stale: a revision that differs from main with a lane file changed between → `Stale`; no lane file changed → not; revision unknown → `Stale` false and the fact says unknown.
