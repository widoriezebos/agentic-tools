## Intent and moved scope

Finding 11 of `plans/machinery-open-findings-plan-2026-10-06.md` (refusals and questions that need a person, at night) and the mechanisms "ask routing" and "the refusal kind of the register" (mechanisms page 1, 4, 12). The source's two critique rounds (Codex Astra, 2026-10-08) left these three units carrying material of a class already fixed in the same unit, so the plan's design row split them here with their design of record and every finding as inputs. Nothing of finding 9 is here.

1. **Ask routing (the source's U3).** `question ask` routes an ordinary question (kind `other`, including an act request whose first line is the command) to the coordinator seat as an `agent ask` while `question.route` is `auto` and a live general grant names that seat; to the person through the channel otherwise. The record says where it went; `question show`, `question list` and the UI read it. Authority kinds (`stop`, `budget-above-norm`, `carry`) go to the person as today.
2. **The routed question's answer, and its return to the person (the source's U4).** The coordinator's reply becomes the question's recorded answer and wakes the asker's wait; "not mine", an ended or revoked grant, or the ask's deadline passing with no reply posts the question to the person once, never suppressed as a repeat.
3. **The refusal kind of the register (the source's U5).** The host's lane steward counts the questions open across the computer's seats by refusal class within an hour; at three it opens one `refusal` alert in the lane's store with the questions as evidence and one notification on the channel; while that alert is open, a further question of the class is recorded, routed to the coordinator as usual, but not posted to the person; `question retry` posts one on demand; `alert list --verbose` shows the questions and their commands.

Acceptance, from the source brief (4-7): with `question.route auto`, a declared coordinator and a live grant, `question ask G ...` of kind `other` writes the record, sends one `agent ask` to the coordinator with the question and the command, posts nothing to the channel; with `person`, with no grant, or for an authority kind, it posts to the channel as today. The coordinator's `agent reply` ends the asker's `question wait` with the reply as the answer; a grant that ended or an ask past its deadline posts the question to the person exactly once. Three questions of one refusal class across the host's seats within an hour open one `refusal` alert in the lane's store with one notification; a fourth is recorded and routed but not posted to the person; `question retry` posts it; `alert list --verbose` lists the questions with their commands; the alert clears when the class has been quiet for an hour or a person clears it. A person's explicit act is never refused by any of the above; an agent gains no authority from a routed ask, an alert, or a copied command.
