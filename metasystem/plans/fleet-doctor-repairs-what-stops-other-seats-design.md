## 1. The decisions in one screen

- Scope is this machine. Default: one checkout (`--repo`, upRepositoryScope's meaning, up.go:40). `--all` (U9, follow-up behind metasystem-stop-fleet-form) uses its fleet discovery, one census verdict per checkout.
- Diagnose leaves the checkout as it found it, for any caller: no artifact, no health.json, no surviving ref; the canonical tip is read through the verbs' own per-operation fetch ref and removed before exit.
- Nine damage classes. Holder-safe, only on the checkout the caller's MAIN session holds: base, goal-revision locks, fixtures (janitor reap; UNAVAILABLE until it lands). Human-only: ledger, engine signals. Name-only: stopped, other locks, budget, handoff, roles.
- The ledger repair is goal.Publish in repair mode over break-glass's planner and candidate content: every transaction gate but the broken-tip validation runs; the commit carries the break-glass record and the receipt; a lost compare re-plans inside the transaction's own deadline and an expired deadline names its rerun.
- The human proof is arm's classifier (process_verbs.go:331-364) plus `--by NAME`; ledger repairs also require humanauthority.Prove valid and record it; a fixture grant never writes the ledger.
- The doctor signals exactly one kind of process: one whose kernel-observed executable is a checkout's own engine pin, in a launcher role shape, that no record and no live creation claim of that checkout names, older than the birth grace, re-proved beside TERM and KILL. Nothing else, ever.
- Every checkout-local human repair on a checkout held by a live or uninspectable session is skipped (Q1). Every time or process observation goes through an injected clock, prober, executable reader, enumerator and signaller, in the owner packages too.

## 3. The verb, its grammar and its outcome table

```text
metasystem doctor [--repo ROOT | --all] [--json]
metasystem doctor --repair [CLASS[,CLASS...]] [--repo ROOT | --all] --by HUMAN [--json]
```

- No flag: diagnose the checkout `--repo` resolves to; any caller. `--all` (U9): every checkout fleet discovery names, one census verdict per checkout; until that seam lands it refuses DOCTOR_FLEET_UNAVAILABLE.
- `--repair`: a named class list is one confirmation per named class; a bare `--repair` from a HUMAN with a terminal asks one yes/no per found class on stdin; bare without a terminal refuses. `--by` is required on every HUMAN repair and refused on an agent's. An agent (the holding MAIN) must name classes from the holder list and may name only the checkout it holds.
- `--json`: the run record's document on stdout (section 7). Routing: a top-level verb beside `up`, `stop`, `arm`.

Line grammar (r2), one line per finding, stable for the seats that read it: `doctor <checkout>: <class> FOUND <n> | NAMED <n> | CLEAR | UNAVAILABLE <why>`; `  <class> <target> <finding> | repair: <what the doctor does, or the verb to run> | needs: holder|human|<goal>`; `doctor repaired <class> <target> <result> by=<who> record=<path>`; `doctor skipped <class> <target> <proof that failed, or: declined>`; `doctor re-check <checkout>: <class> CLEAR | STILL <n>`; `now: <what a blocked seat can do>`; `doctor <checkout>: nothing found`.

| State | Meaning | Exit weight |
|---|---|---|
| CLEAR | nothing found, or FOUND then re-checked CLEAR after this run's repair (printed REPAIRED) | 0 |
| FOUND | repairable damage, not requested or not repairable by this caller | 1 |
| NAMED | a name-only finding (stopped, other locks, budget, handoff, roles, a recorded or starting engine process); the verb is printed | 1 |
| UNAVAILABLE | the class's detector or repair seam is not landed, its census failed, or the canonical remote is unreachable | 1 |
| SKIPPED | requested, but a proof failed, Q1 held, or the human declined | 1 |
| STILL | requested, repaired, and the re-check finds it again, or the ledger transaction expired at its deadline | 3 |

Exit: 2 any refusal (nothing repaired); else 3 if any class is STILL; else 1 if any class is FOUND, NAMED, UNAVAILABLE or SKIPPED; else 0. Class order, always: ledger, base, locks, engine, stopped, fixtures, budget, handoff, roles. A `now:` line is printed per class that went FOUND to CLEAR and, for budget when ledger went CLEAR, the exact reopening command `metasystem goal resume --root <C> --id <goal> --by <name> <budget flags>`, because a NAMED class stays NAMED until its own verb runs. The four-fault bed after a human repair therefore exits 1 with budget NAMED; after the named verb runs, the rerun prints `nothing found` and exits 0 (DR-20).

## 6. Authority

A MAIN session may repair only base, goal-revision locks and fixtures, only on one checkout, and only when `lease.ClassifyVerbAt(target, installation, ppid).Holder` is true (verbs.go:372); a DELEGATE, STEWARD, SUPERVISION or adapter class is never a holder and is refused DOCTOR_NOT_HOLDER (seat ruling 2). These repairs add no authority: the holder's own goal verbs run MaintainBase and take goal-revision locks through goalrevision.Acquire, and its own beds are its runs'. Per class: base by MaintainBase's exact-match rule; goal-revision locks by the holder's Dead exact identity; fixtures by the bed owner's Dead identity and an owner record naming the held checkout (H10; else human-only). Stale pins are named, never deleted.

A2, human classes: ledger, engine signals, any holder class on a checkout the caller does not hold (under Q1). Proof: requireHumanTerminalAt's classifier (class HUMAN). The doctor never inspects tmux, TMUX variables or panes (H6): a seat typing into the enrolled pane in Wido's name (R-109-m1e, R-119-m1e) is HUMAN if and only if the classifier says so. `--by NAME` is required; an agent giving `--by` is refused DOCTOR_BY_NOT_HUMAN; NAME is a non-empty actor name recorded as `human:<NAME>`. Ledger repairs additionally: FixtureGranted is refused DOCTOR_FIXTURE_NOT_LEDGER (a fixture table is a test seam; publishing to the canonical remote under it is exactly the write that must never happen; agreed with BG-PROOF-01), and humanauthority.Prove must be Valid, its Grade, Outcome, TerminalRef and InvokerRef recorded, none optional: the goal ledger's own human acts are gated so (goalsync_mutations.go:1130, 1170), and a repair published to the canonical remote is such an act. Engine signals and non-held holder classes take the classifier and `--by` alone, as `stop` and `arm` do.

Q1, per-target quiescence for every checkout-local human repair (base, goal-revision locks, fixtures) on a checkout C the caller does not hold: lease.CurrentHolder(C) (verbs.go:329) names the holder; its announcement's recorded identity (H9) is probed; Alive or Unknown: every checkout-local repair on C is SKIPPED `held by session <id> (<alive|uninspectable>); run the doctor from that session, or after it ends`; Dead or no lease: the repair proceeds. Two named exemptions: engine signals, because a P1-P5 target is by construction outside every record of C and its holder does not own it; the ledger, because publication is global and the transaction's CAS is its coordination.

A3, never touched, whatever the caller: live leases and mains, live jobs and custody groups, handoffs (cancel is E10's), Codex brokers (R-112-m1e), ANNOUNCED and UNTRACKED census items, a runner or supervisor a record or a live creation claim of its checkout names, a process younger than the birth grace, any Unknown liveness, goal files by hand (goal bytes move only through the transaction), the stop fence, every lock but goal-revision locks, anything outside the repository, other machines.

A4, scope: this machine; one checkout by default; `--all` through fleet discovery (U9). An agent's `--all` may diagnose, never repair. A5: records in section 7; the ledger proof in section 5 steps 2-4.

## 7. Records and the after-report

- Diagnose writes nothing that survives the run (DR-1).
- `--repair` writes artifacts/agents/doctor/<utc>-<opid>.json under the `--repo` root before the first repair (caller class, by, proof fields for ledger repairs, at, checkouts, findings with proofs and recorded Refs, planned repairs) and rewrites it after (per repair: target Ref, action, state, at; the re-check). One line per repair is appended to artifacts/agents/doctor/repairs.log under the `--repo` root and, for a repair on another checkout, to that checkout's repairs.log. Evidence stays under artifacts, never records/misc (seat ruling). `--json` prints this document; for diagnose, the same shape with no repairs.

- A ledger repair is a ledger commit carrying its trailers, the break-glass record and the receipt; the doctor's record names the commit and the transaction's outcome.
- After every `--repair` the doctor re-runs all classes and prints the `now:` lines of section 3.

## 11. Agnosticism and boundaries

- No tmux: the doctor reads stdin, the classifier and the prover; nothing about panes, sessions or drivers (Wido 2026-09-14/15).
- No second process classifier: engine discovery is an executable-path filter over AllPids, ownership is the owners' records and claims; every other process fact comes from the census, fleet discovery and the prober.
- No new authority path: the human proof is arm's; ledger writes take the goal ledger's own Prove; the transaction is the goal verbs'; the lock takeover is goalrevision's; the reap is proof-runs-reap's.
- No goal-file hand edit, no `--no-verify`, no hook setting changed, no deny mode (R-118-m1e item 1: "Units that turn on deny mode or change hook settings beyond observe mode still wait"; none here does).
- recovery-to-good-state is not folded (seat ruling 8, r2). Interface: `doctor.Diagnose(scope Scope, seams Seams) (Report, error)` exported from internal/doctor and `metasystem doctor --json`; the prober's per-platform identity is used unchanged; no platform branch.

## 13. Interface assumption and what stays open
