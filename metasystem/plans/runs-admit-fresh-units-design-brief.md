## Intent and moved requirements

Complete the preparation of a new unit before the driver admits it: merge current main, apply boundary settings without stranding running work, and derive the goal's allowance from its accepted plan. These are the source brief's item 3 and the findings plan's findings 10 and 16. They are not implementation work hidden in the source's driver units.

1. **Merge main before every new unit build.** Fetch the configured endpoint's main, compare it with the goal branch's last incorporated main, and merge only when it moved, before freezing the new unit's base. Preserve existing unit commits and reads; never silently replace the requested merge with a rebase. Conflicts go to the existing resolution/build owner with the exact main and goal tips, and no ordinary builder starts on an unresolved merge. A repeated command after a lost response reconciles the same merge. The integration tree still gets the one full proof at landing. Running units and capacity-held steps keep their retained subject; they are not rebased or merged in place.
2. **Settings take effect at the completed-unit boundary.** Reuse the fleet boundary and existing configuration owner; keep settings and engine identity frozen for the admitted unit, apply queued settings before the next unit, and coordinate with the engine's existing re-arm owner. A changed roster must not invalidate a lawful continuation midway through a unit. The fresh helm/driver hold and actual authority are still checked before every autonomous effect. A person's `settings.apply=now` remains an explicit, impact-explained act. The accepted fleet design already sends its remaining settings/engine boundary work to [provider and session recovery](fleet-provider-and-session-recovery-design-brief.md): that owner implements activation; this follow-up integrates and proves the driver's ordered consumption, without building a second activation store.
3. **Allowance derived from the accepted plan.** At claim, derive allowances from the accepted Units and Estimates rows and tier declarations, including the review-round ceiling that blocked the first fifteen-unit machinery goal. Retain the source design identity. At a boundary, use the existing budget owner for any permitted per-unit extension, with at most one automatic raise per unit, evidenced progress and an ask on repeat. Respect the person's approved ceiling and the process-change owner; never infer spending authority from this estimate or a general grant. Missing or unreadable estimates are explained, not zero. Settle the findings plan's `auto / capped / person` budget semantics against the existing `goal.raise=auto|person` grammar during this design; do not invent a new value in the driver. Preserve claim identity on unrelated ledger mutations through the existing goal owner.
