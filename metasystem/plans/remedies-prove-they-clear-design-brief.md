## Intent

A remedy the machinery prints is an act that clears the condition when followed, or it says plainly that nothing is to be done and who clears it when. A health role whose automatic healing ended re-arms on a person's one act instead of staying dead forever. A question an agent must ask goes first to the seat that holds the person's power of attorney, and to the person only when no grant covers the act or the grant ended; a refusal class that repeats across seats within an hour becomes one register entry with one notification instead of six questions. A person's act is never refused except to prevent damage; an agent never gains a person's power; a person's decision always takes effect (No HAL 9000).

## What the design must deliver (units; intent, not re-cut)

1. **Remedies that clear (finding 9, first unit).** One owner of every health role's remedy, by role and cause: the act (a public verb for the actor who reads it, or a plain act outside the metasystem) and what clears it (the role's own check reads alive at the next observation, or "nothing to do: <who> clears it <when>"). No remedy names a read (`list`, `show`, `check`, `status`) as the act; no person's remedy names an agent's verb. The health tests run the declared act through the public verb in the bed and assert the role clears; a remedy that does not clear in the bed does not ship.
2. **The breaker re-arms on a person's act, and ledger attention clears (finding 9, the design question).** A person's `alert clear` of the seat's health alert re-arms every role whose automatic healing ended, so the steward tries its remedy again after the person's own act (a login, a fix), and accepts the shared ledger's current tip as examined for the ledger-attention role (the plan's option "the steward accepting the examined tip on a person's acknowledgment"). A remedy that did nothing is never recorded as completed.
**(Moved 2026-10-08 to the follow-up brief.)** 3. **Ask routing (finding 11, first unit).** `question ask` routes an ordinary question (kind `other`, including an act request whose first line is the command) to the coordinator seat as an `agent ask` while `question.route` is `auto` and a live general grant names that seat; to the person through the channel otherwise. The record says where it went; `question show`, `question list` and the UI read it. Authority kinds (`stop`, `budget-above-norm`, `carry`) go to the person as today.
**(Moved 2026-10-08 to the follow-up brief.)** 4. **The routed question's answer, and its return to the person.** The coordinator's reply becomes the question's recorded answer and wakes the asker's wait; when the grant ends, is revoked, or the ask's deadline passes with no reply, the question is posted to the person once, never suppressed as a repeat.
**(Moved 2026-10-08 to the follow-up brief.)** 5. **The refusal kind of the register (finding 11, second half).** The host's lane steward counts the questions open across the computer's seats by refusal class within an hour; at three it opens one `refusal` alert in the lane's store with the questions as evidence and one notification; while that alert is open, a further question of the class is recorded, routed to the coordinator as usual, but not posted to the person; `question retry` posts one on demand; `alert list --verbose` shows the questions and their commands.

## Acceptance

1. No printed health remedy names a read-only verb as the act, and no person's remedy names an agent's verb; a static test over the remedy owner fails on either.
2. For every role whose remedy is a public verb the bed can run, the public verb runs in the bed and the role reads alive at the next observation; for the roles the bed cannot run (process roles), the act is `system start` for a person and the owner's test pins it.
3. A dead ledger-attention role is cleared by `alert clear` of the seat's health alert; after the breaker ended, `alert clear` makes the next tick run the role's remedy again; a no-op examination is recorded `FAILED` with its cause, never `PASS_COMPLETE`.
**(Moved 2026-10-08 to the follow-up brief.)** 4. With `question.route auto`, a declared coordinator and a live grant, `question ask G ...` of kind `other` writes the record, sends one `agent ask` to the coordinator with the question and the command, posts nothing to the channel; with `person`, with no grant, or for an authority kind, it posts to the channel as today.
**(Moved 2026-10-08 to the follow-up brief.)** 5. The coordinator's `agent reply` ends the asker's `question wait` with the reply as the answer; a grant that ended or an ask past its deadline posts the question to the person exactly once.
**(Moved 2026-10-08 to the follow-up brief.)** 6. Three questions of one refusal class across the host's seats within an hour open one `refusal` alert in the lane's store with one notification; a fourth is recorded and routed but not posted to the person; `question retry` posts it; `alert list --verbose` lists the questions with their commands; the alert clears when the class has been quiet for an hour or a person clears it.
7. A person's explicit act is never refused by any of the above; an agent gains no authority from a routed ask, an alert, or a copied command.
