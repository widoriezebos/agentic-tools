## 1. Intent (verbatim)

Wido, 2026-09-29, on slice 1, binding here too: "As you can see above, trying to commit something and push something while at the helm is not easy at all. But I really want this to be as easy as possible, even from a tool like GitHub Desktop. While at the helm, it should just do what the human wants to do. [...] figure out what the easiest, most elegant, and intuitive non restricting way that is still safe is for all commands like this. Once at the helm, I don't want any magic flags or anything else."

His go for this page, after being told slices 2 and 3 were not started: "go, build all" (2026-09-29).

On slice 3: "It is really important that the helm return always is successful. So the follow-ups, concluding the goal, etc., etc., should finish successfully. The fact that the human was at the helm should have no impact on that. And if we need to repair or add stuff to make it successful, then that should be fully automatic." Then: "Well, they can still ask me questions about closing the goal or not, because I might not want to close the goal when giving back the helm. So, questions are okay. My point is that whatever is done with an answer should always succeed." And: "And as always, build the smallest thing possible that works. We're not building a nuclear reactor here."

What happened: on another machine a person held the helm with an agent session beside them, in the seat's checkout. `goal open` and `goal approve` from the agent's shell were refused as a person's act ("--by W was refused"; the relay word had expired). `goal claim` said from the agent's shell "a person at a terminal runs goal claim" and from the person's terminal "claim is an agent session's act" (`internal/goal/verbs.go:1319-1324`, the same text in `ClaimArc` at `:4147-4152`); then the claim wanted `METASYSTEM_OWNER_LINEAGE`; `work land` wanted a claimed goal and a started seat; `goal done --reason` from the person's terminal worked. Every refusal asked who was calling while a proven person held the helm.

## 2. How it feels, after slices 2 and 3

Wido runs `metasystem helm take --reason "landing the helm slices by hand"` at his terminal; the take proves him and enrolls that terminal (`cmd/metasystem/intent_helm.go:119-127`, `:150-159`). He asks the agent beside him, in the checkout, to open the goal. The agent types `metasystem goal open g1-s76 --intent … --risk … --basis … --next …`, no `--by`, no lineage. It prints `opened g1-s76` and, on stderr, one line: `HUMAN AT THE HELM (wido): the person proof yields to the helm for goal open; caller DELEGATE; recorded in …/.git/metasystem/helm-yields.log`. The ledger's History line reads `human:wido`, the proof record beside it says `helm: by=wido class=DELEGATE`. `goal approve g1-s76` from the same shell: the same. The agent commits on main; GitHub Desktop pushes (slice 1). Nobody types `goal claim` or `work land`: the claim binds a working session, and the helm's landing is the push.

`metasystem helm return`. It removes the signature, then prints: `returned the helm wido held: landing the helm slices by hand`, `yields since the take: 4`, `commits at the helm on main: 7c8ba19 the guard yields at the helm (on origin/main), 6f7393d production wiring (on origin/main)`, `acts at the helm: goal open g1-s76 (DELEGATE), goal approve g1-s76 (DELEGATE)`. At a terminal it asks `Conclude goal g1-s76 with these commits? [y/N]`; on `y`, `Conclusion [Enter: landed at the helm by wido: 2 commits 7c8ba19, 6f7393d]`; then `Ask independent readers for feedback on these commits? [y/N]`. Each answer's act prints its own confirmation (`goal done g1-s76: confirmed`; `read:REF requested; metasystem work status shows it`). Without a terminal it asks nothing, keeps the goal open and prints the two exact commands. It ends with `the ledger hook is enrolled`, `supervision: recovered` (or `supervision re-arms at the next turn end`) and `the machinery is at the helm again`, exit 0, whatever any step said.

## 3. Step 2 (slice 2): at the helm, the person proof yields

**Behavior.** While the seat is at the helm, every act that today proves a person through `humanauthority.Prove` is admitted when the caller sits in the seat's primary checkout, whoever the process is: the proof records the holder as the person, the real caller class, and one helm yield. Nothing else about the act changes: the ledger line, the proof record and the verb's own checks are those of the holder's act at the enrolled terminal.

**Discriminator: checkout scope, as slice 1, plus the machinery exclusion below.** `lease.ClassifyAt` answers DELEGATE at the first ancestor that is an agent runtime (`internal/lease/classify.go:423-426`), for the agent beside the person and for a job alike, so class cannot separate them. Of the two candidates the page takes the checkout: the verb's working directory is the seat's primary worktree, tested by slice 1's three-answer predicate (the work tree's on-disk `.git` entry, the effective git dir and the common dir resolve to one directory; s1 `internal/landing/landpath/precommit.go:171-211`). Why this one: (a) it is built, reviewed by Astra twice (HA-01, HA-03) and pinned by seven tests; (b) jobs that write are launched into a linked worktree (`cmd/metasystem/intent_worktree.go:237-243`; `internal/gittree/detached.go:250`; a job record's `workspaceRoot`, `internal/dispatch/build.go:557`) and a verb's cwd is where its shell runs; (c) machinery in the chain alone would need `custodyIdentities` and `verifiedStewardBinaries` (`classify.go:279-360`, `:509-523`), which live in `lease`, and `humanauthority` cannot import `lease` (so the exclusion is wired in `cmd/metasystem`, below): `lease` imports `steward` (`classify.go:13`) and `steward` imports `humanauthority`, a cycle; it would take a new package or a second walk. **Plus one exclusion (revision 2, HB-01):** a job can run in the primary checkout without any `cd`: a dispatch that requests no writes and names no workspace launches `shared-checkout` in the repository scope (`internal/delegation/dispatch_phase.go:321-323`, `:423-432`). So `helmAdmits` also refuses when any ancestor of the caller, over the whole chain (not stopping at the first runtime), is a supervision or adapter-supervisor process in the custody records or the steward's own plumbing. That reading lives in `lease` (`classify.go:283`, `:513`); `cmd/metasystem`, where `helmAdmits` is wired, imports `lease`, so the cycle does not arise: `lease` gains one exported function, `MachineryAncestor(root string, caller int64) (bool, error)`, the same walk as `ClassifyAt` over `ParentPid`/`StartedAt` that reports whether a custody or steward ancestor exists anywhere above the caller; an error is "not admitted". The Partner and the person's own agent have no such ancestor and stay admitted. The Partner's turns run in the checkout (`internal/ui/partner/host.go:1369-1375`, `command.Dir = checkout`) and are admitted: they are the signed-in person's own conversation. A person's agent working in a linked goal worktree is refused as today (slice 1 D-scope).

**Chokepoint: `humanauthority.Prove` (`internal/humanauthority/authority.go:850-941`).** Every person-only act reaches it: the goal verbs through `proveGoalHumanAuthorityFor` (`cmd/metasystem/goalsync_mutations.go:2170-2200`, `prove` = `ProveOrTemporaryGoalAuthority`, `authority.go:417-426`), `actorArgs` (`cmd/metasystem/intent_goals.go:152-190`, `proveHuman` = `Prove`, `goalsync_mutations.go:521`, `:566`), the person/agent helper of the planning verbs (`cmd/metasystem/intent_planning.go:470-530`), the lineage derivation (`goalsync_mutations.go:667-690`), split ratification (`:3148`), the breach-stop orderer (`cmd/metasystem/delegate_host.go:46`) and disk clean (`cmd/metasystem/intent_disk.go:359`). `Prove` gains one owner seam, `var AtHelm func(root string, invokerPID int64) (HelmGrant, bool)`, nil in the library and in every existing test; `HelmGrant{By, Since, Class, Checkout string}` is declared in `humanauthority` (no `helm` import). When the walk ends in a refusal (`:882-940`, any outcome) and `AtHelm` grants, `Prove` returns `HelmProof(root, grant, now)`, one constructor beside `SignedInSessionProof` (`:288-306`): `Outcome` proven, `Grade` enrolled, `TerminalRef`, `TerminalGeneration` and the private observed terminal id from the enrollment when readable (the take enrolled the holder's terminal; revision 2, HB-03: `goal done --by` with no lineage derives it from `ObservedTerminalID()` and refuses an empty one, `cmd/metasystem/goalsync_mutations.go:659-665`; `Prove` fills the same field from the enrollment at `authority.go:934`), `Nodes` as walked, `Helm: &grant`, observed for this root. `Valid()` (`:117-166`) gains the arm `FixtureOnly` has (`:118-125`): a helm proof is valid when observed, schema 1, proven, dated and `Helm.By` non-empty; so `ValidFor`, `TerminalValidFor` and `EnrolledTerminalFor` (`:203-222`) hold, done's terminal-grade rows pass (`internal/goal/verbs.go:320-346`, `:2604-2620`) and the lineage derivation binds the act to the enrolled terminal (`goalsync_mutations.go:667-690`). `resolveGoalHuman` (`:1499-1517`) names `proof.Helm.By` before reading the enrollment, so the actor is the holder even where the enrolled name differs. `RecordProof` (`:952-954`) writes the proof as it is; the `helm` block is the audit trail. Two sites ignore a helm proof on purpose: the take's enrollment grade (`intent_helm.go:132-137`), else a re-take at another terminal would call itself enrolled, and the interface's boot proof (`internal/ui/act/act.go:113-123`), because that proof outlives the helm; both read `proof.Helm == nil`.

**Wiring and the yield.** `cmd/metasystem` wires `humanauthority.AtHelm` once, at startup, to `helmAdmits(root, pid)`: `cwd := os.Getwd()`; `state := helm.Active(cwd)`; when active, and `helm.Locate(root).CommonDir` equals `helm.Locate(cwd).CommonDir` (revision 2, HB-02: the act's authority root, which `--repo` can move, `cmd/metasystem/intent.go:625`, must be the seat whose helm is held; else not admitted), and `lease.MachineryAncestor(root, pid)` answers false, `landpath.PrimaryCheckout(git, toplevel(cwd))`, the s1 predicate exported (s1 `helmAdmission`, `precommit.go:171-211`, calls it; same tests); when both hold it records `helm.RecordYield(cwd, helm.Yield{Boundary: "person-proof", Gate: "human-proof", Would: "refuse", Subject: "verb=<public verb> class=<lease.ClassifyAt class, or unavailable> cwd=<cwd>"})`, prints the one stderr line of §2 and returns `HelmGrant{By: state.By, Since: state.Since, Class: class, Checkout: common dir}`. The class is the classifier's own answer (`classify.go:389-477`), never rewritten. `helmAdmits` has owner seams for cwd, git, classify and yield, so `cmd/metasystem` tests fake it as they fake the guard. The stderr line names `(signature unreadable)` under slice 1's D-malformed, By "unknown".

**The claim: removed as a step, not answered with an identity.** `goal claim` stays an agent session's act (`verbs.go:1319-1324`); `work land` stays the session's landing, needing the claim and the holder at its boundaries (`internal/landing/landpath/commit.go:102`, `land.go:209`; `cmd/metasystem/goal_branch.go:449-473`). At the helm neither is needed: the landing is the person's commit and push (slice 1), and the conclusion is `goal done`, a person's act that needs no claim (`verbs.go:2586-2640`: a goal claimed by another pair or opened by the human needs a terminal-grade proof, which the helm proof is). The lineage refusal (`goalsync_mutations.go:655-660`) is only reached by an act naming neither a person nor a session; at the helm the intent layer names the holder from the proof before the owner runs (`intent_goals.go:171-189`; `intent_planning.go:494-530`).

**What still refuses, and why.** From a linked worktree: as today (jobs live there). `helm take` from an agent's shell (`ProveTerminal`, `authority.go:670-709`, untouched: the take is the one proof). `session stop` (`cmd/metasystem/session_stop.go:22-30`, `:41-47`, class HUMAN then `ProveTerminal`; moot at the helm, the Stop hook allows every turn). The lease write gates (`internal/lease/verbs.go:428-441`: HUMAN passes, DELEGATE not; goal branch, evidence gc, shutdown) and `system start`/`steward arm` (`cmd/metasystem/process_verbs.go:405-425`) from the agent's shell: not in the transcript, later. A checkout declared the brain (`goalsync_mutations.go:740-761`, a brain seat). A seat whose enrollment is unreadable at the helm (the take already printed the remedy, `intent_helm.go:150-159`).

**Safety.** Admission needs three things at once: a signature only a person at a terminal writes; the verb's cwd in the primary checkout, where no job is launched; an act the enrolled person could make anyway, at the grade their own terminal gives. Every admitted act leaves a yield naming the real class, a ledger line under the holder's name and a proof record with the helm block, and return reads them back. Background delegates keep running under the helm with none of this: their cwd is their worktree, or, when a job runs in the shared checkout, a supervision or adapter-supervisor ancestor refuses it (HB-01). The act's root must be the helm's seat (HB-02).

## 4. Step 3 (slice 3): `helm return` catches up

**Behavior.** `runIntentHelmReturn` (`intent_helm.go:254-282`) keeps its first moves: remove the signature (`:256`), decode the removed record, append the return to `helm.log`. Then `helmCatchUp(seat, record, since)` runs in place of the plain report, every part best-effort and idempotent, each failure one line, exit 0 always; `helmHeldBatches` (`:330-359`) is called as today and not touched (batch 21 changes it).
1. **Readback.** The yields since the take (as `helmYieldCount` scans them, `:311-328`). Pre-commit yields carry `branch=… tree=…` (s1 `helmSubject`, `precommit.go:215-228`); for each branch named, `git log --format=%H%x1f%T%x1f%s --since=<take At> <branch>` in `seat.Checkout` (read-only git, as `goalBranchGit` runs it, `goal_branch.go:28`), matched on tree id: `commit <sha7> <subject> (on <remote>/<branch>` when `git merge-base --is-ancestor` says so, else `not pushed)`. A yield with no matching commit prints `admitted, no commit found`. Person-proof yields print `act <verb> (<class>)`.
2. **Questions**, only when `os.Stdin` is a character device; otherwise nothing is asked, the goal stays open and the exact commands are printed. Q1 `Conclude goal G with these commits? [y/N]`, G proposed from the branch a yield names (`refs/heads/goal/<id>`) or the one live goal claimed by this seat's holder (`lease.CurrentHolder`, `internal/lease/verbs.go:337-357`, matched on machine and lineage); with no candidate, `Conclude a goal with these commits? [goal id / Enter keeps every goal open]`. Then `Conclusion [Enter: landed at the helm by <By>: <n> commits <sha7,…>]`. Q2 `Ask independent readers for feedback on these commits? [y/N]`. Enter answers no; nothing irreversible happens by default.
3. **What an answer does, and why it succeeds.** Yes to Q1 runs `goal done G --reason <conclusion> --by <By>` in-process, the call `runIntentGoalDone` makes (`intent_goals.go:815-828`) with `dependencies.proveHuman` (`goalsync_mutations.go:521`, a seam) answering `humanauthority.HelmProof(root, grant, now)`, the constructor `Prove` itself uses, built from the removed record: the holder's take is the authority, and the proof record says so. Done then needs only its own preconditions (`verbs.go:2586-2620`): no open read item, obligation or blocker; the transcript's case has none. Yes to Q2 writes `<common>/metasystem/helm-<take At>.patch` (`git diff <parent of the oldest helm commit>..<tip>` per branch) and beside it `helm-<take At>.brief.md` (revision 2, HB-04: the read refuses without a brief, `intent_manual_review.go:40`, and `StartRead` reads a non-empty file, `standalone_read.go:133`, `:175-177`): three lines naming the commits (sha and subject), the scope ("the diff in the patch") and the purpose ("feedback only; no goal is bound"); and runs `runIntentReviewDiagnostic` in-process (`cmd/metasystem/intent_manual_review.go:33-58`, `launch.UnitRunner.StartRead`, `internal/launch/standalone_read.go:129`; no identity gate in `internal/launch`), feedback only, no goal bound, so it cannot block a conclusion. The goal-bound review is not the verb: `work review --commit SHA --goal G` reads a unit on `origin/goal/G` under the seat's claim (`cmd/metasystem/intent_unit_review.go:377-379`; `goal_branch.go:206-244`, `:449-473`), which helm commits on main never have.
4. **Bookkeeping, not asked.** `ledgerfence.Ensure(root)` (`internal/ledgerfence/fence.go:35`, idempotent; every goal verb runs it). Supervision: the recover path `system start --if-down` takes, `runUpWith(--recover-only --if-down)` (`cmd/metasystem/intent_process.go:580-591`), which the Stop hook runs from an agent with no person's word (`internal/hooks/runtime_hook_stop.go:518-524`); when it fails, the line says the Stop hook re-arms at the next turn end (today's line, `intent_helm.go:279`). The steward decided nothing at the helm (`internal/steward/tick.go:236-253`) and resumes at its next tick. Nothing else gates a hand landing: `goal done` checks no receipt or landing record (`verbs.go:2562-2640`); the receipt line is a landing-time observation of a candidate (`cmd/metasystem/landing_path.go:321-336`); the batch lane's records are listed, not required (`helmHeldBatches`).

**Seams.** `helmOwners` (`intent_helm.go:27-34`) gains `git func(dir string, args ...string) (string, error)`, `stdinTerminal func() bool`, `ask func(prompt string) (string, bool)`, `holder func(root string) (lease.CurrentHolderView, error)`, `done func(inv, id, by, reason string, proof humanauthority.Proof) intentResult`, `read func(inv, patch, brief string) intentResult`, `recover func(scope processScope) string`, `fence func(root string) error`; zero values are the production calls named above. `withDefaults` (`:36-61`) fills them. Return's help gains one clause: "then asks whether to conclude the goal and whether to request a read; without a terminal it prints the commands".

## 6. Later, when it hurts

- Goal-state refusals of an answer not caused by the helm (an open read item, a carry obligation, a breach stop already on the goal): return prints the verb's own reason and keeps the goal open; a steward completion of the conclusion when the item closes is the later step (Astra HB round 1: not reached in the first-use case).

- The lease write gates and `system start`/`steward arm`/`session stop` from the agent's shell at the helm (one helm arm in `RequireHolderAt` and one in `humanTerminalCheck`).
- A conclusion deferred until an open read closes, completed by the steward, so both answers can be yes in every case.
- One task-receipt line per goal concluded at return (`receipt add`).
- Return naming the goal from commit subjects when no branch or claim names one.

## 7. Not in it

No change to `helm take`'s proof, `ProveTerminal`, `ClassifyAt`, `ClassifyVerbAt`, `RequireHolder` (`lease` only gains `MachineryAncestor`), `goal claim`, `work land`, `helmHeldBatches`, the hook script, the guard's messages or the Partner. No flag, token or environment variable. No push and no pre-push hook. No admission in linked worktrees or other clones. No new queue, store or state machine: an answer runs the existing verb in-process, or prints it. No conclusion, read or repair without a terminal answer, except the fence and the supervision recovery, which are today's idempotent paths.
