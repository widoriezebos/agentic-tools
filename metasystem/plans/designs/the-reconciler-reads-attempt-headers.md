# Design: the reconciler reads attempt heads and skips settled attempts

- Id: 01M4ZB0000000000000000MEM2
- Kind: design
- Status: accepted
- Goals: the-reconciler-reads-attempt-headers
- Evidence: lane test finding 335 (2026-10-15 17:31: the m1e seat's job reaper at 1.0 GB physical footprint, the landing seat's reaper at 24 MB); analysis/memory-hogs-and-root-causes-2026-10-15.md H2; Astra rounds 1 (analysis) and 1–2 (pages) folded.

## Problem

Every 60 s the seat's job reaper (`supervise component --component reaper`) ends its pass with `proofrun.ReconcileAttempts` (`cmd/metasystem/supervise_component.go:378`), which calls `readAttemptsSkippingUnreadable` (`internal/proofrun/attemptload.go:518`): every attempt record of the checkout is read through `ReadAttempt` (`attempt.go:374`: the whole file into memory, a strict decode of the whole record with `DisallowUnknownFields`, including `deliveryReceiptBytes`, base64 of 12.5 MB in the largest record, and `testResult`, 9.4 MB) and every decoded attempt is retained in one slice for the pass (`attemptload.go:523`). The m1e checkout holds 268 attempt records from September (518 MB of JSON; 267 terminal, 0 live), so each tick allocates about a gigabyte to learn that nothing is live, and for every terminal attempt re-runs `clearObservation` and `settleTerminalRecords` (`reconcile.go:83-91`), re-reading every process record. `LiveAttempts` (`reconcile.go:333`) and `liveAttemptsOtherThan` (`attemptload.go:500`) scan the same way. A live attempt is not small either: `RecordTestResultAt` persists `testResult` while the attempt is still live (`attempt.go:1550-1594`), and a finalised attempt carries `deliveryReceiptBytes` and `testResult` together (`attempt.go:1529`). `ReadAttempt`'s validation is the authority before any action on a record: the attempt schema (`attempt.go:389-393`) and the nested result schema (`:395`), an id that is not the file's name and a foreign control root (`:403-410`), unknown fields and trailing data, unsupported older schemas and invalid contents (`:499`), launcher and accounting identities, process keys, and the terminal's invariants: consistent timestamps, result against exit status, success against cancellation, a valid observed duration (`:563-587`); `reconcileOne` acts on a dead launcher before any later full read (`reconcile.go:127`). The steward's health duty already reads heads with a streaming reader (`internal/steward/health.go:2109`). Retention (`retention.go`) empties payload directories and never touches the record.

## Decisions

D1. **The scan lists; every action follows a full, validated read; a settled attempt is never read.** The pass lists the attempts directory once. An id with the settled sidecar (D2) is skipped without opening its record. Every other record is read with `ReadAttempt` (full, strict, validated as today) before anything is done with it: a live attempt enters `reconcileOne` as today; a terminal attempt is settled (`clearObservation`, `settleTerminalRecords`) as today, and if settled gets the sidecar and is never read again. A record `ReadAttempt` refuses is reported as unreadable, stays pending and gets no sidecar, as today. On this seat the first pass after the change reads the 267 terminal records once (the gigabyte once); later passes read only live attempts, terminal attempts whose settlement is still incomplete, and unreadable records; successfully settled attempts are skipped.

D2. **The settled sidecar.** `settleTerminalRecords` (`reconcile.go:237-250`) reports whether every process record of the attempt was read and is done, or was marked done by a `markDone` that returned no error (today it reports nothing and drops `markDone` errors). After a terminal attempt read in full by `ReadAttempt` (so its terminal, identities and process keys passed every validation) is settled by that answer, the pass writes `<attempts>/<id>.reconcile-settled` once (atomic write, beside the existing `<id>.reconcile-observed`, `reconcile.go:295`). The sidecar is evidence of a settlement established from the validated record and its process records, never authority: a live attempt never gets one (process publication refuses a terminal attempt, `attempt.go:1393`, so a settled attempt cannot come alive); the sidecar authorises no signal, no reservation release and no scratch deletion; a missing or removed sidecar costs one full read; a sidecar whose record is gone is removed by the pass. Retention and the disk classes list `*.json` only (`retention.go:204, :488`), so nothing fails closed on a sidecar.

D3. **The counts read heads, streaming, stopping at the first payload key; a head is advisory.** `LiveAttempts` and `liveAttemptsOtherThan` take no action; they count and render. They read each unsettled record with `ReadAttemptHead`: a streaming reader over a buffered file (`json.NewDecoder(bufio.NewReader(file))`, token by token) that collects `schemaVersion`, `attemptId`, `goalId`, `startedAt`, `endedAt` (top level, `attempt.go:167`), `observedMinutes`, `controlRoot`, `launcher`, `processKeys`, the complete `terminal` object (`AttemptTerminal`, `attempt.go:113`, when present) and `cancellationIntent` (`attempt.go:177`), and returns as soon as it meets the first payload key, `deliveryReceipt`, `deliveryReceiptBytes`, `testResult` or `load` (`attempt.go:193-200`, all after the head fields in the record's order), or the end of the object, whichever comes first, so no payload value is ever tokenised, for a terminal or a live record alike. The head reader validates what it holds, with the same helpers `ReadAttempt` uses (extracted from their inline form): the attempt schema, both the newer-version refusal (`:389-393`) and the supported-version membership (`:500`), the file-name identity and the control-root binding (`:403-410`), the launcher identity, and the head-local terminal invariants (`:563-587`: timestamps, result against exit status, success against cancellation, observed duration); a head that fails them is reported as unreadable, never counted as terminal or live. What the head cannot see stays exclusively with `ReadAttempt`: the nested result schema (`:395`), unknown fields, trailing data and payload contents; the full reader keeps refusing those, and because every action follows a full read (D1), a head never authorises anything.

D4. **Bounded by bytes, measured on the first pass.** Over a fixture of 300 terminal attempts whose records carry a 1 MB `deliveryReceiptBytes` and a `testResult` after the head and one done process record each, plus one live attempt with a 1 MB retained `testResult` and two process records of a dead launcher: the first `LiveAttempts` and `liveAttemptsOtherThan` pass allocates fewer than 8 MB in total (`runtime.ReadMemStats` before and after, `TotalAlloc` delta, one `runtime.GC()` before, no warm-up); the first `ReconcileAttempts` pass reads each of the 300 initially terminal records once, leaves a sidecar on each, and reconciles the live one as today's fixtures expect (that attempt takes its existing transition reads, `reconcile.go:279` and `attempt.go:1470` inside finalisation, becomes terminal, is settled on the next pass and skipped from the pass after); a counting opener on the fixture root proves the 300 are never opened again, and a later pass allocates fewer than 8 MB. The bound covers the attempt scan and the settlement in that fixture; scratch recovery (`ReconcileScratch`, `reconcile.go:100`, with its own `ReadAttempt` at `scratch.go:870`) is a separate contributor, measured separately and not changed here.

## Units

- U1 (D1–D4): the sidecar-aware listing in `ReconcileAttempts` (`reconcile.go:56-107`); `settleTerminalRecords` reporting settlement; the sidecar written and honoured; `ReadAttemptHead` in `attemptload.go` with the shared validation helpers; `LiveAttempts` and `liveAttemptsOtherThan` on heads; the tests of D4 plus: a terminal record whose `markDone` write is refused gets no sidecar and is retried next pass; an unreadable process record gets no sidecar; a live attempt with a live launcher, and one with an uninspectable launcher, reconciled twice: no sidecar exists and the second pass examines them again; a sidecar planted for a live attempt is ignored by the verdict path (`attempt.go:424` reads in full) and removed when its record is gone; through BOTH readers: a newer attempt schema, a foreign control root, an id that differs from the file name, a malformed launcher identity, and contradictory terminals (mismatched timestamps, success with a non-zero exit status, failure with zero, success with cancellation, an invalid observed duration) are reported unreadable and get no sidecar; through the full reader: a newer result schema, an unknown field and trailing data are refused and the attempt stays pending; the full-read boundary before any action, on both kinds: `ReconcileAttempts` on a terminal record whose head is valid but whose `testResult.schemaVersion` is newer than supported reports it unreadable, leaves its process records unchanged and writes no sidecar on two passes; and on a LIVE record with a valid head, a newer result schema, a dead launcher and a live recorded child, reconciled twice: reported unreadable, zero signals sent (a counting stop prober), process records unchanged, no sidecar (`reconcileOne` invokes the stop ladder at `reconcile.go:127` before the later full reads, so a head must never reach it); the existing reconcile fixtures keep their verdicts. Mutations: settle on the validated head without `ReadAttempt` (the terminal newer-result-schema test fails: the head cannot see that refusal); pass heads into `reconcileOne` for live attempts (the live newer-result-schema test sees a signal); write the sidecar before settlement is confirmed (the refused-write test fails); write it for a live attempt (the twice-reconciled live test fails); tokenise past the first payload key (the byte bound fails on the live record with a retained result); drop a head-local terminal invariant (its refusal test fails). About 160 net-added production lines; the changed-line allocation also counts the validation extraction and the replaced call sites, tests budgeted separately.

## Not in scope

The record's shape (payload fields stay in the record; moving `deliveryReceiptBytes` and `testResult` into the retained-proof directory that retention governs is a later design), the other full-record readers (retention's plan, scratch reconciliation, coverage, the launcher), the steward's own peak (unattributed, analysis H2b), the reaper's job sweep and its 60-second interval.
