## 1. Intent

A unit round that built green and was read is collected and continues by itself. The read already holds the verdict. The machine takes it, decides, and goes on: close and commit, or a correction round. A person acts only where the read cannot be had twice, where the decision is his, or where he stopped the run.

Any other path is unknown.

  - The dead `UnitRunner.ExaminationRoot` (`unit_run.go:202-203`, `intent_work.go:515`) is removed.
