## Problem (the ui seat held a claim for 3 h 47 min with no session, and no verb could free it)

1. A person who starts MetaSystem from their own terminal is pushed into announcing that terminal as an agent session main. `up` derives a session identity from a runtime signature in the caller's ancestry or, without one, demands `--pid <session-pid> --start-time <epoch>` (`internal/up/up.go:855, 1065`); the pair is recorded with provenance `explicit-ancestry-fallback` (`up.go:384-391`, `lease.AnnounceWithProofAt` at `up.go:864`, keys `internal/census/announcement.go:31-41`) in `artifacts/agents/mains/` (`internal/census/run.go:668`), and the announcement claims the checkout lease `worktree-lease.json` there (`internal/lease/verbs.go:218`, `internal/lease/lease.go:60`). On 2026-10-16 18:25 the steward did that for the ui seat from Wido's pane (shell pid 8458): `up --pid 8458 --start-time 1788676628`.
2. A shell that matches no runtime signature is absent from census inventory (`internal/census/run.go:206`), but `supplementWorkers` (`internal/steward/universe.go:56`) probes every pid-bearing record in `artifacts/agents/mains` (`:23`) and counts each live one in `Live` and `LiveSeatMains` (`:120-122`), folded in at `internal/steward/census.go:45-48`: the pane is "the seat's main".
3. The backlog classifier returns `WorkClaimable` whenever any claimable goal exists, before the branch for this machine's own claims (`internal/steward/openwork.go:111-119`), so the held, ready claim asks-clear-on-their-effect counted as "claimable work" and `owned` was false.
4. `seatDecision` then takes `!owned && workers.LiveSeatMains > 0` and returns `Decide(Snapshot{Work: work})` (`internal/steward/seat_start.go:459-461`), whose text is "claimable backlog has no live claim or delegate process; …" (`internal/steward/verdict.go:112-114`): the ladder never reached `PlanSeat` (`internal/steward/seat_ladder.go:165`). Every tick from 18:55 to 22:42 notified that text (finding 357).
5. Nothing could end the announcement. `session stop --by Wido` from the announced pane: the shell is classed MAIN, "only a person can stop a session quietly: an agent started this shell" (`cmd/metasystem/session_stop.go:58-59, 119`, `cmd/metasystem/process_verbs.go:508-511`); from a plain human pane: "the current checkout holder lacks a main identity, announced session, or lease epoch" (`session_stop.go:79-80`). That refusal's missing field is not reproduced (D4). The same shell stayed HUMAN in other checkouts: the demotion is per checkout (finding 355). The steward moved the announcement aside by hand (S358).
6. After that, the ladder held the seat on another goal's record (finding 365). The current launch seat-ba0b8339b4808206 ran goal commit-goal-binding from 18:21:50; the steward paused that goal to redirect the seat, and the launch was cancelled 18:59:23 with outcome no-progress. `abnormalRestartState` (`internal/steward/revive.go:456-558`) reads the current launch (`:483-488`) whatever its goal and holds: "the automatic revival produced no retained work progress; person's remedy: metasystem machine revive <ui>" (`:538-539`, `:450-452`), checked before `PlanSeat` (`seat_start.go:484-489`) and again inside `startSeatWithDependencies` just before launch (`seat_start.go:614`, `:739-747`). The remedy binds that same launch (`seat_start.go:664`) and refuses: "the failed seat's approved held work is no longer held by this seat" (`:701-704`). The held claim has no launch history: a person's ordinary redirection deadlocks the ladder.

Root cause: one announcement record and one worker count stand for a person's terminal and an agent's session main, and the ladder's text names its verdict class, not its evidence. The abnormal hold reads the latest launch, not the launch of the work it blocks.

## Decisions (round 5: Astra round 4 folded; accepted by the stop rule)

D1. **A person's terminal is never announced as a main, and never holds the checkout as one.**

- *Predicate.* When `up` resolves the identity from an explicit `--pid/--start-time` pair, the lease owner (`internal/lease`) decides whether the NAMED process is a person's terminal. It requires positive terminal evidence and observed identity, ancestry and custody, excludes the target and its ancestors against every installed runtime signature, and leaves unknown or unreadable cases on today's announcement path. Signatures: `allAdapterSignatures` (`internal/lease/classify.go:266-270`); today's walk starts at the parent (`:421-428`), the predicate examines the target too; custody: `custodyIdentities` (`:415`); terminal evidence: the controlling-terminal observation that separates HUMAN from UNTRUSTED (`:452-457` vs `:480`). Attorney promotion (`internal/lease/person.go:10-17`) plays no part.
- *Branch.* For a person's terminal `up` records the lifecycle identity and takes the PERSON-TERMINAL branch: no announcement, no main-session association, no holder work (`verbs.go:218` claims at announcement today; `verbs.go:381` answers `Holder=false` without one; `up.go:891-893` must not read `CurrentHolder` here). Startup continues with an explicit non-main result, said in one line ("your terminal <pid> is a person's; no agent main is announced; the steward's ladder may start a seat").
- *Legacy candidate.* An authenticated announcement makes its own process MAIN (`classify.go:408-409`). For a legacy fallback announcement, exclude only that exact record from the classification, keep all other evidence, and revalidate the candidate's process identity before withdrawal. A signed announcement, or another authenticated announcement in the target's ancestry, keeps the record untouched.
- *Generation guard (SEAT-R3-01, SEAT-R4-01).* Under the checkout lock, withdrawal retains both records whenever unfinished jobs or monitored runs belong to the current OR an older lease generation, or an unfinished generation-less steward continuation remains; unreadable state also retains both, and the dependent work is reported. Why: a missing lease selects `freshClaim` (`internal/lease/claim.go:102`), which resets the generation to 1 and stamps cleanup complete (`:156-176`). A takeover advances the generation (`:256`) and writes the new lease (`:281`) before it sweeps older jobs and runs (`:286`); an interrupted sweep is resumed on a later claim (`:182`, `completeInterruptedSweep` at `:312`). So a fallback shell can hold generation 2 over unfinished generation-1 work; the sweep also concludes an epoch-less `steward-continuation` (`internal/lease/sweep.go:112-119`). The guard reads the sweep's stores: non-terminal job records (`sweep.go:20-28`) and the dispatch runs (`sweep.go:66-68`).
- *Withdrawal.* Registry-then-checkout-lease locking (`Announce`'s order: `verbs.go:121`, then `claim.go:82`; the registry lock alone, `verbs.go:42`, does not exclude `Renew` at `:497-499` or `WithHeld` at `:536-545`); recheck candidate and holder identity; remove the lease before its announcement; report success only after both. An interrupted removal leaves the announcement, so the next pass retries, and the supplementary scan still counts it (`universe.go:120-122`), which blocks revival until complete. Then a successor acquires the checkout while the shell lives (`claim.go:108` preserves a live holder today).
- *Wiring.* `internal/lease` imports `census` and `steward` (`classify.go:11, 14`), so neither may import the repair. The composition calls it beside both census entrypoints: the watcher (`cmd/metasystem/supervise_watchercfg.go:45-53`) and the steward (`steward.RuntimeWorkerCensus`, `cmd/metasystem/steward_verbs.go:45`, `cmd/metasystem/intent_machine.go:112`). The repair is reported once and counts neither a live worker nor doubt (`workersFromVerdict`, `internal/steward/census.go:56-82`, every non-RACED-EXIT diagnostic Unprovable at `:63-67`). The scan's hardcoded `identity.KernelProber{}` (`universe.go:95`) becomes injected (R-104).

D2. **Withdrawn (SEAT-04).** The backlog order stays (`openwork.go:111-119`): reordering would route a foreign-lineage claim into legacy revival (`seat_start.go:420-422`; `tick.go:839`; `verdict.go:155-156`). The foreign-claim regression stays a test.

D3. **The ladder's reason is its evidence, on both paths.** When the steward declines to start a seat because a main is counted alive — owned path (`seat_start.go:420-422`) and claimable path (`:459-461`, replacing `Decide(Snapshot{Work: work})`) — the reason names it: "main <mainId> (runtime R, pid P) is alive and holds <goal>; no second seat starts", or "… is alive; N claimable goals wait for it: …". The generic text (`verdict.go:112-114`) is never returned while a main is counted alive. *Evidence source (SEAT-R3-03):* `Workers` holds only counts (`verdict.go:48`) and `workerRecord` lacks main identity and ownership (`universe.go:27`); U2 carries the observed main identities (mainId, runtime, pid) through the worker result and joins goal ownership by recorded lineage (the announcement's `ownerLineage`, `announcement.go:35`; the claim's lineage, `seat_ladder.go:130`). Unproved association: the sentence names the process only, goals listed separately.

D4. **Deferred (SEAT-06).** The epoch comes from the checkout lease whatever the caller's class (`verbs.go:376-383`; `cmd/metasystem/session_stop_test.go:234-235`), so the plain-pane refusal is unexplained; D4 returns when a fixture reproduces it and names the missing field.

D5. **The abnormal-restart hold is scoped to the goal of the launch it reads (finding 365).**

- *Ladder and launch (SEAT-R4-03).* Both the ladder and the final automatic-start check apply the selected goal and approval to abnormal-restart admission. In `decideSeat` the hold is read after `PlanSeat` selects (today before it, `seat_start.go:484-489`); `startSeatWithDependencies` calls `abnormalRestartState` again before launch (`:741`) and passes the same selection. A launch record (`:483-488`) holds only a start of its own goal under its own approval. When the read launch's goal left the seat's hold — the predicate of `seat_start.go:701-702` (`OwnedClaim`, seat lineage, same approval) fails — by a pause, release or done whose history actor (`internal/goal/file.go:479`) is not the seat lineage, after the launch started, the launch counts as closed by the person's act: class "", no hold, and `PlanSeat` decides. A seat's own release keeps today's hold (`TestAutomaticRevivalStopsAfterClaimReleaseWithoutWorkProgress`, `internal/steward/seat_ladder_test.go:667`). The continuation-nonce branch (`revive.go:511-533`), `tick.go:619` and `revive.go:215` keep today's read.
- *Remedy.* `machine revive SEAT` (`runIntentMachineRevive`, `cmd/metasystem/intent_machine.go:65`, into `steward.ReviveSeat`, `seat_start.go:547`) with no bindable failed launch — no records (`:661-662`) or the last launch's goal fails `:701-702` — starts the seat on the held claim `PlanSeat` selects and says so: "launch L on G1 is closed (G1 paused by <actor>); seat starts on held claim G2". The recovery link names no launch (`:753-754` indexes the last record and must not be reached with none). With no held claim: "nothing is held by this seat". The other refusals stay (`:680`; census not free).
- *Replay (R-129).* Today a repeat succeeds only through a matching `RecoveryOf` (`:669-677`, `confirmSeatStart` at `:589`), which the fallback lacks. For revival without a bindable predecessor, a repeat reconciles the previously started launch for the same held goal and approval (the record's `Goal` and `ApprovalOpid`, `:789`) and returns confirmed success before the failed-launch and free-census checks (`:682`, `:685-688`), without creating another launch or treating an unrelated live session as its result (`plans/designs/fleet-provider-and-session-recovery.md:60`).

A terminal job → withdrawn. Untouched: another authenticated announcement in the ancestry; a signed announcement. Identity changed after classification → untouched. Renewal: a `Renew` paused by a deterministic hook after its final revision check (`verbs.go:522`) and before `saveLease` (`:525`), and one arriving after, never write the lease back. Interrupted removal: failure between the removals → no success, announcement kept, `LiveSeatMains > 0`, no revival; the next pass completes. The foreign-claim regression. - U2: a live main holding a goal by recorded lineage → "main … is alive and holds …" with id, runtime, pid; a live main holding nothing beside three claimable goals → "… is alive; 3 claimable goals wait for it: …"; a live main whose lineage is unproved → the process only, goals listed separately; no live main → PlanSeat's sentence. - U3: the ui sequence as a fixture: launch on G1, G1 paused by a person → launch cancelled no-progress, G2 held and ready → next tick ActRevive for G2, `StartSeat` completes and the fake launcher observes G2's launch, reason naming G1's closed launch.
