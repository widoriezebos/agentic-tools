## Decisions

- D1 The lane steward is a role of its own on the landing install. It reads the lane's health facts on every keeper tick, judges them against the noticing table (D3), acts with the lane's verbs under the lane's own authority (the lane acts as the seat, Wido 2026-10-10), records every act as an intervention (what, why, how — the shape of the human steward's log), and asks the person once and plainly only when no verb resolves the situation (D5). It replaces nothing the agent does inside a batch; it watches the lane from outside the batch.
- D2 Health facts, one source (every fact says measured or unmeasured; a missing record is never read as a quiet host, a current premise or a green main; reading health writes nothing — where the status reader persists what it observes, such as an abandoned resolution through `RefreshMerge`, health observes through a read-only path and a test asserts the records unchanged after a read) (`landing status --json` gains a `health` object; `landing health` prints it): per waiting goal its age since hand-in and the reason it waits; batch state and age; the agent's step and idle-since (U8 of lane-housekeeping-2); paused/stopped with reason and since; every open question with its age and its premise: current, gone or unknown — gone only on evidence the question carries (the agent's "main is at commit X" fact with main moved past X; the checkout-not-on-main fact with the checkout on main; a policy request whose recorded effect satisfies it), unknown otherwise; every incident with the recorded attribution and the repeat evidence that exists (a named repeat green, a repeat red, no repeat yet) and the load at the time where a result recorded it; the host load now, and at each proof's start and end once the lane records them (from this design on every result carries its load samples; older results say unmeasured); the lane engine's commit against main; the last three proof durations against the median; the last push time; each goal's time in the lane against the one-hour target (Wido 2026-10-11 08:00: a green batch lands in about an hour; a red goal returns with no human touch).
- D3 The noticing table is the specification. Each row is one observation from the log, the signal in the health facts, the judgement, and the act or the question. The first table is written from the 2026-10-09..12 log (below); the steward appends a row whenever a person intervenes in the lane again (the intervention log is the queue of rules not yet coded).
- D4 Acting. The steward may: prove main (`landing prove --trunk`, D8 of lane-batching repeats its reds first); withdraw its own stale question and re-ask after a fresh proof; start and stop the lane; end a silent agent session through the settle path (`settleLandingAgent`; `landing stop` only pauses admission), and wake the agent; return a goal as the lane with its disagreement recorded; redeploy the lane engine when main moved with lane code (the queued goal landing-deploys-the-engine is this act); restart a young proof for a newcomer (lane-batching D2); hold the host's builds while a proof attributes, or defer the attribution (the capacity guard of S63 as machinery: proofs and builds on one host are metered by one rule); open the fix goal of a load-fragile test the register names twice (a broken machine cannot heal itself, Wido 2026-10-12 08:30: the fix is not left to a person's notice; the goal is built and landed as any goal). Metering other installs' builds is not an act of this design (see Step 1); the steward reports the load. Every act is one record in artifacts/agents/landing/interventions.jsonl and one line in status and in the fleet view. Bounded: one act per situation per hour; never a person's process ended; never a code push without its gate; never a person's verb refused (R-142/143).
- D5 Asking. One question per situation; line one is the situation in plain words, then the verbs that would resolve it; the question is withdrawn by the steward when its premise changes (main moved, incidents cleared, the goal returned) and re-asked only if the situation returns. The person is told what was tried.
- D6 Instructions. The lane steward is Go code on the keeper's tick (no model, no session, no tokens): the table is its checklist, the interventions file its memory, and the hourly report the judgement Wido asked for on 2026-10-03 (quality: reds and their causes; speed: cycle time per goal against the hour; tokens: the agent's and the critics' spend per landing, or unmeasured and which record is missing). The landing agent's skill gains a section about the steward: what it watches, what it does, that a withdrawn question is not re-asked without a fresh proof, that `landing health` is read before any question to a person.
- D7 Fleet. The fleet view's "stalled since" line for the lane becomes the lane steward's line: what it noticed, what it did, what it asks. m1e stops watching the lane by hand the day U6a lands (revision 2: U2 gave nobody the line, so the day moved).

Revision 2 adds D8-D13.

- D8 The reach. Every steward step writes one small record, `artifacts/agents/landing/steward-line.json`: the time, `Health.Line()`, the fired rows (row, subject, minutes), the last report's time and summary, and an identity. The step already builds the line (`steward.go:204`). The identity is a hash of the sorted (row, subject) pairs and the last report's time. Minutes, act text, load and sample time are not in it, so an unchanged situation keeps its identity while its ages grow (`health.go:465`, `:546` put minutes in the act text). Three readers. They read the record or the status the lane already serves, never a second `buildHealth`: (a) `goal next` prints one line, "lane: <line> (<n> min ago)", when the host has a registered lane (`lane.Read(home)`) and a row fires or a report is newer than an hour (`goal.go:467`). (b) The browser's fleet page. Its lane block renders the board payload, which is `landing status --json`'s data (`internal/ui/httpd/board.go:71-74, 143-152`; `FleetPane.tsx:286`, `LaneBlock` in `LandingLane.tsx:52`), not the text lines of `internal/ui/fleet/lines.go` (those are the Partner's words). So `Health` gains `line` (json), set where the status assembles health (`status.go:246-256`), and `LaneBlock` renders it as its first line. (c) The post-clear boot reads the narrator digest (`cmd/metasystem/brain_boot.go:505-511`, `narratordigest.PendingWithLayoutReader`). The seat steward's health narration writes a different file, `artifacts/agents/narration.log` (`internal/steward/narrate.go:29`), so it is not the reader. The lane line goes into the digest the way the lane's design warnings already do: the seat steward that owns the lane appends one digest entry (`carryLaneDesignChecks`, `internal/steward/lane_silent.go:61-79`, through `narratordigest.Append`), with the identity as its source id. The digest keeps an exact retry once (`lane_silent.go:59-60`), so an unchanged identity adds nothing. Messages go out only on a change of identity (Wido 2026-10-25). An unreadable or missing record is said ("lane line unreadable: …"), never read as a quiet lane (D2).
- D9 The wait ends on the lane's word. `work wait G --for landing` also returns when, after the wait's registration boundary, the lane records for G: a return (the queue's newest entry for G is `returned`), a stop (an open stop whose subject names G, or the lane while G waits), or a question (an open lane question about G, or about the lane while G waits). It names the state word: landed, returned, stopped or question. Landed keeps exit 0 and the outcome "landing" for today's readers; the other three exit 1 (`ExitRed`, `internal/run/waiter.go:35`). The summary carries the word and the reason the record gives. The boundary. The observe function gets the selector, the target and the ledger tip only (`internal/run/waiter.go:219`, `:680-690`), not the registration time. So `WaitSelector` (`waiter.go:60-72`) gains `LaneAfter` (RFC 3339). The store sets it once, from `registeredAt`, when it registers a landing wait without one (`waiter.go:1025-1030`). It is persisted in the row's selector, so a resumed or renewed wait keeps it: a renewal resets `RegisteredAt` (`waiter.go:1496`) but keeps the selector. The lane is read in the verb's observe closure (`cmd/metasystem/wait_verb.go:290-316`), after `goal.ObserveLedgerForWait` (`wait_verb.go:301`) says pending, against `selected.LaneAfter`. `internal/goal` does not import the lane. A return at or before the boundary does not end the wait.
- D10 A loop is one situation. A `lane-return` stop with subject lane that records a fingerprint (`measure.now`) is not closed while the keeper's current barren fingerprint is the same. A changed barren count alone is not a changed premise (`state/lifecycle.go:29-31` reads the fingerprint; `stop.go:61-63` supplies it). A stop without a recorded fingerprint keeps today's rule, and a barren count back at 0 (lane progress, `stop.go:318-319`) still closes it. So its question stays open, it is not withdrawn, and nothing is re-asked until the fingerprint changes or a person acts. The fired row says how many barren runs the stop has seen. The count goes in the row and the report, not in the question text (wording: D13).
- D11 An act that fails the same way twice is wrong, not early. The bound reads row and outcome, not only row and subject (`steward.go:128-133`): when the last two acts of a row failed with the same outcome, the steward stops acting on that row, records one defect intervention (row `steward-act-failed`, with the outcome text), and asks once. It acts on the row again when the outcome changes or a person acts. The upstream cause is fixed too: `stale-question` fires only for questions its act can withdraw (`health.go:488-491` against `question.go:23-26`); lane-stop questions are the lifecycle's (`lifecycle.go:78`). This is Wido's unit stop rule applied to the steward.
- D12 The six rows of 2026-10-25 (the table's second edition, below) are coded in U6a (the loop row) and U6b (the other five). Each reads a recorded fact named in its row. Rows that ask a person ask once per situation (D5) and say what was tried.
- D13 What stays with the-answer-is-the-act. The words of every lane question and delivery (its section 1.3, and designs/the-answer-is-the-act-input-20261025-lane-questions.md item 2) are that goal's. This page decides when the lane asks, how often, and when a question closes (that input's item 1 is D10 here). U6's questions use today's lane ask path. They take the 1.3 contract when the-answer-is-the-act migrates the lane's producers; U6 does not wait for it.

## Threat model

(1) The steward acts wrongly under load: one act per situation per hour, every act logged and visible, no act ends a person's process or pushes code without its gate. (2) Question churn: one question per situation, premise-based withdrawal. (3) HAL9000: the steward never refuses or reverses a person's act; it records disagreement (R-142/143). (4) The steward and the agent act at once: the steward acts only outside the agent's turn (agent idle) or on facts the agent cannot see (host load, engine version, question premises). (5) The table rots: every human intervention appends a row; the hourly report names rows that fired and rows that never did.

Revision 2. (2) failed on 10-25: the lifecycle treated a new barren attempt as a changed premise, so "one question per situation" held per stop, not per situation. D10 makes the fingerprint the situation. (1) failed too: "one act per hour" keyed on the subject, and every loop question was a new subject. D11 keys on the outcome. (6) The steward's own line can become noise: D8's readers print only on a change, and the line is one line.

## Rare cases (no mechanism)

Each of these gets the generic fail-safe already on the page: the row reports, the bound holds, a person decides (Wido 2026-10-22: not a nuclear reactor).

- A fingerprint that changes on every tick while nothing lands: not `lane-loop`; `cannot-heal` catches it at 120 minutes.
- A person acts twice within 5 minutes: one `person-act-no-effect` record per recorded act; the hourly bound holds.
- A build that never ends: the proof waits at most 30 minutes, then starts with its load recorded.
- A wait registered after the return already happened: it does not end on the old return; `goal next` shows the lane line.
- Two lanes on one host: not in this design.
- `steward-line.json` written while a reader reads it: the write is atomic (`atomicfile`); a torn read is "unreadable", said, and read again on the next tick.
- The seat steward is not running: `goal next` and the fleet page still read the lane directly; only the digest line waits for it.
- A seat steward held by a stranded handoff continuation (ui, from 13:28Z, continuation 2c39c074c128f246, consumed, its launch never stamped, of a done goal; `machine revive` does not clear it): not the lane's. Its removing unit is a-persons-terminal-is-not-a-seat-main D5/U3. U6 only shows it where a lane row already fires; no new row.
