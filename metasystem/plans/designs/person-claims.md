## What it delivers

1. A proved person reserves goals without a running session. The next ordinary matching session adopts every visible reservation without replacing ownership, approval or accounting. Neither reservation nor adoption launches work or spends its budget.
2. An agent's claim prepares its actual session once when necessary, then reclassifies the original caller and makes a fresh claim decision.

**Step 1 is U1: reservations and adoption together.** It includes their storage, work admission, steward eligibility and public remedies, so no intermediate unit publishes reservations its readers cannot interpret. U2 builds on U1's adoption callback and remains independently deferrable.

Use the area owner from accepted `lane-policies-and-helm` U5. That design specifies an actor-free predicate; the implementation already passes `Actor` for same-owner arc exemptions. This goal retains that parameter and adds verified person advice, rather than adding another actor argument. Consume the accepted `ledger-reads-are-fresh` design's `FreshProjection`, pinned observation and typed adoption-pending result when available; U1 can extend today's offline callback first. U2 requires that fresh reader for its post-preparation decision. Explicit commands then use fresh reads; the session-start hook stays offline. This dependency does not move transport or hook deadlines here.

Prove/classify the original caller before named lookup, frontier selection, held-work answers or seat fallback. Personal reservation requires a named goal and direct enrolled-terminal proof, with `Proof.Helm == nil`; neither `--by`, a grant nor stored provenance proves a person. Carry the proof to the claim transaction and check it before each advisory override, including recovery. An unnamed personal claim presents available choices and asks for a goal name; it cannot guess a person's choice. A named personal claim never takes the automatic seat-fallback path. Unknown freshness or advice cannot veto it: follow the accepted fresh-read design's direct safe-publication path.

Extend `ClaimRecord` with optional `By`, rendered as `by=human:NAME`. The ordinary claim and its history are the only reservation record. A person can reserve live unowned queued or approved work without approval or budget. Keep any existing approval, budget and their provenance byte-for-byte; leave absent values absent. Pin, unfinished-dependency sequencing, quota and established area overlap become recorded warnings for the proved person. Do not remove dependency edges, alter a pin or manufacture an approval. Explicit parks keep their existing separate resume act; the arc cascade continues skipping parked members rather than silently resuming them. Terminal or foreign-owned work is never silently reopened or stolen. Taking another pair's claim uses existing `goal claim G --take-over --reason TEXT`, preserving takeover's target scope and fence checks.

Claim, ClaimArc and takeover use the same proof, lineage, reservation binding and warning policy for each claim they create. Arc selection remains explicit, atomic on a foreign claim, and confined to that arc; queued members become reservable by a person. Automatic claims keep their approval, quota and sequencing gates. A claim accepts no budget tuple: approval and budget remain separate public acts.

## Decision 4: prepare the agent once, then decide freshly (U2)

**Sites.** `cmd/metasystem/intent_process.go:665–697` owns session start; `cmd/metasystem/intent_planning.go:1084`, `:1246`, `:1284` own public claim and its direct acquisition helper. `internal/up/up.go:713–851` already owns enrollment, announcement, real holder classification, adoption and supervision.

Extract command-layer `prepareClaimSession`, shared by session start and agent claim, returning the typed up result instead of rendering it internally. Preserve up as lifecycle owner. Pass the original invocation's caller identity and actual owning lineage in process; no subprocess that becomes its own caller, no fabricated runtime ancestor and no recursive claim. A person goes straight to U1. An authenticated live owning agent reuses its session; otherwise prepare once, preserving system stop, pause, drain and helm. An advisor result never confers writing authority or displaces a foreign live holder.

After preparation reclassify the original caller, reread holder and epoch, and call `FreshProjection` before any candidate, fallback, quota or held-work decision. Reuse preparation's adoption snapshot only for its reported adoption outcomes; it cannot be the post-preparation claim decision. Each required decision gets the freshness owner's one bounded attempt, never an added retry loop. Publication recaptures the current tip and ownership normally. Preserve per-claim pending results even if this later fresh read or publication fails; acquired session authority and successful adoptions remain facts, not a fully successful claim.

A foreign writer names the existing `metasystem session isolate` remedy, followed by claim in the resulting real writer session. An engine difference uses the existing build/start remedy; engine comparison belongs to the accepted parent's engine-input decision and its recorded follow-up, not this goal. Unreadable identity/engine/ledger or failed preparation publishes no automatic claim. Report the underlying cause and exact clearing act, then permit the same claim to reuse preparation on retry. No automatic work runs while transport is pending because selection depends on its result.
