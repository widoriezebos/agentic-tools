## Wido's binding words

- The remedy a refusal prints must succeed when followed, and following it must not undo the gate it names. "A refusal a human can hit states exactly what to change. A fix that grows the human's surface is wrong" (R-135-m1e). "Messages a Person Reads": line 1 the plain reason, line 2 the one command with every known value filled in, or an explicit "nothing to do".
- A person's act is never refused except to prevent damage, and then the refusal names the runnable public command that achieves the intent the right way (rule H1, `internal/refusal/h1.go:3-10`; R-135-m1e; "humans are never denied a verb", 2026-09-27).
- An agent never gets a person's power. A general power of attorney admits one seat's main session as the person, is granted only by the person at their own terminal, and is checked again at every act (`internal/goal/general_attorney.go:29-35`; `cmd/metasystem/attorney_admits.go:26-36`). A routed ask, an alert or a copied command carries no authority.
- NO HAL 9000: a person's decision always takes effect; the machinery may warn, explain the consequence in plain English and record it, then it complies (R-142-m1e, R-143-m1e; R-75-m3 "never refuses a verified human in a critical situation").
- "Every refusal that needs a person names the act, the pane and the person; the escape hatch routes to the helm holder of the fleet first (the coordinator seat under its grant) and to the person only when the act is outside the grant. A refusal class that repeats N times in an hour across seats becomes a machinery finding on its own" (finding 11). "A remedy is a verb whose effect is proven. The health tests run the declared act in the bed and assert the role clears. A remedy that does not clear in the bed does not ship" (finding 9).
- The quiet channel: a message to the person only for a landing on main or when a response is needed (2026-10-02). "We're not building a nuclear reactor": the smallest thing that works. At most five units, each at most 250 production lines; Scrooge on tests: each unit its new and changed tests and their mutations, the full suite once when the goal lands. No caps (2026-09-20, reaffirmed 2026-10-06).
- The design stop is an automated rule, not a question to him (2026-10-06 22:40 CEST): the plan's design row stops the loop when a class already fixed recurs in the same section; the clean sections are accepted and the sections still carrying material split into a follow-up with its own design loop, queued next in the plan.

## What it delivers

Every health remedy is an act that can succeed or an honest "nothing to do", owned in one table and proven in the bed. A person's `alert clear` re-arms ended healing, every time it is run, and a person's clear, enrolled or not, acknowledges a ledger move. The coordinator-first routing of questions, the reply as the answer and the `refusal` kind of the register are the follow-up brief's (decision 14).

**Step 1 is U1**, the remedy table: on its own it corrects what a person reads today for the three acts and the process roles (`incident list`, `session start`, the budget placeholder) and gives the bed its first clearing proof. U2 adds the person's re-arm and acknowledgment. U3 brings every remaining check site onto the table and deletes the old switch. Each unit has a consumer the day it lands.

The threat model is mistakes and stale observations by trusted participants on one computer: a remedy written from memory, a breaker nobody re-arms, two processes taking two locks in two orders, a person at a terminal the metasystem never enrolled. Nobody attacks. No new transport, store, grant type or authority proof is built; no verb or flag is added.

## Failure, cutover and deferred homes

A remedy renders at observation time and fails to nothing worse than the reason line. A clear that cannot write the health or attention state reports the path and leaves the episode open; a clear whose person proof is refused still clears and re-arms, and says who acknowledges and where. Nothing here waits: every step is one bounded read or write inside an existing tick or command, under the tick's lock order. Until U3 lands, a role the table does not own yet prints what it prints today; U1's static rule covers the table, U3's covers every role.

Deferred homes: the routing, the return and the refusal alert, with the generic act matcher, the `remedy.<role>` declaration, refusals nobody asked about and the hand-run landing verbs' routing (the follow-up brief); the runner's burn (housekeeping); answering from the UI and free-form messages (blocked-agent, step 2); the driver's consumption of a stop's `ask` handoff (runs-advance); the lane's own policy questions (lane-reads-its-policies).

## Questions for Wido

Only answers that would change what is built:

1. **Ledger attention's clearing act.** (a) Acknowledgment: a person's `alert clear` of the health alert accepts the current tip as examined (chosen; after round 1 the acknowledgment needs the person proof, after round 2 the proof admits an unenrolled terminal).
