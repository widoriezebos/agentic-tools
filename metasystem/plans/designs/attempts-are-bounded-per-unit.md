## Step 1

After U1 alone: a goal with any number of units never closes its admission for its attempt count. Each unit has its own count of attempts (its rounds, retries and corrections), and the box's attempts member bounds that count; a unit that reaches it is refused alone, in plain words, with the one command that gives it more, while the goal's other units and its work outside units go on. Work outside units (design reviews, test runs, delegates, governed runs) is counted together as one more unit of the goal under the same number. No admission reader compares the goal's total any more: not the unit launch, not dispatch, not test-run headroom, not the automatic extension, not the governed run's exhaustion, not the corrupt-over-limit stop. A person's budget act on a running goal keeps what the goal has spent; only an approval starts the counts afresh. After U2: goal show, goal budget, the steward's seat box, the fleet's terminal lines and the health lines show the goal's total as spend beside the busiest unit's count against its limit, and the channel's proposed box says attempts per unit. The fleet page in the browser keeps today's words over the seat record's two numbers, so it shows the busiest unit's count against the limit ("attempt 4 of 6 · 2 attempts left") and not the total; its per-unit words and the total come later (section 7). The two setup-refusal texts and the tier-1 description are fixed.

The next command is complete and typed for the person: `metasystem goal budget a-program-goal 5d/8/2400m/1/20` ("gives every unit of this goal two more attempts; this unit goes on at its next launch"). A person at their terminal may raise attempts past the tier's number; an act under the steward's grant may not (it stays within the tier box), so a unit's bound is a person's call.
- Work outside units (design reviews, test runs, delegates) is counted as one more unit. When it reaches the number, those launches are refused with today's texts, and the box's words say "attempts per unit; work outside units counts as one unit".
- A person's budget act keeps what the goal has spent, as Wido ruled: the attempt, job-minute and critique counts go on from where they stood, as the elapsed clock already does, so the raise above gives U5 exactly two more attempts (6 of 8). Only an approval starts the counts afresh. So `metasystem goal budget a-program-goal norm` on a goal that has spent its tier box gives it nothing more, and the steward's grant, which stays within the tier box, cannot extend a spent goal: more is a person's act that names the new total. ## 2. Risks

- **A runaway that splits instead of looping.** Each new unit name is a new run with a fresh count. That is the remedy the counted-rounds cap already offers ("split the unit", `metasystem/internal/launch/coded.go:63-69`), each split is an agent's visible act (a new work name on the goal), and the goal's job minutes and elapsed time still bound the total. Accepted: the box bounds spend, and a split spends visibly.
- **Work outside units that grows with the goal's size.** If a per-unit activity charged a reservation outside its unit, the outside count would measure goal size again. Read today: a unit's build, proof and read all reserve under its run (`metasystem/cmd/metasystem/intent_work.go:732-769`, every step kind through `ReserveUnitLaunch`); design reviews are per design page, landing proofs per goal. T1 pins the unit steps; the busiest-unit line names the outside count when it is the highest, so a growth is seen; section 7 names the remedy.
- **Approved boxes loosen.** A box a person approved at 12 attempts per goal now allows 12 per unit (D7). The goal's attempt count then has no bound of its own; its spend has (minutes, elapsed), and inside a unit the correction budget (2) and the counted-rounds cap (6) bind before 12.
- **A spent goal is no longer refreshed by a budget act** (D8). `norm`, `keep` and an act under the steward's grant gave a spent goal a fresh box; after U1 they do not, and a goal whose spend reached its box waits for a person to name a bigger one. Accepted: it is Wido's ruling (a budget is a ceiling over the goal's whole life), and for a unit D6's refusal types the raise for the person. A goal stopped as over its limit (a launch that bypassed admission, `metasystem/internal/dispatch/stop.go:118-126`) is freed only by a box above what was spent; one more day frees only a stop for time.
- **Mixed engine versions read the seat record.** D5 keeps `attempts` and `attemptLimit` a count against the limit it is bounded by, so an older reader still prints a true sentence.
- **Records written before the change.** Every unit reservation already carries its run (`unitRun`, `metasystem/internal/dispatch/unit_launch.go:80-82`); nothing is migrated. A unit's name appears in the readers from its first reservation after U1 (older reservations have none).
- Scope: two units, about 100 and 60 production lines (section 5).

- **Admission.** `budgetAdmissionBreaches` refuses at `Attempts >= AttemptLimit` (`metasystem/internal/dispatch/admission.go:518-519`) for its five callers: the lineage seam `EvaluateGoalAdmission` (`:156`), revision admission for dispatch (`:336`), candidate consumption for a cross-goal proof (`:394`), governed admission (`metasystem/internal/dispatch/governed.go:207`) and the unit launch (`metasystem/internal/dispatch/unit_launch.go:66`). ## 4. Decisions

- **D1 A unit is its run; work outside units is one more unit.** A reservation that carries `unitRun` belongs to that run: the run the unit runner keeps for one worktree, goal and unit name (`metasystem/internal/launch/unit_named.go:19-50`), the same identity the counted-rounds cap and `MaxRounds` already bound. Every other reservation the projection counts (a job record without `unitRun`: critic roots and follow-ups, delegates, implementers; a proof attempt; a live or terminal governed run) belongs to the goal's one "outside units" count. The reservation also records the unit's name (`"unit"` beside `unitRun`, written by `ReserveUnitLaunch`, which gains the name as a parameter from `metasystem/cmd/metasystem/intent_work.go:769`), for the readers only; the count keys on the run. Rejected: keying on the unit name (old reservations have none, so a unit in flight at the change would split into two counts; the runner bounds runs, not names); one count per critic chain, obligation and proof outside units (a chain identity threaded through three admission entry points for a case that does not occur today; section 7 keeps it for when it does).
- **D2 The attempts member bounds each unit; the goal's total is spend, never a bound.** The projection keeps `Attempts` exactly as today (the total: every test that pins it stays) and adds a count per unit, keyed by run, with `""` for outside units, incremented at the same four places the total is (`metasystem/internal/dispatch/budget.go:580`, `:753`, `:856`, `:893`), plus the unit names it saw. Two exported readers serve the rest: `AttemptsIn(unit)` and `BusiestUnit()` (the highest count, its run, its name, or outside units). The corrupt-over-limit breach compares the busiest unit's count, not the total (`metasystem/internal/dispatch/budget.go:1139`, `:1158`), so the generic fail-safe against a launch that bypassed admission stays (`metasystem/internal/dispatch/stop_test.go:563-600` keeps its meaning: its three jobs are outside units) and an eight-unit goal is never stopped as corrupt. Rejected: dropping the member, alternative (b): the correction budget and the counted-rounds cap come from settings, `MaxRounds` counts rounds (not retries) and is frozen at the run's start, and work outside units has no other count bound, so the member is the only count a person sees and raises for both; section 7 says what would show it is not needed. Rejected: deriving the goal's limit from its Units table, alternative (c): the table is often absent and goals split while running. Rejected: dropping the attempts over-limit breach (it is the one fail-safe for a path that launched past admission). Rejected: raising the number (the stopgap; it moves the wall).
- **D3 Every admission reader reads the count of the unit it admits into.** `budgetAdmissionBreaches` takes the count to compare as a parameter; its callers pass: the unit launch, the unit's count less one when it joins an already counted round (today's adjustment, `metasystem/internal/dispatch/unit_launch.go:63-65`, applied to the unit's count); the lineage seam (`metasystem/internal/dispatch/admission.go:156`), revision admission (`:336`), candidate consumption (`:394`) and governed admission (`metasystem/internal/dispatch/governed.go:207`), the outside count. The extension's admit-this-proposal checks (`metasystem/internal/dispatch/admission.go:370`, `:418`) compare the outside count, the only one on those paths; the offer and its raise are unchanged (it raises the per-unit number once and, as today, keeps the counts: it changes the box and never the approval, `metasystem/internal/goal/verbs.go:1819-1825`), and unit launches still get no automatic extension, as today. Test-run headroom (`metasystem/cmd/metasystem/proof_run.go:1183`) reads the outside count. Readers whose code does not change: the cold-build refusal (`metasystem/cmd/metasystem/test_cold_budget.go:105-116`; an `attemptLimit` breach there now means the outside count is full, still a person's call), the validation-weight reset (`metasystem/internal/gaterun/weight.go:394`, reads only status and epoch), the design chain (`metasystem/internal/dispatch/design_chain.go:49-54`, the setup-refusal rule only), the lane (`metasystem/internal/dispatch/budget.go:1041-1053`, elapsed only), the live stop (`metasystem/internal/dispatch/stop.go:118-126`, fed by D2's breach). The critic review-round check (`metasystem/internal/dispatch/admission.go:337-349`) is unchanged.
- **D4 A governed run's ordinal stays an identity; its exhaustion reads the outside count.** `AttemptOrdinal` stays the total plus one (`metasystem/internal/dispatch/governed.go:225`): obligation state refuses a repeated ordinal within an epoch (`metasystem/internal/obligationstate/state.go:130-146`) and proof reservation owners match on it (`metasystem/internal/dispatch/budget.go:734`, `metasystem/cmd/metasystem/proof_run.go:1122`), so renumbering would make a state file written across the change invalid. `SpendSnapshot` gains `Attempts`, the outside count without this run, from the conclusion's re-projection (`metasystem/internal/dispatch/governed.go:110-115`, which already excludes the run), and the exhaustion test becomes `snapshot.Attempts+1 >= limit` (`metasystem/internal/run/conclude.go:375`). Rejected: the ordinal from the outside count (repeated ordinals across the change).
- **D5 The readers show the total as spend and the busiest unit against the limit.** goal show (`metasystem/cmd/metasystem/goal_show_view.go:195`): "attempts: 13 in all" and "per unit: at most 4 of 6 (U3)", or "(work outside units)", or no name for a unit whose reservations predate U1. goal budget (`metasystem/cmd/metasystem/intent_goals.go:306`): "attempts 13 (at most 4 of 6 in one unit)". - **D6 A unit's refusal says what happened, keeps the rest going, and gives the one command.** When the unit's attempts are its only breach, `ReserveUnitLaunch` refuses with code `BUDGET_REFUSED` (kept: misclassification evidence and the lane read it, `metasystem/internal/dispatch/admission.go:16`), facts `unit=U5 goal=G` plus today's breach detail, and the reason "Unit U5 of goal G has used the N attempts one unit may have (its rounds, retries and corrections), so nothing was started. The next command travels as arguments on the error, as the counted-rounds refusal's does (`UnitRoundCapError`, `metasystem/internal/launch/coded.go:36-41`): `metasystem goal budget G` followed by the current box with attempts raised by two (two: one more round and one retry or correction), with the reason "gives every unit of this goal two more attempts; this unit goes on at its next launch". Because a budget act keeps the counts (D8), the command grants exactly what it names: the unit's count stays at N and its limit becomes N+2; every other unit and the outside count gain the same two, and nothing is restarted. The unit runner's renderer (`metasystem/cmd/metasystem/intent_work.go:1591-1594`) and the publication gate (`metasystem/cmd/metasystem/intent_unit_review.go:435-437`) use that command when the error carries one, and today's placeholder otherwise. The raise takes effect at the unit's next launch: the count is read at every launch, unlike `MaxRounds` and the counted cap, which are frozen at the run's start. Approved boxes keep their numbers and are not rewritten: the program goals approved at `5d/12/2400m/1/20` and `5d/24/2400m/1/20` (the stopgap) now allow 12 or 24 per unit, looser than needed and harmless. New program goals are approved with the tier's attempts number (6 for tier 2), so attempts are no longer a budget exception; elapsed and minutes stay what the plan needs. The stopgap of decision 102(c) is no longer needed once U1 lands. Whether to lower the approved program boxes is a person's budget act: open question 9.1. Rejected: a migration that rewrites approved boxes (the machine would change a person's approval).
