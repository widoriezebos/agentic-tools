# Design: the keeper's trunk clock prefers waiting work, and the headline follows the agent

- Id: 01M4Y5CZSK36VY1S36GT0FNM2N
- Kind: design
- Status: accepted
- Goals: lane-clock-prefers-work
- Evidence: lane test findings 303, 313, 317 (2026-10-14); Astra positions in scratchpad/codex-discuss-plan2310-1.log (Q1).

## Problem

1. `fullProofClock` (`internal/landing/plain/wake.go:123–145`) starts a full trunk proof of main every `proof.trunk-every` (4h), counted from any trunk result (red included) or a qualifying full-proof push. On 2026-10-14 it started at 11:14 (red on a flake), 16:58 and 22:46, each 45–60 minutes with the host's one heavy slot. At 17:04 a goal was handed in while the 16:58 clock proof ran; the lane finished that proof (17:39 by the retained result), then sealed the batch (17:51) and proved the candidate on the same base again (17:52→18:44). Hand-in to landed: 1h40, of which 41 min proved a base the candidate proof covered minutes later. The scoped proof debt (`wake.go:153–181`) is separate and unchanged here.
2. `internal/landing/state/describe.go:203–208` overrides the agent's headline outside proof and resolution states, so after the person's `landing resume` at 13:29 the headline and to-do kept "main has unstable checks …; run: metasystem landing resume" while the agent prepared and proved the batch (finding 313). This override is transferred to this goal from one-person-one-truth (its D4 mentioned the describer's words); one owner.

## Decisions

D1. The decision owner is the keeper's run, not the clock computation. `fullProofClock` (`wake.go:123–145`) only computes when the cadence is due; starting work is `AgentKeeper.Run` with `SelectBatch`'s `PolicyDecision` (`batch.go:453–474`, `lane/agent.go:232–247`). When the cadence is due, the keeper first attempts batch preparation on that wake. If a batch is prepared and admitted to its candidate proof (sealed), the candidate proof is marked full-depth and no standalone trunk proof starts on that wake. Gates, candidate proofs and trunk proofs share one proof owner (`running.json`; `Start` refuses a trunk proof while a gate is alive, `prove.go:493–502`; `ProofHold` holds the agent, `wake.go:186–191`), so the fallback is serialized, never concurrent: while the batch's merge gate or candidate proof runs, nothing else starts; the candidate continues first (gate → candidate proof → the agent's push opportunity, D1a). The trunk proof starts only when no gate or proof runs and the batch attempt ended without a sealed candidate (selection held because it needs a person, admission closed, no eligible goal, the gate failed) and the cadence is still undischarged. One batch attempt per due cadence: a waiting hand-in never defers the clock past that attempt (Astra round 1 LC-1, round 2 LC-5).
D1a. Credit. The cadence is discharged only by qualifying evidence under the existing rule: a trunk result (green or red), or a qualifying push of the candidate (`pushes.jsonl` joined to a green, full-scope result, `wake.go:130–145`). A candidate that fails its proof does not discharge it. A failed candidate with an attributable defect is retained as a checkpoint in `running.json` (`prove.go:1052`) so that its repair round can be admitted; a trunk proof start overwrites `running.json` (`prove.go:583`) and would destroy that repair identity. Rule: the fallback never overwrites a checkpoint; while a failed candidate's repair obligation is open (checkpoint retained, repair admissible or in progress) no trunk proof starts; the fallback starts at the next proof-free wake only after that obligation is settled (the repair admitted and its proof run, or the candidate returned or abandoned and the checkpoint cleared). A failed candidate with no repair obligation (returned at once) starts the fallback at the next proof-free wake (Astra round 3, LC-8). "Sealed" means admitted to its candidate proof; a refused admission (`proof_admission.go:227` seals before refusing) routes to the fallback as closed admission (LC-9). A candidate that finished green is "awaiting its push": the proof completion appends the result without pushing (`prove.go:1036–1058`) and the agent pushes on its next wake (the retained records show 18:43:34 green, 18:45:27 pushed); the clock gives that candidate one agent wake to push before any fallback, so the successful path never triggers a trunk proof; only when the push does not happen on that wake (agent held, push refused, candidate abandoned) does the next wake start the trunk proof. Credit stays push-only. The clock predicate and its assertions say so (Astra round 1 LC-2, round 2 LC-6).
D2. A running trunk proof never counts for a later candidate, and nothing is cancelled. `ProofHold` (`wake.go:186–191`, wired at `landing_agent.go:343–349`) keeps the agent from launching while a proof runs; the keeper may select work, the agent assembles after the proof, and the clock is credited by the trunk result only (Astra round 1, LC-4: the agent does not assemble concurrently).
D3. The headline distinguishes an outstanding action from retained evidence. `shadow.go:76–90` calls a live agent idle between steps and `shadow.go:219–242` repopulates `Main.Flakes` from historical proof results, so an idle-only rule would resurrect "run … resume" between steps after the person resumed (Astra round 1, LC-3). Rule: the to-do "run landing resume" (and any person ask) is shown only while that action is outstanding in the lane's recorded state; once the act is taken it is gone, between steps too; retained flake evidence is shown as information ("flaky test on record: …; its fix …") never as the headline or the to-do; while the agent runs a step the headline is that step. Tests: unstable-check headline → resume → active step → between steps → next step: no ask returns.
D4. UX: the keeper says why it skipped or started a clock proof in one line in `landing status --verbose` ("clock due 22:46; batch 20261014T2250 sealed, its full proof counts" / "clock due; no batch within one wake; proving main"); no internal state words.

## Units
- U1 (D1, D1a, D2): the cadence-due decision in `AgentKeeper.Run` (prepare first; held or gate running → trunk proof), the full-depth mark on the sealed batch, the credit predicate (trunk result or qualifying push only); tests through the keeper: cadence due + preparable batch → sealed, no trunk proof while gate/proof run, credited only when pushed; green candidate → one agent wake to push, no trunk proof in between (proof → wake → push sequence); push not taken on that wake → trunk proof at the next proof-free wake; candidate red with a repair obligation → no trunk proof while the checkpoint stands, repair admission still succeeds after the cadence is due, fallback only after the obligation is settled (test: candidate red → trunk fallback withheld → candidate repair admitted → repair proof → then fallback if still due); candidate red without repair obligation → trunk proof at the next proof-free wake; selection needs a person → trunk proof when no proof runs; gate running → wait for it, then the candidate (never a trunk proof while a gate runs); trunk proof running + hand-in → batch after, credited by the trunk result only. ≤200 lines.
- U2 (D3, D4): outstanding action vs retained evidence in the describer (`describe.go:203–208`, `shadow.go`); the to-do gone with the act, between steps too; the one-line keeper reason; tests on the describer with the 13:29 sequence through to between-steps. ≤120 lines.

## Not in scope
The scoped proof debt, batching policy, the lane's recovery of an unclaimed reservation (goal runs-advance-on-their-own), the person resolver.
