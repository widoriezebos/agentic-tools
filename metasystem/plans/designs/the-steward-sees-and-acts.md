### G2a the-steward-decides (decision 94)

**D2 The grant names classes, and a decision in his name is a record bound to its act (U2a, U2b1, U2b2).** Extend the authority owner:
- A general grant may name classes: `grant add --acts everything --classes mechanical,recovery,ordering,acks,stale-releases --for 7d --lineage seat`. `PowerOfAttorneyEntry` gains `Classes` (`root.go:48-65`); `WithinBounds` (`:97`) refuses an unknown class. No classes means today's general grant, unchanged. The rollout's classes are decision 40's: mechanical and recovery acts, ledger ordering, acknowledgements, releases of stale claims and fences. Value-bearing design questions, stop-line raises and budgets beyond the tier box are outside every class in this build; widening is Wido's own act with the grant verb, and the verb stays a person's act (`intent_grant_everything.go:93`).
- `--lineage seat` binds the grant to `launch.SeatOwnerLineage` (`internal/launch/settings.go:52`) instead of the holder's lineage at the grant (`general_attorney.go:14-17`), so a steward-started successor on that checkout acts under it. The admission's lineage test (`attorney_admits.go:151`) is unchanged.
- One table beside `attorneyActor` (`intent_grant_everything.go:280`) gives each attorney-admissible act a class by verb and subject: `goal approve` of a goal the lane opened as a flake fix is recovery; any other `goal approve` is outside; `goal prioritize`, `pin`, `group` are ordering; `alert ack`, `question withdraw` acks; `work stop` recovery; `goal release` of a stale claim stale-releases. `landing resume` and `machine revive` are in no class in this delivery: they want the person's own enrolled-terminal proof by design (`lane_person.go:75`; `intent_machine.go:88`, `intent_work.go:3013`, `seat_start.go:550`) and stay a person's direct acts (section 7). The class table admits only verbs whose own checks accept the grant; a test runs each table row's real verb under its class. A grant with classes admits only its classes; anything else falls to the agent's own authority, which refuses a person's act as today. A person at the enrolled terminal is never affected.
- **The decision record is bound to the act** (U2b1). `metasystem decision record --class C --text TEXT --rejected TEXT --undo TEXT [--context TEXT] -- VERB ARGS…` runs the act as a child verb with `--json`, under the same caller, and appends one line to the host decision log (`artifacts/agents/authority/decisions.jsonl` under the board home, beside the authority log, so both seats read it): id, time, actor, machine, grant, class, decision, rejected alternative, context, undo, the act's argv, its outcome and its effect. The outcome is the child's own result outcome: confirmed, partial (the act landed and a follow-up did not, `intent.go:1322-1336`) or refused, with the verb's words. The effect is what the existing owner already publishes and nothing invented: for a ledger verb the `owner` publication, outcome, tip (the ledger commit), detail and code (`intent_goals.go:163-167`, `goalsync_owner_report.go:78-83`); for any other verb the result's targets (kind and id, as `intent_operations.go:647` renders them). Authorization is checked before the effect only: an agent with no live grant of that class is refused before the child runs, and the child's own admission and the ledger's re-check (`general_attorney.go:157`) guard the effect. Once the child has run, the line is always appended, with its grant, outcome and rejected alternative, even if the grant ended meanwhile (R-142-m1e: every such decision is recorded). A decision that is not an act (a disposition, below) has no child. A person may record any decision.
- **The overrule shows its impact first** (U2b2, R-143-m1e). `decision record --overrules ID` (a person's act) prints one screen before anything is written: what the decision did (its act and effect), that overruling records his decision and does not undo the act, and the undo the decision recorded; then it records the overrule with the impact text it showed. `decision list` and `decision show` show the log beside the `docs/decisions` pages.
- **The disposition contract** (U2b2). One small type, `Disposition{Row, Subject, Occurrence, Kind (act|wait|ask), Trigger, WaitUntil, Decision}`, in one package read by the writer, the situation and the lane: `decision record --row ROW --subject S --occurrence O --dispose wait --trigger TEXT --until TIME --text … --rejected …`, or `--dispose act -- VERB …`, or `--dispose ask`. A wait needs a trigger and a time at most 24 h ahead; an act needs its child; an ask needs neither. Handled follows the child's actual outcome: an act disposition is handled only when its child is confirmed, or partial (the effect landed; the row then shows the follow-up's recovery and stays a first thing until it clears); a refused or failed child leaves the occurrence unhandled. The same rule decides progress (D1).
- Revalidation of a decision whose `--context` changed: the skill says to re-read it; no mechanism.

- W2: a live grant `--classes ordering --lineage seat`; a seat-lineage main runs `decision record --class ordering … -- goal prioritize G 3` through the production child: the log line carries the child's own result, outcome confirmed and the `owner` publication's tip and detail as the verb printed them. A successor main of the same lineage is admitted too. A wait disposition is written in the contract's shape and read back by the situation.
- S2: the same main runs `decision record --class values …` or `-- goal budget G …`: refused before the child runs, the class named; the person at the terminal runs `goal budget` and it succeeds. A grant revoked before the child runs: refused, no act. A grant revoked between the child's end and the append: the line is written with the grant, the outcome and the rejected alternative. A child that refuses: the line records "refused" with the verb's words, and the disposition is unhandled. `landing resume` and `machine revive` under a recovery grant: refused by the verbs themselves, the person runs them. An overrule prints the impact and records it. Mutations: admit any class; drop the line when the grant ended after the act; record confirmed from the exit code alone; read an invented operation id; put `landing resume` in a class; drop the impact screen.
