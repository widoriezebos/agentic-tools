## 1. What happens today (verified)

**A launched machine has a steward and no agent.** The fleet launch runs nine steps, clone to presence (`internal/seat/launch/sequence.go:191-209`); enrollment is `steward arm` (`:687`), supervision `up --recover-only --if-down` (`:736`), and the record tells the person to type `cd <destination> && claude` themselves (`:183-186`). Every machine ticks (`internal/steward/runner.go:265-357`, `metasystem.steward.tick-seconds`, default 600, `:101-110`) and acts on nothing until a person opens a session.

**Ready goals with nobody present only notify.** Each tick reads the ledger fresh (`internal/steward/openwork.go:41`; `goal.ReadClaimableBudgetedWork`, `internal/goal/project.go:375-416`): ready goals with no live claim or job are `WorkClaimable` (`openwork.go:52-54`), a branch that wins over a held claim (`:55-61`). The ladder answers it with `ActNotify` (`internal/steward/verdict.go:111-114`) before the census is read (`internal/steward/tick.go:550-558`). Ready is strict: approved, unexpired, unpinned-elsewhere, dependency-free, budget-admitted (`project.go:752-780`; `internal/goal/approval.go:356-386`); a queued goal is never ready; a next step waiting on a human word is not excluded (`:753`).

**An idle session is never woken; a session is the only authority.** The Stop verdict blocks an idle seat while something is claimable and allows the stop once nothing is (`internal/goal/turnverdict.go:1267-1271`, `:1281-1292`); nothing reaches a stopped session later (`docs/orchestration.md:246`). Every governed act keys on the live lease-holding main: claims need the holder's positive epoch (`cmd/metasystem/goalsync_mutations.go:737-742`; `internal/goal/verbs.go:412-415`), `work build` requires the calling process to be the holder with the claim's lineage (`cmd/metasystem/goal_branch.go:463-486`), the batch return hands work back to the live holder (`goalsync_mutations.go:100-128`). An agent's commands act under the lineage its launcher named in `METASYSTEM_OWNER_LINEAGE` or `--lineage` (`cmd/metasystem/intent_planning.go:472-490`; `goalsync_mutations.go:670-676`), a person's under the enrolled terminal's (`:677-715`); the machinery's own launchers name one: the landing lane's owner runs every verb under the constant `LandingOwnerLineage` (`internal/landing/batchowner/owner.go:33`; `land.go:565`, `prove.go:545`), the mission runner's host turns under `MissionLineage` (`internal/missionrunner/host.go:449`). No code exports the variable for an interactive session. A steward-launched delegate has none of this authority, which is why every revival on this seat failed (11 `steward-*` jobs, all `failed` within a second on `--json-schema`).

**The seat's own launch path already exists.** `internal/launch` starts a headless runtime for the build, read, design and critique lanes (`launch.go:645-663`): `claude -p --model M [--effort E] --dangerously-skip-permissions --output-format json --name <kind>-<tag>` with the brief on stdin in a working directory, no `--settings`, no `--json-schema` (`internal/launch/claude.go:41-59`); a detached supervisor `metasystem launch supervise --id` (`process.go:147-148`) records supervisor, child and process-group identities (`record.go:66-96`) under `~/.metasystem/launch/<id>` (`:123-128`); the child inherits the launcher's environment except the keys the launch overrides (`process.go:88-102`). `work status` lists launches (`cmd/metasystem/intent_process.go:842`), `work stop <id>` cancels one, SIGTERM then SIGKILL on the group (`:888-895`; `launch.go:561-585`). `system stop` runs the checkout stop transition and nothing else (`intent_process.go:672-698`); `machine stop` cancels this user's running launches only after it (`cmd/metasystem/intent_machine.go:645-697`); neither `Start` nor `Supervise` consults the process-creation fence (`launch.go:200`, `:292-300`), which the runner's own start does through a creation claim and a re-read (`runner.go:224-253`; `internal/stopfence/fence.go:167`, `:280`). Those sessions run the checkout's hooks: SessionStart hands the runtime's pid and start time to `up` (`internal/runtimes/enforcement/claude-code-hooks.json:4-12`; `internal/hooks/runtime_hook_start.go:602-603`); `up` proves the pair and the caller's descent, nothing about further ancestry (`internal/up/up.go:226-285`), takes the owner lineage from the environment (`:347-353`), announces (`:744-757`; grammar-only validation, `internal/lease/verbs.go:77-78`), classifies by the announcement, MAIN (`internal/lease/classify.go:408-410`), and the lease claimer decides: unheld → fresh, epoch 1 (`internal/lease/claim.go:79-80`, `:133-149`); a live holder keeps it (`:118-121`); a dead holder of the same owner lineage is succeeded with the epoch preserved (`:124-125`, `:201-227`); a dead holder of another lineage is taken over, epoch plus one (`:127-128`, `:232-260`); `up` answers "holder" or "advisor" (`up.go:789-807`) and restamps only claims of its own lineage (`cmd/metasystem/up.go:78-131`). SessionEnd retires the announcement and nothing else (`internal/hooks/runtime_hook_stop.go:365-387`). The census counts an announced live process as a seat main (`internal/census/run.go:337-347`; `internal/steward/census.go:72-74`). A same-machine claim under another lineage is refused by name; the own pair is "already claimed by this session" (`verbs.go:1481-1486`).

**The caps do not hold.** `DryRevivals` resets whenever the accepted goals ref moves (`evidence.go:35-41`; `marks.go:24-51`). A usage-limit result feeds no outage mark (`internal/outage/outage.go`, `classifyLine`). An identical re-approval is a deliberate no-op (`approval.go:619-627`); unapprove clears it (`:747`).

## 2. Step 1: what exists after the slice

**The top-level agent is a seat main the steward starts**, under one owner lineage of its own. When ready work has no seat, the steward starts the configured runtime headless in the checkout as a launch of kind `seat`; the ordinary hooks announce it, it holds the lease, and everything after is the existing law: its Stop hook keeps it claiming while work is ready, its claims, builds, landings and returns are the holder's own, and at idle its Stop is allowed and the process exits. A successor under the same lineage inherits a dead predecessor's lease and claim by the lease's own succession rule. Wido's second question: the next tick reads the ledger again and starts the next main.

- **D-ladder: ready work, no seat → start; a dead seat of ours holding a claim → start its successor.** `decideNowWithDependencies` reads the census for `WorkClaimable` as for `WorkOwned` (`tick.go:550-558`). For `WorkClaimable`, in order: a seat launch of this steward not yet reaped → `ActNone`; a live seat main (`Workers.LiveSeatMains > 0`) → today's `ActNotify` (that session's Stop hook owns it, `turnverdict.go:1281-1284`); an incomplete census, an untracked or an unprovable process → `ActNotify`, never on doubt (`verdict.go:130-134`); a standing outage → `ActNotify`, holding (`:147-150`); a closed fence → `ActNone` with its reason (`fence.go:167`); then the selection, held work first: a goal this machine holds under the seat lineage (`Claimed`, `Landing`; the pair `ownPair`, `verbs.go:590`) whose holder the census proved dead → `ActRevive`, the successor, and the brief and the seat record name that held goal, whatever the ready set holds, because the reader ranks `WorkClaimable` above a held claim (`openwork.go:52-61`) and `Next` keeps `Claimed` apart from `Ready` (`project.go:734-746`); a claim this machine holds under any other lineage → `ActNotify` naming it (a person's or a dead person's claim is the owned-work ladder's business); no held goal and no ready goal whose next step is free of a human word (`goal.NextStepNamesAPendingHumanWord`, `internal/goal/humanword.go:11`, over `GoalFacts[id].NextStep`) → `ActNotify` naming them; the named goal capped (D-retry) → `ActNotify` naming the reset; otherwise `ActRevive`, a seat start naming the first free ready goal. For `WorkOwned`, the proven-death branch (`verdict.go:136-152`) starts the successor seat instead of a delegate when the held claim's lineage is the seat's and the census proved the death; every other owned claim keeps today's path. `DryRevivals` is not consulted for seats. The helm holds everything (`tick.go:206-208`; `revive.go:121-123`). The mint and the start run in one runner pass (`runner.go:269`, `:310-321`).
- **D-seat: the launch names its lineage.** The steward stages a brief ("You are this seat's session; `metasystem goal claim <goal>` takes the work named here, or continues what this seat already holds; work it, land it; stop when nothing is claimable") naming the goal D-ladder selected, the held goal of the seat lineage when there is one and otherwise the first ready goal free of a human word, and calls `launch.Manager.Start(StartSpec{Kind: "seat", WorkingDirectory: <checkout root>, Brief, Tag: <nonce>})` (`launch.go:87-130`). `adapterForLane` admits `seat` beside the four lanes (`:645-652`); settings gain `launch.seat.runtime`, `launch.seat.model` and `launch.seat.effort` beside `launch.seat.window.tokens` (`internal/config/defaults.go:312`; resolved as the lanes', `launch/settings.go:237-260`). The command is the lane's (`claude.go:41-59`), run in the checkout with `METASYSTEM_OWNER_LINEAGE=<seat lineage>` set and `METASYSTEM_DELEGATE_ROOT` and `METASYSTEM_SESSION_ID` set empty (`process.go:88-102`). The seat lineage is one constant per installation, `steward-seat` (grammar `internal/lease/lease.go:127`), the machinery's own convention (`LandingOwnerLineage`, `owner.go:33`; `MissionLineage`): `up` adopts it for the announcement and the lease (`up.go:347-353`, `:756-757`, `:797-799`; `claim.go:137-142`), `actingAs` sees an agent (`intent_planning.go:475`, `:485-490`), and the claim, the lease and every command carry one pair. No job record, no worktree, no `--settings`: the checkout's hooks and tool gate apply as to a session a person starts. The steward records the launch id, the named goal and its approval opid (`ApprovalRecord.Opid`, `internal/goal/file.go:199-204`), the ready set with each goal branch tip (`refs/heads/goal/<id>`, `internal/goal/branch/commit.go:50`; absent recorded as absent) and the start time under `artifacts/agents/steward/seats/<launch-id>.json`, and queues one notification: "steward: started seat <launch-id> on <machine> for <goal>; `metasystem work stop <launch-id>` ends it, `metasystem helm take` keeps the steward from starting another".
- **D-fence: `system stop` ends seats and a seat start binds to the fence.** `systemStopResult` (`intent_process.go:678-698`), after a stop that is neither refused nor unchanged, cancels this user's running launches of that checkout through `stopResolvedJob` as `stopHostMachines` does (`intent_machine.go:645-697`), one line per launch; `machine stop`'s own pass then finds none (a repeat). A launch belongs to the checkout when its working directory is the checkout or below it, as today (`placeLaunches`, `intent_machine.go:303-330`; `machinePathWithin`, `:335-341`), or is one of the checkout's registered worktrees or below one (`git worktree list --porcelain`, read by `registeredWorktrees`, `cmd/metasystem/intent_worktree.go:105-129`): the goal worktree is created there as the sibling `<checkout>-<goal>` (`:217-235`) and the build lane runs in it (`launch/unit_run.go:217`, `:338`), so a seat's running build ends with the seat. The predicate is extended in `placeLaunches` itself, so `machine list`, `machine stop` and `system stop` place launches alike; the launch record's `Goal` (`record.go:70`) is not the association, a goal id names no checkout. The steward reads the fence before every start (`stopfence.Closed`, `fence.go:167`); the seat kind's `Supervise` opens a creation claim before the child (`stopfence.Creating(root, "seat-launch", generation, self)`, `fence.go:280`; before `launch.go:292-300`) and re-reads the fence after the child record is published, ending the group when closed (`launch.go:465`), the runner's protocol (`runner.go:224-253`).
- **Authority and recovery: the seat's own lineage, no new rule.** The announced main is MAIN, the holder with the lease's epoch (`lease/verbs.go:375-385`); its `goal claim <goal>` gets the epoch at the command edge (`goalsync_mutations.go:737-742`) and `bindClaim` admits it (`verbs.go:412-415`); `work build` passes the holder check (`goal_branch.go:463-486`) and builds through the launch build lane (`launch/unit_run.go:217`, `:338`), never through dispatch (the tier-1 hazard refusal, `internal/dispatch/admission.go:300-303`, is not on its path); landing and return address the live holder. A seat that exits holding a claim, killed or limited, is a dead holder of the seat lineage: the successor's `up` succeeds the lease with the epoch preserved (`claim.go:124-125`, `:201-227`), so the claim's stop capability matches the lease (`health.go:1368-1371`; `up.go:110-112`) and `goal claim` answers "already claimed by this session" (`verbs.go:1481-1483`); the brief says to continue it. No handover, no take-over, no release, no SessionEnd: safe when the process is killed. The alternative, a release at SessionEnd, does not run on a kill and would drop work in progress (`runtime_hook_stop.go:365-387`). The Stop hook is the seat's schedule (`turnverdict.go:1286-1292`; a held claim continues; nothing claimable allows the stop; the runtime's continuation limit, `docs/design/turn-verdict-delivery-contract.md:54`, unobserved `:127`).
- **Coexistence.** A person's live session keeps today's path. A person who opens a session while the seat runs is "advisor" (`up.go:789-791`). To take the seat back in one existing verb: `metasystem work stop <launch-id>` ends the main (`intent_process.go:888-895`), and the person's next session takes over the dead holder's lease (`claim.go:232-260`); the seat's claim is then a dead session's under today's law, `goal claim G --take-over --reason TEXT` (a person's act, `verbs.go:1333`); `metasystem helm take` keeps the steward from starting another and allows the running main's next Stop; `metasystem system stop` ends everything (D-fence). Never two seats: one unreaped seat launch per steward, the census's live seat main, the lease.
- **D-retry: progress is a retained work advance; one count per approval episode; provider weather does not count; the reset is unapprove then approve.** When a seat launch ends (its record terminal, `record.go:31`), the next tick classifies it: `outage.ClassifyProviderResult` on the launch's `result.json` (`claude.go:60-98`; `outage.go:239-262`; `classifyLine` gains the usage-limit class: `usage limit`, `rate_limit`, HTTP 429) → `provider-limit`, mark fed, never counted; the ladder holds while the mark stands and starts the successor when it lapses (`Horizon`, `outage.go:35`). Otherwise progress is one of: a goal branch tip of the recorded ready set, or of a goal the seat lineage holds, that moved or appeared (`branch/commit.go:50`); a `land-ready` or `done` history line by the seat lineage (`verbs.go:2175`, `:2592`). Claim, release, park and every other history line are not progress. Consecutive no-progress seats whose named goal carries the same approval opid count; at `MaxRevivals` (3, `tick.go:79-81`) that goal is capped: the steward names the next free ready goal instead, or starts none and notifies "3 seats ended without progress on <goal>; `metasystem goal unapprove <goal> --reason TEXT` then `metasystem goal approve <goal>` starts again, `metasystem goal pause <goal> --reason TEXT` parks it" (`approval.go:747`, `:683`, `:460-463`; the identical re-approve stays the no-op of `:619-627`). Any seat with progress resets the count.
- **Bounds.** The Stop contract, the goal's tuple (`approval.go:356-386`), the seat's window (`launch.seat.window.tokens`); the process by `work stop`, `helm take`, `system stop`. No wall-clock cap in step 1 (section 4).

**The human review before landing** (Wido's caveat, 2026-09-30). Already true in the engine: a goal at or above `landing.review.human-from-tier` (default 2, `internal/config/defaults.go:229`) waits for a person's word before its work lands, and one below it lands by itself after `landing.review.auto-after` (default 4h, `:231`; `internal/goal/landgate.go:103-105`, `:240-270`, `:304-343`); the word is `goal review G --record PATH --verdict clear-to-land|send-back`, bound to the branch tip it was drafted for (`:262-267`; `internal/goal/review.go:29-40`), a sitting `--hold` that lets nothing land whatever the tier (`landgate.go:126`, `:245-248`), or `goal land-without-sitting G --reason TEXT` (`:371`); a landing claim stands outside the one-claim quota (`review.go:41-46`; `verbs.go:1342-1349`), so the seat that holds it claims the next ready goal meanwhile; and the holder takes the due step on its own Stop: `HolderStepsDue` (`landgate.go:614-630`) yields a revision while a send-back stands (`SentBackOf`, `review.go:564-570`) and a landing when `LandingDue` (`:345-370`), taken through the public command by the Stop hook's holder step (`cmd/metasystem/goal.go:597-600`; `cmd/metasystem/holder_step.go:27-31`), and the batch lane evaluates the gate again before anything lands (`internal/landing/batchowner/prefix.go:88`, `:138`). What the build adds is one rule in D-ladder's held-goal selection: a `Landing` claim of the seat lineage is held work for a successor only when `HolderStepsDue` names a step for it and no review hold stands on it (`HoldsOf`, `landgate.go:126`), because `LandingDue` at or above the tier answers on the word alone and ignores a hold placed after clear-to-land (`:355-368`) while `Gate` refuses under any hold (`:245-248`); while the gate waits for the person's word, or a sitting holds it, or the grace below the tier has not run, it is neither selected nor counted, for `WorkClaimable` and for the proven-death branch alike. The human-word filter does not see the gate (the next step says nothing; the wait is the `Landing` record and the history), which is why the rule is needed. So: (a) a seat whose goal waits on the gate takes other ready work if there is any and otherwise stops at idle (`turnverdict.go:1267-1271`), and no successor starts, nothing counts, no cap is reached while the goal waits or a hold stands; (b) after clear-to-land at the tip, D-ladder selects the goal once its landing is due, the successor starts under the same lineage with the goal named, and its first Stop takes the landing (`holder_step.go:29-31`, verb `land`): at or above the tier the word makes it due at once, so the next tick; below the tier the person's word restarts the `auto-after` grace (`ReadGate` takes the clock from the newest human act after the `Landing` record, `landgate.go:325-334`; `LandingDue` needs `Eligible`, `:355-361`), so the successor starts at the first tick after that grace, four hours by default; (c) after a send-back with its correction brief, published beside the history line (`review.go:25-26`, `:47-50`), `SentBackOf` stands, the successor starts the same way and its Stop takes the revision from that brief (verb `revise`), which the `send-back` answer line ends (`landgate.go:611-613`); (d) a person who wants every goal reviewed sets `landing.review.human-from-tier=1` (1 through 4, 4 meaning none waits; `internal/config/landinggate.go:82-88`) in `metasystem.conf.local` or the committed conf, and `auto-after` then applies to nothing: Wido's enterprise mode is that one setting.
