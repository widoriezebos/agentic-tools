## 1. What happens today (verified)

**A launched machine has a steward and no agent.** The fleet launch (`internal/ui/httpd/launch.go:82-84`) runs the sequence's nine steps, clone to presence (`internal/seat/launch/sequence.go:191-209`, `:229-251`); enrollment is `steward arm` (`:687`), supervision is `up --recover-only --if-down` (`:736`), and the record's `Next.Session` tells the person to type `cd <destination> && claude` themselves (`:183-186`). Nothing starts a session. So every machine ticks (`internal/steward/runner.go:265-357`, every `metasystem.steward.tick-seconds`, default 600, `:101-110`) and acts on nothing until a person opens a session.

**Ready goals with nobody present only notify.** Each tick reads the shared ledger fresh (`internal/steward/openwork.go:41`, `goal.ReadClaimableBudgetedWork`, `internal/goal/project.go:375-416`) and classifies it (`openwork.go:48-80`): ready goals with no live claim or job are `WorkClaimable` (`:52-54`). The ladder answers `WorkClaimable` with `VerdictIdleBacklogDead`, `ActNotify` (`internal/steward/verdict.go:111-114`) before the census is even read (`internal/steward/tick.go:550-558` reads workers only for `WorkOwned`). Revival exists only for owned work whose worker is provably dead (`verdict.go:136-152`; `runner.go:310-321` runs `revive` on `ActRevive`). Ready is already strict: `Next` admits only approved, unexpired, unpinned-elsewhere, dependency-free goals that claim admission accepts, budget included (`project.go:752-780`; `internal/goal/approval.go:356-386`); a queued goal is never ready (`goal show seat-works-without-a-person` today: "the goal waits for a person's approval").

**An idle session is never woken.** The Stop verdict blocks an idle seat only while something is claimable (`internal/goal/turnverdict.go:1267-1271`: no claimable goals resets the count and allows the stop); after three refusals it prepares a `seatIdle` continuation intent with the seat's own actor and a deferred claim (`:1281-1284`, `:1367-1409`; `cmd/metasystem/goal.go:730-760`). Nothing reaches a stopped session later: `session wait` covers a pid, a job or a question (`docs/orchestration.md:246`). Wido's second question is answered by the tick, not the session: the tick's fresh read sees a goal the moment it turns ready; only the ladder refuses to act on it.

**The continuation path exists and has never worked on this seat.** Intent minting, staging, one-shot consumption, launch and stamp are in place (`internal/steward/stage.go:101-135`, `intervene.go:125-194`, `revive.go:94-217`), launched through `internal delegate --revive <nonce>` (`cmd/metasystem/steward_verbs.go:237-255`; `delegate.go:434-439` maps it to `dispatch --steward-intent`). Every one of the 11 `steward-*` job records on this seat is `failed`/`runtime_error` within a second (2026-09-29 05:34 to 2026-09-30 11:39), every log the same: `Error: --json-schema is not a valid JSON Schema: no schema with key or ref "https://json-schema.org/draft/2020-12/schema"`, then `HANDSHAKE-FAILED` (`internal/delegation/launch.go:272-275`). Cause: the Claude adapter hands the compacted role schema to `claude -p ... --json-schema` (`internal/adapter/supervisor/claude.go:146-156`, `internal/adapter/claude.go:411-413`), and `steward-continuation.schema.json:2` is the only role schema declaring draft 2020-12 (`implementer.schema.json:2` and `design-critic.schema.json:2` declare draft-07); Claude CLI 2.1.285 has no 2020-12 meta-schema. All 13 continuation closures in `notifications.jsonl` are "outcome unknown".

**The seat-idle path strands a claim.** `CompleteRevival` claims first (`revive.go:175-193`), then consumes, counts and launches (`:199-216`); a launch that returns an error (`:210-212`) leaves the claim standing. Live case: intent `9d3b378259b303a0` claimed `ask-what-happened-follow-ups` as `ui+main-1790664507-87928-61899b` at 11:24Z, the launch failed as above, the reaper closed it unstamped at 11:49Z (`reap.go:177-192`), the goal is `claimed` with no job (`work status`: 0 running), and the seat's next third refusal minted `5f3fa07aee97ea1f` for the same held claim. Wido took the helm at 13:58 to hold the steward off it (`system status`); `CompleteRevival` honours that (`revive.go:121-123`).

**Two caps do not hold.** `DryRevivals` is reset whenever the marks move (`internal/steward/evidence.go:35-41`), and the marks are HEAD plus the accepted goals ref tip (`marks.go:24-51`); on a shared ledger any seat's claim, the steward's own included, moves that tip. `highwater.json` reads `dryRevivals: 0` after three launches today. And a Claude usage-limit result feeds no outage mark: `classifyLine` knows overload words and HTTP codes only (`internal/outage/outage.go`, `classifyLine`; fed from `internal/adapter/adjudicate.go:134-150`), while spend already recognises the CLI's own words `Your claude.ai usage limit` (`internal/spend/reader_claude.go:596`).

## 2. Step 1: what exists after the slice

**The top-level agent is the steward tick plus one continuation job per ready goal**, launched by the machinery that exists. Why the others lose: a long-lived headless session is exactly the live idle main the ladder never displaces (`verdict.go:6-7`, `:120-128`); it would hold the checkout lease, turn every ready goal into "owned by a live worker", need a wake-up channel that does not exist, and spend a session on nothing. A new daemon beside the steward duplicates the tick, the arbitration lock, the intents and the reaper. The steward is already armed on every launched machine (`sequence.go:687`) and already owns "open work with no worker"; step 1 widens that to "ready work with no seat".

- **D-ladder: claimable work with nobody present revives.** `decideNowWithDependencies` reads the census for `WorkClaimable` as it does for `WorkOwned` (`tick.go:550-558`), and `decideForRevivalWithDependencies` likewise (`revive.go:274-281`). In `Decide`, `WorkClaimable` becomes: an unreaped continuation → `VerdictIdleBacklogDead`, `ActNone` (the shape of `:138-141`); a live seat main (`Workers.LiveSeatMains > 0`, `census.go:70-74`: an announced session) → today's `ActNotify` unchanged, because the seat's own Stop hook owns that case (`turnverdict.go:1281-1284`); an incomplete census, an untracked or an unprovable process → `ActNotify`, never spawning on doubt (the rule of `:130-134`); the dry cap or a standing outage → `ActNotify` as `:142-150`; otherwise `ActRevive`, reason "ready work and no live seat; starting a continuation". The helm still holds everything (`tick.go:206-208`; `revive.go:121-123`). No grace ticks: a goal is claimable the tick it is approved and the standing order forbids waiting; the mint and the launch run in the same runner pass (`runner.go:269`, `:310-321`), so hours of idleness followed by an approval cost at most one tick. What stops it: the continuation ends at a milestone (`internal/protocol/roles/steward-continuation.md:28-30`), the reaper closes it (`reap.go:32-78`) and frees the guard, and the next tick reads the ledger again.
- **D-mint: the steward names the goal from the reader, never from the reason text.** `stewardRevive` mints today only for `WorkOwned` from `LegacyOpenWork` and takes the goal as `TrimPrefix(reason, "current goal: ")` (`steward_verbs.go:200-205`); in the converted world that reason is a sentence (`openwork.go:60`), so the brief would name a sentence. Step 1: `stewardRevive` reads `goal.ReadClaimableBudgetedWork` once (as `revive.go:323`), and mints for `WorkOwned` with `Claimed[0]`, reason "worker provably dead with open work" as today, or for `WorkClaimable` with the first id of `Claimable` whose next step does not wait on a human word (`goal.NextStepNamesAPendingHumanWord`, the loop of `turnverdict.go:1319-1326`, kept steward-side so `internal/goal` is untouched), reason `seatEmpty` (a new constant beside `seatHandoffReason`), `ClaimNeeded` true, `SeatActor` nil. `StageIntent` is unchanged: same role, `workspace` preset (`stage.go:18-22`), roster from `role.steward-continuation.runtime` falling back to `role.default.*` (`internal/dispatch/roster.go:148-193`; this seat resolves claude, `claude-opus-5-5`, `metasystem.conf.local:25`).
- **D-claim: nothing is claimed before the launch is stamped.** A `seatEmpty` intent skips the deferred claim (`revive.go:175` stays `seatIdle`-only) and launches unclaimed; the continuation claims on arrival as its contract already says (`steward-continuation.md:16-19`: "run metasystem goal claim, which takes only the ready goal goal list --ready returns; if another seat wins that claim, claim again"). A failed launch therefore strands nothing. Across machines the ledger claim and the one-claim-per-machine quota arbitrate (`internal/goal/validate.go:393-394`; `verbs.go:1326-1357`); on this machine one unreaped consumed intent is the guard (`tick.go:576`; `intervene.go:264-276`). The `seatIdle` claim-before-launch defect stays as it is (section 4, open question 2).
- **D-schema: the continuation schema declares draft-07** like every other role (`steward-continuation.schema.json:2`). Live intents staged on the old digest are refused as "return schema drifted" (`stage.go:207-209`), cancelled, and re-minted next tick. Without this line step 1 launches nothing.
- **D-cap: the cap is per goal and survives ledger movement.** Before minting for a goal, the steward counts consumed intents naming it (`intervene.go:231-260`, all records, reaped included) that were minted after the goal's current approval (`ApprovalRecord.At`, `internal/goal/file.go:199-201`) and did not end "with a valid return" (`reap.go:228`). At `MaxRevivals` (3, `tick.go:79-81`) that goal is skipped and one notification says so, naming `goal approve` (a re-approval resets the count) and `goal park`; when every ready goal is capped the tick notifies and mints nothing. `DryRevivals` stays as it is.
- **D-limits: a usage limit is provider weather.** `classifyLine` gains the usage-limit class (`usage limit`, `rate_limit`, HTTP 429), so a continuation that dies on Wido's own limit feeds the mark and `ProviderOutage` holds revival (`verdict.go:147-150`; `revive.go:164-174`) until the mark lapses (`Horizon`, 30 minutes, `outage.go:35`), after which one probe launch either works or feeds it again: wait and resume, one launch per half hour, never a burn per tick. Budgets need nothing new: the goal tuple gates admission (`approval.go:356-386`), the job carries `capMin` and `--max-budget-usd` (`adapter/claude.go:421-424`).
- **D-words: a person sees the start and knows the two stops.** After `StampLaunch` (`revive.go:213`) one notification is queued: "steward: started continuation <job> for <goal>; `metasystem work stop j2:<job>` ends it; `metasystem helm take` holds the steward" (today a successful revival is silent, `runner.go:335-337`). `system status` already lists running dispatch jobs and `work status` the job; `system stop` closes the process-creation fence and refuses every launch (`runner.go:84-93`; `launch.go:241-251`).
- **Coexistence.** A person's live session keeps today's path: the tick notifies, the session's third idle refusal hands off with its own actor. A session announced between mint and launch bumps the enrollment fence (`internal/lease/verbs.go:208`) and cancels the revival (`revive.go:134-139`). A session opened after the launch is the lease holder; the continuation works in its job worktree under the `workspace` preset (`internal/protocol/permissions/workspace.json`) and takes no lease (delegate hooks skip announcement and lease, `docs/orchestration.md:485-487`); the person's Stop hook sees the job in flight (`project.go:315-322`) and is not idle-blocked; a claim by the person on the same goal is refused by the ledger, on another goal by the quota with its release command (`verbs.go:1356`), which is the existing law; `work stop` gives the seat back.

## 4. Later, when it hurts

- `seatIdle` claim-before-launch: release the steward-made claim when the launch seam errors (`revive.go:210-212`), one test; today it hurts only because every launch fails (D-schema).
- Many ready goals: one continuation at a time per checkout is the guard; a second lane means a second guard, not before one works.
- A person's idle session beside ready work for hours without a turn ending: the Stop hook never fires, so nothing escalates; a tick-side rule for "live main, no progress, claimable work" is the next step if it is ever observed.
- `machine list`'s board says "installation .../artifacts/agents/steward is not the armed checkout" for every seat today (`system status`): a root-resolution defect in the board, outside this slice.
