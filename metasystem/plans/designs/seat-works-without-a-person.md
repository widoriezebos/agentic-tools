## 1. What happens today (verified)

**A launched machine has a steward and no agent.** The fleet launch (`internal/ui/httpd/launch.go:82-84`) runs the sequence's nine steps, clone to presence (`internal/seat/launch/sequence.go:191-209`, `:229-251`); enrollment is `steward arm` (`:687`), supervision is `up --recover-only --if-down` (`:736`), and the record's `Next.Session` tells the person to type `cd <destination> && claude` themselves (`:183-186`). Nothing starts a session. So every machine ticks (`internal/steward/runner.go:265-357`, every `metasystem.steward.tick-seconds`, default 600, `:101-110`) and acts on nothing until a person opens a session.

**Ready goals with nobody present only notify.** Each tick reads the shared ledger fresh (`internal/steward/openwork.go:41`, `goal.ReadClaimableBudgetedWork`, `internal/goal/project.go:375-416`) and classifies it (`openwork.go:48-80`): ready goals with no live claim or job are `WorkClaimable` (`:52-54`), and that branch wins over a held claim (`:55-61`). The ladder answers `WorkClaimable` with `VerdictIdleBacklogDead`, `ActNotify` (`internal/steward/verdict.go:111-114`) before the census is read (`internal/steward/tick.go:550-558` reads workers only for `WorkOwned`). Revival exists only for owned work whose worker is provably dead (`verdict.go:136-152`; `runner.go:310-321` runs `revive` on `ActRevive`). Ready is already strict: `Next` admits only approved, unexpired, unpinned-elsewhere, dependency-free goals that claim admission accepts, budget included (`project.go:752-780`; `internal/goal/approval.go:356-386`); a queued goal is never ready.

**An idle session is never woken.** The Stop verdict blocks an idle seat only while something is claimable (`internal/goal/turnverdict.go:1267-1271`); after three refusals it prepares a `seatIdle` continuation intent with the seat's own actor and a deferred claim (`:1281-1284`, `:1367-1409`; `cmd/metasystem/goal.go:730-760`). Nothing reaches a stopped session later (`docs/orchestration.md:246`). Wido's second question is answered by the tick, not the session: its fresh read sees a goal the moment it turns ready; only the ladder refuses to act.

**The continuation path exists and has never launched on this seat.** Intent minting, staging, one-shot consumption, launch and stamp are in place (`internal/steward/stage.go:101-135`, `intervene.go:125-194`, `revive.go:94-217`), launched through `internal delegate --revive <nonce>` (`cmd/metasystem/steward_verbs.go:237-255`; `delegate.go:434-439`). Every one of the 11 `steward-*` job records on this seat is `failed`/`runtime_error` within a second, every log the same: `Error: --json-schema is not a valid JSON Schema: no schema with key or ref "https://json-schema.org/draft/2020-12/schema"`, then `HANDSHAKE-FAILED` (`internal/delegation/launch.go:272-275`). The Claude adapter hands the compacted role schema to `claude -p ... --json-schema` (`internal/adapter/supervisor/claude.go:146-156`, `internal/adapter/claude.go:411-413`), and `steward-continuation.schema.json:2` is the only role schema declaring draft 2020-12 (`implementer.schema.json:2`, `design-critic.schema.json:2` declare draft-07); Claude CLI 2.1.285 has no 2020-12 meta-schema. All 13 continuation closures in `notifications.jsonl` are "outcome unknown".

**The claim is not bound to the launch.** The seat-idle path claims first (`revive.go:175-193`), then consumes and launches (`:199-216`); a launch that errors (`:210-212`) leaves the claim. Live case: intent `9d3b378259b303a0` claimed `ask-what-happened-follow-ups` as the seat's pair at 11:24Z (the claim side worked), the launch failed as above, the reaper closed it unstamped (`reap.go:177-192`), and the goal is `claimed` with no job; Wido took the helm at 13:58 to hold the steward off it (`revive.go:121-123`). The launch itself carries no goal: the steward branch of dispatch copies role, brief, job, runtime, model and permissions and nothing else (`internal/delegation/dispatch_phase.go:133-136`), so the job record has `goalId` null, goal admission is skipped (`admission.go:208-210`) and the budget projection never counts it (`internal/dispatch/budget.go:390`). `work stop` cancels the process and closes the record only (`cmd/metasystem/intent_process.go:909-923`). And a delegate cannot claim: the command edge gives a claim epoch to the lease holder, a `--by` human or a HUMAN caller only (`cmd/metasystem/goalsync_mutations.go:737-742`), `bindClaim` refuses without one (`internal/goal/verbs.go:412-415`), and an agent's goal verbs take their lineage from `METASYSTEM_OWNER_LINEAGE` or `--lineage` (`goalsync_mutations.go:670-676`).

**The caps do not hold.** `DryRevivals` resets whenever the marks move (`internal/steward/evidence.go:35-41`); the marks are HEAD plus the accepted goals ref tip (`marks.go:24-51`), which any seat's claim moves: `highwater.json` reads `dryRevivals: 0` after three launches today. A valid return is protocol validity, not progress: `blocked` and `yielded-to-live-worker` are valid outcomes (`steward-continuation.schema.json:58-66`), `reap.go:228`. A usage-limit result feeds no outage mark (`internal/outage/outage.go`, `classifyLine`: overload words and HTTP codes; fed from `internal/adapter/adjudicate.go:134-150`, `:186-191`) while spend recognises the CLI's words (`internal/spend/reader_claude.go:596`). An identical re-approval is a deliberate no-op (`approval.go:619-627`).

## 2. Step 1: what exists after the slice

**The top-level agent is the steward tick plus one continuation job per ready goal.** A long-lived headless session is the live idle main the ladder never displaces (`verdict.go:6-7`, `:120-128`), would hold the lease, and needs a wake-up channel that does not exist; a new daemon duplicates the tick, the arbitration lock, the intents and the reaper. The steward is armed on every launched machine (`sequence.go:687`) and already owns "open work with no worker"; step 1 widens that to "ready work with no seat" and binds the claim to the launch.

- **D-ladder: claimable work with nobody present revives.** `decideNowWithDependencies` reads the census for `WorkClaimable` as for `WorkOwned` (`tick.go:550-558`), `decideForRevivalWithDependencies` likewise (`revive.go:274-281`). In `Decide`, `WorkClaimable` becomes, in this order: an unreaped continuation → `VerdictIdleBacklogDead`, `ActNone` (the shape of `:138-141`); a live seat main (`Workers.LiveSeatMains > 0`, `census.go:70-74`) → today's `ActNotify`, because that session's own Stop hook owns the case (`turnverdict.go:1281-1284`); an incomplete census, an untracked or an unprovable process → `ActNotify`, never spawning on doubt (`:130-134`); a standing outage → `ActNotify`, holding (`:147-150`); every candidate capped or waiting on a human word (`Snapshot.ClaimableHeld`, from D-mint) → `ActNotify` naming the reset; otherwise `ActRevive`. `DryRevivals` is not consulted in this branch; it stays the owned-work cap. The helm holds everything (`tick.go:206-208`; `revive.go:121-123`). No grace ticks: the mint and the launch run in the same runner pass (`runner.go:269`, `:310-321`), so hours of idleness followed by an approval cost at most one tick. What stops it: the continuation ends at a milestone (`internal/protocol/roles/steward-continuation.md:28-30`), the reaper closes it (`reap.go:32-78`) and frees the guard, the next tick reads the ledger again.
- **D-mint: one chooser, held claim first, human word and cap excluded.** `stewardRevive` reads `goal.ReadClaimableBudgetedWork` once (as `revive.go:323`) in place of `LegacyOpenWork` and the `TrimPrefix(reason, "current goal: ")` derivation (`steward_verbs.go:200-205`, which in the converted world names a sentence, `openwork.go:60`). A claim this machine holds is continued, never switched (`Claimed[0]`, `ClaimNeeded` false, the rule of `turnverdict.go:1300-1307`); a held goal whose next step waits on a human word (`goal.NextStepNamesAPendingHumanWord`) is reported and nothing is minted. With no held claim the target is the first ready goal in priority order that neither waits on a human word nor is capped (D-retry), the loop of `:1319-1326` kept steward-side. Reason `seatEmpty`, `ClaimNeeded` true. `StageIntent` is unchanged (`stage.go:101-135`; roster `role.steward-continuation.*` falling back to `role.default.*`, `internal/dispatch/roster.go:148-193`; this seat resolves claude, `claude-opus-5-5`, `metasystem.conf.local:25`). The intent records the goal's approval opid at mint (`ApprovalRecord.Opid`, `internal/goal/file.go:199-204`) as `GoalApproval`.
- **D-claim: the steward claims, the launch carries the goal, the same pair releases.** Before consumption `CompleteRevival` claims a `ClaimNeeded` target as it does for `seatIdle` (`revive.go:175-193`, `:239-266`, generalised to `seatEmpty`), with the pair and epoch of D-authority recorded on the intent (`SeatActor`, `SeatClaimEpoch`, `intervene.go:48-53`); a refused claim (the one-claim-per-machine quota, `verbs.go:1326-1357`; a race lost to another machine) cancels and notifies as today (`:180-192`). The launch is bound to the goal: the steward branch of dispatch copies `authorization.Goal` into the goal argument (`dispatch_phase.go:133-136`; `DispatchAuthorization.Goal` already exists, `dispatch_authorization.go:12`), so goal admission runs (`admission.go:208-216`, refusing a spent attempt or minute allowance), the reservation is bound to the claim (`internal/dispatch/claim.go:394-432`, `binding.Machine == MachineID`) and the record carries `goalId`, which the budget projection counts (`budget.go:390`). The continuation acts as the claim's own pair: the revive child exports `METASYSTEM_OWNER_LINEAGE=<pair lineage>` (`steward_verbs.go:243-249`, beside `METASYSTEM_DELEGATE_ROOT`), which the supervisor carries into the runtime child (`internal/adapter/supervisor/deps.go:94`, `:110`; `claude.go:240-253`) and the goal verbs read (`goalsync_mutations.go:670-676`); its bare `goal claim` continues the held goal and never switches (`cmd/metasystem/intent_planning.go:1069-1073`), and it needs no epoch of its own because it claims nothing. One function, `ReleaseContinuationClaim(root, nonce)`, releases exactly the pair the intent recorded through `goal.ReleaseWithReason` (`verbs.go:2099-2105`; own pair only, `:2136-2139`, a foreign release stays a human act; no standing claim is the repeat, `:2133`, which is what a landed goal answers). Three callers: `CompleteRevival` when the launch seam errors (`revive.go:210-212`), the reaper at every close (`reap.go:57-66`), and `work stop` after `CANCELLED` for a job whose id names a steward intent (`intent_process.go:909-923`), whose summary then says "released the claim the steward made on <goal>". A `ClaimNeeded` false intent releases nothing. Health reads a claim whose lineage is the steward's and whose intent is unreaped as current, not foreign (`internal/steward/health.go:1373-1387`; with no lease holder the role is already alive, `:1352-1354`).
- **D-authority: the pair and epoch of a claim nobody's session holds (open question 1).** With no lease, `ClassifyVerb` supplies no epoch (`internal/lease/verbs.go:375-382`), and the seat-idle path only works because it passes the lease holder's recorded epoch in-process (`revive.go:251-258`). Recommended default, designed here: pair `{machine, "steward-g<generation>"}` (grammar `internal/lease/lease.go:127`), epoch the enrollment generation (`InstallIdentity.Generation`, `internal/steward/identity.go:61-63`, always positive): the one act a person made at a terminal with `steward arm` or `system start` that put this steward in charge, which every intent already binds to (`intervene.go:28-29`; `dispatch_authorization.go:45-48` refuses a superseded generation). Epoch consumers tolerate it: breach stop records it for the resume (`internal/goal/stop.go:485`), restamp touches only the holder's own lineage (`internal/goal/restamp.go:42`), health is D-claim's last line.
- **D-schema: the continuation schema declares draft-07** like every other role (`steward-continuation.schema.json:2`). Live intents staged on the old digest are refused as "return schema drifted" (`stage.go:207-209`), cancelled and re-minted. Without this line step 1 launches nothing.
- **D-retry: one count per goal per approval; provider weather does not count; the reset is unapprove then approve.** At every close the reaper classifies the job with the adjudicator's own classifiers (`outage.ClassifyLogs` on the job log, `ClassifyProviderResult` on its result, `adjudicate.go:186-191`; `classifyLine` gains the usage-limit class: `usage limit`, `rate_limit`, HTTP 429): a hit is recorded on the consumed intent as `provider-limit`, feeds the mark, and is never counted; the ladder holds while the mark stands (`verdict.go:147-150`) and, when it lapses (`Horizon` 30 minutes, `outage.go:35`), launches again on its own, one probe per horizon. Every other close is progress or not: progress is a valid return whose `landed` is non-empty (`steward-continuation.schema.json:67-72`); `blocked`, `yielded-to-live-worker`, an empty `landed`, no return and a failed launch alike are not. Before minting, the chooser counts consumed intents naming the goal with `GoalApproval` equal to its current `Approved.Opid` and a non-progress outcome; at `MaxRevivals` (3, `tick.go:79-81`) the goal is capped: skipped by D-mint, and one notification says "3 continuations for <goal> ended without progress; `metasystem goal unapprove <goal> --reason TEXT` then `metasystem goal approve <goal>` starts it again, `metasystem goal pause <goal> --reason TEXT` parks it". Unapprove clears the approval and the budget (`approval.go:747`, human-only `:683`); the next approve writes a fresh record with a new opid (`:460-463`); an identical re-approve alone is the no-op of `:619-627` and resets nothing, by design. Budgets need nothing new beyond D-claim: the goal tuple gates admission (`approval.go:356-386`; `admission.go:208-216`), the job carries `capMin` and `--max-budget-usd` (`adapter/claude.go:421-424`).
- **D-words: a person sees the start and knows the stops.** After `StampLaunch` (`revive.go:213`) one notification is queued: "steward: started continuation <job> for <goal> as <pair>; `metasystem work stop j2:<job>` ends it and releases that claim; `metasystem helm take` holds the steward" (today a successful revival is silent, `runner.go:335-337`). `system status` lists running dispatch jobs; `system stop` closes the process-creation fence (`runner.go:84-93`; `launch.go:241-251`).
- **Coexistence.** A person's live session keeps today's path. A session announced between mint and launch bumps the enrollment fence (`internal/lease/verbs.go:208`) and cancels the revival (`revive.go:134-139`). A session opened after the launch is the lease holder; the continuation works in its job worktree under the `workspace` preset (`internal/protocol/permissions/workspace.json`) and takes no lease (`docs/orchestration.md:485-487`); the person's Stop hook sees the job in flight (`project.go:315-322`); a claim by the person on another goal meets the quota with its release command (`verbs.go:1356`), and `work stop j2:<job>` ends the job and releases the steward's claim in one command (D-claim), after which the person claims freely. Never two workers on one goal: the ledger claim, the quota, one unreaped continuation per checkout (`tick.go:576`; `intervene.go:264-276`).

## 4. Later, when it hurts

- A continuation's landing hand-back: the batch return hands a claim back to its pair at its epoch (`internal/landing/batch/return.go:55-61`) through a handover whose target must be the live lease holder with a live announcement (`goalsync_mutations.go:100-128`); a steward pair has neither, so a continuation that reaches `work land` gets its return refused. Step 1 proves claim, launch, work on the goal branch and a return with `landed`; the hand-back is the next slice (open question 2).
- Many ready goals: one continuation at a time per checkout is the guard.
- A person's idle session beside ready work for hours without a turn ending: nothing escalates; a tick-side rule if it is ever observed.
- The board's "installation .../artifacts/agents/steward is not the armed checkout" line for every seat (`system status`): a root-resolution defect outside this slice.
