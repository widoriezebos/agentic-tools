## 1. What happens today (verified)

**A launched machine has a steward and no agent.** The fleet launch runs nine steps, clone to presence (`internal/seat/launch/sequence.go:191-209`); enrollment is `steward arm` (`:687`), supervision `up --recover-only --if-down` (`:736`), and the record tells the person to type `cd <destination> && claude` themselves (`:183-186`). Every machine ticks (`internal/steward/runner.go:265-357`, `metasystem.steward.tick-seconds`, default 600, `:101-110`) and acts on nothing until a person opens a session.

**Ready goals with nobody present only notify.** Each tick reads the ledger fresh (`internal/steward/openwork.go:41`; `goal.ReadClaimableBudgetedWork`, `internal/goal/project.go:375-416`): ready goals with no live claim or job are `WorkClaimable` (`openwork.go:52-54`), a branch that wins over a held claim (`:55-61`). The ladder answers it with `ActNotify` (`internal/steward/verdict.go:111-114`) before the census is read (`internal/steward/tick.go:550-558`). Ready is strict: approved, unexpired, unpinned-elsewhere, dependency-free, budget-admitted (`project.go:752-780`; `internal/goal/approval.go:356-386`); a queued goal is never ready.

**An idle session is never woken; a session is the only authority.** The Stop verdict blocks an idle seat while something is claimable and allows the stop once nothing is (`internal/goal/turnverdict.go:1267-1271`, `:1281-1292`); nothing reaches a stopped session later (`docs/orchestration.md:246`). Every governed act keys on the live lease-holding main: claims need the holder's positive epoch (`cmd/metasystem/goalsync_mutations.go:737-742`; `internal/goal/verbs.go:412-415`), `work build` requires the calling process to be the holder with the claim's lineage (`cmd/metasystem/goal_branch.go:463-486`), the batch return hands work back to the live holder (`goalsync_mutations.go:100-128`; `internal/landing/batch/return.go:55-61`). A steward-launched delegate has none of it, which is why every revival on this seat (11 `steward-*` jobs, all `failed` within a second on `--json-schema`) and every rev-2 patch failed the same way (section 6).

**The seat's own launch path already exists.** `internal/launch` starts a headless runtime for the build, read, design and critique lanes (`launch.go:645-663`): `claude -p --model M [--effort E] --dangerously-skip-permissions --output-format json --name <kind>-<tag>` with the brief on stdin in a working directory, no `--settings`, no `--json-schema` (`internal/launch/claude.go:41-59`); a detached supervisor `metasystem launch supervise --id` (`process.go:147-148`) records supervisor, child and process-group identities (`record.go:66-96`) under `~/.metasystem/launch/<id>` (`:123-128`); `work status` lists launches (`cmd/metasystem/intent_process.go:842`), `work stop <id>` cancels one, SIGTERM then SIGKILL on the group (`:888-895`; `launch.go:561-585`). Those sessions run the checkout's hooks: SessionStart runs `internal hook claude start` (`internal/runtimes/enforcement/claude-code-hooks.json:4-12`), which hands the runtime's own pid and start time to `up` (`internal/hooks/runtime_hook_start.go:602-603`); `up` proves the pair and the caller's descent from it, nothing about further ancestry (`internal/up/up.go:226-285`), announces it (`:744-757`), classifies it by its own announcement, MAIN (`internal/lease/classify.go:408-410`), claims the lease when none is held (`lease/verbs.go:126`; `claim.go:58`, fresh epoch 1 `:133-149`, takeover of a dead holder `:232-260`) and answers "holder" with the epoch, or "advisor" beside a live holder (`up.go:789-807`). The lineage is its own main id when no `METASYSTEM_OWNER_LINEAGE` reaches it (`:347-353`, `:797-799`); the child inherits the launcher's environment except the keys the launch overrides (`process.go:88-102`). The delegate marks are job custody and `METASYSTEM_DELEGATE_ROOT` (`runtime_hook_start.go:94`; `internal/adapter/supervisor/deps.go:82-94`); a launch carries neither. A blocked Stop is `{"decision":"block","reason":…}`, an allowance a system message, and the runtime's finite continuation limit is part of the delivery contract (`docs/design/turn-verdict-delivery-contract.md:20-22`, `:54`), unobserved on a real Claude host (`:127`). SessionEnd retires the announcement (`claude-code-hooks.json`, `internal/hooks/runtime_hook_stop.go:365-387`). The census counts an announced live process as a seat main (`internal/census/run.go:337-347`; `internal/steward/census.go:72-74`).

**The caps do not hold.** `DryRevivals` resets whenever the accepted goals ref moves (`evidence.go:35-41`; `marks.go:24-51`). A usage-limit result feeds no outage mark (`internal/outage/outage.go`, `classifyLine`). An identical re-approval is a deliberate no-op (`approval.go:619-627`).

## 2. Step 1: what exists after the slice

**The top-level agent is a seat main the steward starts.** When ready work has no seat, the steward starts the configured runtime headless in the checkout, as a launch of a new kind `seat`; the ordinary hooks announce it, it takes the lease, and from there everything is the existing law: its Stop hook keeps it claiming while work is ready, its claims, builds, landings and batch returns are the holder's own, and at idle its Stop is allowed and the process exits. A delegate, headless or not, loses because no governed act admits one (section 1); a daemon loses because the steward already ticks. Wido's second question: the next tick reads the ledger again and starts the next main.

- **D-ladder: ready work, no seat, no held claim → start.** `decideNowWithDependencies` reads the census for `WorkClaimable` as for `WorkOwned` (`tick.go:550-558`). In `Decide`, `WorkClaimable` becomes, in order: a seat launch of this steward not yet reaped → `ActNone`; a live seat main (`Workers.LiveSeatMains > 0`) → today's `ActNotify`, that session's own Stop hook owns it (`turnverdict.go:1281-1284`); an incomplete census, an untracked or an unprovable process → `ActNotify`, never spawning on doubt (`verdict.go:130-134`); a standing outage → `ActNotify`, holding (`:147-150`); a claim this machine holds (`Claimed`, `Landing` or fenced, from the reader) → `ActNotify` naming it, because a dead lineage's claim is the owned-work ladder's business, not a new seat's; the head ready goal capped (D-retry) → `ActNotify` naming the reset; otherwise `ActRevive`, which now means "start a seat". `DryRevivals` is not consulted here. The helm holds everything (`tick.go:206-208`; `revive.go:121-123`); a closed process-creation fence refuses the launch as it refuses every child (`runner.go:84-93`). The mint and the start run in the same runner pass (`runner.go:269`, `:310-321`): hours of idleness followed by an approval cost at most one tick.
- **D-seat: the launch.** `stewardRevive`'s `ActRevive` for `WorkClaimable` stages a brief ("You are this seat's session; `metasystem goal list --ready` names the work; claim, work, land; stop when nothing is claimable") and calls `launch.Manager.Start(StartSpec{Kind: "seat", WorkingDirectory: <checkout root>, Brief, Tag: <nonce>})` (`launch.go:87-130`). `adapterForLane` admits `seat` beside the four lanes (`:645-652`); the settings gain `launch.seat.runtime`, `launch.seat.model` and `launch.seat.effort` beside the existing `launch.seat.window.tokens` (`internal/config/defaults.go:312`), resolved as the other lanes' are (`launch/settings.go:237-260`); this seat's runtime is claude. The command is the lane's own (`claude.go:41-59`), run in the checkout with three environment overrides set empty, `METASYSTEM_OWNER_LINEAGE`, `METASYSTEM_DELEGATE_ROOT` and `METASYSTEM_SESSION_ID` (`process.go:88-102` keeps everything else the runner inherited), so `up` derives the lineage from the main itself (`up.go:347-353`) and no delegate mark reaches the hooks. No job record, no worktree, no steward claim, no `--settings`: the checkout's hooks and tool gate apply as they do to a session a person starts (`claude-code-hooks.json:4-30`). The steward records the launch id, the ready head goal and its approval opid (`ApprovalRecord.Opid`, `internal/goal/file.go:199-204`) and the accepted tip at start under `artifacts/agents/steward/seats/<launch-id>.json`, and queues one notification: "steward: started seat <launch-id> on <machine> for ready work (<goals>); `metasystem work stop <launch-id>` ends it, `metasystem helm take` keeps the steward from starting another".
- **Authority: the main's own, no new rule.** The announced main is MAIN by its own announcement (`classify.go:408-410`), the holder with the lease's epoch (`lease/verbs.go:375-385`; `claim.go:133-149`), its lineage its main id (`up.go:797-799`; `lease/verbs.go:191`). Its `goal claim` gets the epoch at the command edge (`goalsync_mutations.go:737-742`) and `bindClaim` admits it (`verbs.go:412-415`); bare `goal claim` takes the ready frontier's head and continues a held goal (`cmd/metasystem/intent_planning.go:1069-1073`); `work build` passes the holder check because it is the holder (`goal_branch.go:463-486`) and builds through the launch build lane (`launch/unit_run.go:217`, `:338`; `intent_process.go:992`), never through dispatch, so the tier-1 hazard refusal on dispatch admission (`internal/dispatch/admission.go:300-303`) is not on its path; landing and the batch return address the live holder (`goalsync_mutations.go:100-128`), which it is. The Stop hook is its schedule: claimable work blocks the stop with the claim it should make (`turnverdict.go:1286-1292`), a held claim continues, nothing claimable allows the stop, and in print mode an allowed stop ends the process; the runtime's continuation limit ends a turn that keeps refusing (`turn-verdict-delivery-contract.md:54`), and the three-refusal seat-idle escalation stays as it is (`:1281-1284`; section 4).
- **Coexistence.** A person's live session keeps today's path (D-ladder's second rule). A person who opens a session while the seat main runs is "advisor" (`up.go:789-791`) exactly as beside any holder; the seat's card is on `work status` and the board. To take the seat back in one existing verb: `metasystem work stop <launch-id>` ends the main (`intent_process.go:888-895`), after which the person's next session start takes over the dead holder's lease (`claim.go:232-260`) and its claim is a dead session's claim under today's law, `goal claim G --take-over --reason TEXT` (a person's act) or `goal release` at the terminal (`verbs.go:2136-2139`); `metasystem helm take` keeps the steward from starting another (`revive.go:121-123`) and makes the running main's next Stop an allowance, so it exits at its turn end; `metasystem session stop --by NAME` is the quiet form of that. Never two seats: one unreaped seat launch per steward, the census's live seat main, the lease.
- **D-retry: one count per approval episode; provider weather does not count; the reset is unapprove then approve.** When a seat launch ends (its record is terminal, `record.go:31`), the steward classifies it at the next tick: `outage.ClassifyProviderResult` on the launch's `result.json` (`claude.go:60-98` reads the same file; `outage.go:239-262`; `classifyLine` gains the usage-limit class: `usage limit`, `rate_limit`, HTTP 429) → `provider-limit`, mark fed, never counted, the ladder holds while the mark stands and starts again when it lapses (`Horizon` 30 minutes, `outage.go:35`); otherwise progress is at least one ledger history line whose actor lineage is the main's (`HistoryLine.Actor`, `verbs.go:400-407`: a claim, a slice, a landing, a release), read from the accepted projection since the recorded tip; no line is no progress. Consecutive no-progress seats whose recorded head goal carries the same approval opid count; at `MaxRevivals` (3, `tick.go:79-81`) that goal is capped: no seat starts while it heads the frontier, and one notification says "3 seats ended without progress on <goal>; `metasystem goal unapprove <goal> --reason TEXT` then `metasystem goal approve <goal>` starts again, `metasystem goal pause <goal> --reason TEXT` parks it". Unapprove clears the approval and budget (`approval.go:747`, human-only `:683`); the next approve writes a record with a new opid (`:460-463`); an identical re-approve alone is the no-op (`:619-627`). Any seat with progress resets the count.
- **Bounds.** A turn ends by the Stop contract (allowed when nothing is claimable; the continuation limit); the work by the goal's tuple (`approval.go:356-386`) and the seat's context window (`launch.seat.window.tokens`); the process by `work stop`, `helm take`, `system stop`. No wall-clock cap in step 1 (section 4).
