## What this goal delivers

Four changes, three of them built; each usable the day it lands:

1. **U1, built (`5423205bf`).** `sh proof/full.sh` is the one gate, by hand and in the lane: static first, then every test of `internal/...` and `cmd/...` as test-name shards in parallel, one line per package and shard, every test that never reported named, red unless every expected package and test said `ok`. A panic loses one shard, never the package. Its second round, the first tier inside the full proof, is deferred: in the lane the cheap tier is the gate before the full proof (fact 11), by hand it is `metasystem test impact` before `sh proof/full.sh`.
2. **U5, built (`a239787be`), with a correction round to build (`plans/landing-takes-an-hour-u5-round-2.md`).** Every landing has a clock: hand-in, merge, the cheap gate, each proof with its shards, each fix round (a return and its re-hand-in), push, in minutes, on the lane's push record, in `landing status`, on the goal's landed line and in `goal status`; a push completes its bookkeeping once, and a repeat after a lost response is a success. The round adds the cheap gate's minutes (the lane's cheap tier, read from `gates.jsonl`) in place of the words "not measured until the pipeline's first tier", and finishes a confirmed push's bookkeeping before the keeper replaces its batch.
3. **U4, built (`d9c55e8d7`) with its reverse-dependents round committed (`944588c71`), and a second round to build (`plans/landing-takes-an-hour-u4-round-2.md`).** The unit's declared check, `metasystem test impact`, selects from the round's base: every changed `internal/` package whole, every `internal/` package that imports a changed package whole (measured 03:30: two landings left main red in importers that never ran; the round kept `cmd/` importers by name, since nearly every internal package has `cmd/metasystem` as a dependent), the tests of `cmd/...` by name, the unit's own new and changed tests, and the callers of a changed test helper. The second round makes the verb a lane proof command: it verifies its base as a commit and an ancestor, failing closed (measured: a mistyped base selected nothing, silently), runs exactly the selections `LANDING_ONLY` names when the lane replays a red, and ends with the landing report lines the lane reads.
4. **U3, the one unbuilt unit here, about 60 lines by Astra's estimate.** A red proof is told from main's red with one replay run: the reporter and the cheap command take `LANDING_ONLY` as a list, so the lane runs the failed units once on main's tip instead of once per unit; a unit red on main is main's and holds the batch (Wido 2026-09-16; measured 2026-10-09: main's red is fixed first), the rest are the goal's and go back with it. The cheap gate hands its base as a commit, and the proof budget counts each attempt's newest outcome and only reds, so a flake's green repeat and a validation after main moved never spend the goal's two attempts.

Not in this goal, deferred to a later goal with the reason under "Deferred to a later goal": per-package owners across several members, the lane's fix commits and their admission, the owner's return, the budget per membership (one goal per batch makes each moot); U2 (the heavy proof lease, `test full`, `work wait --lease`, the host lines, red-so-far, the settle); U1's second round; the scoped fix-round reprove; `BatchOwned`; the reconstruction that carries fixes.
