## What this goal delivers

1. `landing drain` closes automatic admission, lets admitted work finish under the lane's policies, then holds. `landing stop` remains the immediate pause. Only a proved person clears either fence.
2. Ledger, records and runtime-settings commits do not make an otherwise identical engine stale.

**Step 1 is U1a, drain over today's lane.** Its owners are the plain lane's queue lock, queue/proof/regeneration records, the existing landing start/stop commands, and the keeper. Its public proof drains a nonempty lane, refuses a new agent hand-in, finishes admitted work, then follows the printed retry after a person reopens admission. It is usable without the lane-policy follow-up's execution consumers. U1b adds mechanism 1's helm take/return drain integration after 1b's helm; U3 adds engine-input comparison to its existing owners; none is needed to make U1a useful. Units are build increments; this goal is handed in only when all its units are finished and read.

The retained units add no fresh-ledger-read prerequisite. Decision and unit numbers remain stable after removing Decision 2/U2 and Decisions 4–5/U4–U5.

The threat is mistakes, races, stale reads and interrupted processes on this trusted host. A person's act overrides advice, never another writer's ownership or the requirement for a proved tree. “No HAL 9000” and “YOU ONLY INTEGRATE WHEN YOU ARE DONE WITH A GOAL” remain binding.

## Decision 1: drain is one admission fence beside the queue (U1a; helm integration U1b)

**Sites.** `internal/landing/plain/queue.go:112` owns `withLock`, `:262` owns `HandIn`, and `:411` owns `Pending`. `cmd/metasystem/landing_plain.go:150` adapts public hand-ins. `cmd/metasystem/intent_landing.go:684` owns `laneResumable`, `:719` owns `startLane`, and `:951` owns `stopLane`; `internal/landing/lane/pause.go:35` reads an unreadable pause as standing. The keeper is composed at `cmd/metasystem/landing_agent.go:221`, with `Observe` at `:227`; wake decisions are in `internal/landing/plain/wake.go:39`, `:51` and `:83`; shared status is `internal/landing/plain/status.go:114`. Proof start takes the queue lock at `internal/landing/plain/prove.go:324`; regeneration is owned by `internal/landing/plain/regeneration.go:34`, `:58`.

**Record and owner.** New `plain.SetDrain`, `ReadDrain`, `ClearDrain` and `AdvanceDrain` own one lane-local `drain.json` beside the queue. Its fields are `by`, `at`, `reason`, `source` (person or the originating helm's checkout/by/at identity), and `state` (`draining` or `held`). Missing means open; unreadable means admission unknown and closed for agents. Time is stored in UTC and displayed locally. Write by atomic replacement under `plain.withLock`; no copied drain queue or new daemon. `SetDrain` is called by person-only `landing drain [--reason TEXT]` and a lane helm take. A repeat preserves the first standing drain's attribution; an explicit drain can replace unreadable bytes. It never changes pause or a running process.

**Admission.** Inside `HandIn`'s existing lock, classify the proposed change before appending. A same-goal/same-commit repeat or metadata update to an existing waiting line adds no work and is allowed. A new goal, a newer commit, a returned goal submitted with `--again`, or a new records hand-in adds work and is refused for an agent while drain stands. A returned line without `--again` remains the existing return response, never a new admission. Carry the verified actor separately from the line; supplied `by`, exception metadata or a held helm cannot manufacture person proof. A person's explicit hand-in may extend the drain set, with a warning and `by` provenance on that line; a held drain becomes draining in the same locked act. All whole-goal, branch ownership and proof requirements still apply. No queue lock spans a network publication.

An agent's refused hand-in says who closed admission (or that the drain is unreadable). Its next action is **`metasystem work land G` after `metasystem landing status --repo <lane>` reports admission open**; preserve necessary original options such as `--again` or `--records`. Status is the observation, never the reopening act. The message identifies that a person must reopen admission and does not instruct the agent to run `landing start`. It does not create a new question on every retry. Existing questions remain visible.

**Finishing.** The keeper calls `AdvanceDrain` from `Observe` after releasing the home lock, alongside `SyncStopQuestion` (`internal/landing/lane/agent.go:171–179`, `cmd/metasystem/landing_agent.go:227`). Under the queue lock it reads all waiting membership, landed containment at one captured main tip, the proof record and regeneration record. Count incident-held waiting entries too: `Pending` filters eligible work and is insufficient to decide that the drain is empty. Only no waiting entry and no unfinished proof/regeneration permits `held` in U1a; `lane-reads-its-policies` adds its active-batch check when it introduces that record. A failed read or undecidable process leaves progress unknown, never held. Invalid queue lines must be reported as unknown membership rather than skipped into an empty drain. `AdvanceDrain` returns unknown membership as status data, never as an `Observe` error: admitted eligible work whose membership is known can still run, while unknown membership prevents recording held. An actual `AdvanceDrain` write error does not hold the keeper; a failed held-state write leaves the drain draining. Report any actual `AdvanceDrain` operation error separately from `SyncStopQuestion`'s error, retaining each cause and its own remedy. The keeper observation/result adapter must name the drain read or progress write and the affected record for drain failures, with repair of that source followed by another observation; a stop-question synchronization failure names that operation and its repair/retry. Never wrap a drain failure as “the lane's stop question cannot be reconciled”, discard one error when both occur, or short-circuit question synchronization on unknown drain progress. A completed push observed at a later pass can release membership; stale main can delay completion but cannot declare unlanded work landed.

While draining, the keeper may continue eligible admitted work and wake for its completed proof. Held drains do not launch empty agents. Both drain states suppress the periodic trunk timer; a proof needed to clear an incident blocking admitted work remains available through today's proof owner; the later proof-policy decision belongs to `lane-reads-its-policies`. Explicit person-requested proof also remains available. Read the fence again at each new agent/proof/regeneration admission, with the same membership and policy checks; a stale wake result alone grants nothing. A proof already admitted when drain closes finishes and its proved work can push. A person's hand-in that changes membership and held-to-draining state serializes with completion under the queue lock.

Pause always wins over automatic progress. Keep the existing host pause lock and regeneration stop path; do not introduce reverse lock nesting between the host keeper lock and the plain queue lock. `AdvanceDrain` takes the install/queue lock only after `Observe` has released the home lock, following the existing install-before-home order, never its reverse (`internal/landing/lane/agent.go:171–179`). Fence rechecks already under the home lock, including the keeper's start claim and `runRegeneration`, read atomically replaced `drain.json` without taking the queue lock. `landing drain` and `AdvanceDrain` may wait for regeneration to release the queue lock: `Resolve` holds it across regeneration (`internal/landing/plain/resolve.go:75`), which takes the home lock at `internal/landing/plain/regeneration.go:61`; that wait must never retain the home lock.

**Start, including F1.** Preserve `laneResumable`'s person proof and extend it to any standing pause **or** drain, including unreadable records. An agent start clears neither; with no fence it can still request the ordinary readiness check. For a proved person, clear drain under the queue lock and pause through its existing owner **before** independent keeper readiness checks. Report each successful removal and any partial I/O failure accurately, then readiness and its actual remedy. A dead or unarmed keeper cannot veto reopening. Start never returns the helm. An unset in progress remains an ownership fence: finish `landing unset` before starting a lane whose registration is being removed.

**Helm integration (U1b, mechanism 1's drain sentence).** Extend 1b's lane take to call `SetDrain` after its policy signature write, recording that signature's identity as source. Report a partial take if either effect fails; the same explicit take retries the missing effect. Every explicit lane take ensures a drain stands, including a repeated take after a person used start. Preserve an already standing manual drain. Return removes the policy override and clears only a readable drain whose source matches the returned helm; manual, replaced or unreadable drains remain and the person is told to run `landing start`. Return never clears pause. Clearing drain with start while a helm stands reopens admission immediately; there is no reopening record and no observer that recreates drain merely because a helm exists. `--all` reuses 1b's per-target acts and partial results.

`landing status` and the board use `ReadStatus`: admission open; draining with the total waiting count; drained and holding; paused with drain still standing; or unknown drain/membership with its read error. Status explicitly says when undecodable queue lines make membership unknown and prevent the drain from reaching held. Show helm policy holds separately. Draining under a helm retains today's holds; the later person batch/proof acts belong to `lane-reads-its-policies`, and drain itself supplies no such permission.

## Decision 3: reuse the existing engine projection for re-arm and dispatch (U3)

**Sites.** `internal/steward/runner.go:724` defers on whole-commit inequality before source verification. `internal/steward/rearm_resolver.go:519` checks ancestry and the narrower `internal,cmd` list; `:632` already compares ENGINE projections; `:686` verifies the enrolled source at a destination. `internal/delegation/infra.go:50` owns dispatch's separate skew preflight. `internal/behaviorsurface/policy.v2.json:3` declares ENGINE inputs: `cmd/**`, `internal/**`, `scripts/agents/**`, `go.mod`, `go.sum`. Expose the existing projection comparison as `steward.EngineInputsEqual`, returning equal/different or error plus the first changed path when available. Re-arm, enrolled-source verification and dispatch preflight share it and the existing landed-stamp resolver; remove their contradictory path-list/whole-commit shortcuts. Keep source membership in the configured landed history and the existing witness-stamp handling. Resolve the actual stamped source, enrolled provenance and captured destination afresh. Dispatch compares with the destination's landed base, as its current preflight intends, rather than treating a goal's unlanded edits as an engine deployment.

Use the existing ENGINE input declaration rather than another suffix allowlist: it covers Go sources and embedded files, module/toolchain declarations in `go.mod`, the build owner in `cmd/devgate`, and agent scripts. Preserve the projection's file-kind, submodule and unknown-diff handling. Runtime `metasystem.conf`, ledgers and records are outside it; embedded schemas/defaults remain inside it. If implementation finds an actual build input outside this declared closure, add that input to this one declaration with a discriminating test, not a second filter. This is engine-input equality, not equality of the commit stamp or of the landing proof's ledger allowlist.

Equal inputs allow reuse of the enrolled bytes despite different commits and clear the stale deferred note. Different inputs retain the current rebuild/re-arm route. Dirty engine inputs, unowned sources, an in-flight operation and source/checkout races retain their existing protection; comparison of two captured commits does not bless changed working bytes. Report source commit, destination commit and comparison in existing command/re-arm diagnostics, using today's reads without a fresh-ledger observation dependency. No host deployment stamp service or records ref is built here.

**Five answers.** The three production consumers above call the comparator; their command/re-arm results consume its diagnostics. Immutable commits and the embedded projection definition identify each comparison; current enrollment, landing ref, destination and dirty-state checks are read per admission. The comparison never confers person authority: a person can use existing explicit system rebuild/start acts, but cannot bless unknown executable bytes as proved. A real difference names `go run ./cmd/devgate build`, then `metasystem session start` in the affected checkout; the public test follows both. `EngineInputsEqual` returns a Git/comparison error, never equality. Its re-arm and enrolled-source validation callers treat missing source, malformed stamp, failed comparison or unreadable build input as unknown executable/source identity and retain their safety refusal. The dispatch owner `internal/delegation/infra.go:50` alone treats a Git failure in its advisory skew preflight as unavailable and fails open to the normal work gates. Dispatch's skew preflight retains its existing fail-open behavior for no merge base and Git failures (`internal/delegation/infra.go:57–64`): report comparison unavailable and continue to normal work admission, without claiming equality or prescribing a rebuild that cannot repair the cause. If normal admission names an independent repair, follow that owner's remedy and retry `metasystem work build`; the skew preflight adds no rebuild/start loop. Source/enrollment validation retains its own safety checks. Failures in source/enrollment validation remain executable/source safety failures, not advisory settings refusals; a person's independent preparation is not refused merely because ledger/settings commits moved.
