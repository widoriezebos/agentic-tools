# Design: a person's reply reaches the seat that asked, helm or not

- Id: 01M4WX5C0B3QFP1781C941SRYD
- Kind: design
- Status: accepted
- Goals: replies-reach-the-asking-seat replies-reach-the-asking-seat-2

## Problem

One bot and one central inbox serve every seat on the host (Wido's rule). On 2026-10-14 the m1e seat asked a channel question at 08:59 (thread 1216). Wido replied "yes" at 09:13 (message 1217, reply to 1216). The landing seat polled the bot, verified the sender, committed the record to the shared ledger (`channel inbox telegram-1217`, `question: unmatched`, 09:23) and said nothing. The m1e seat never matched it: `matchInbox` (`internal/channel/inbox.go:477`) matches committed records against this seat's own questions only when this seat polls, and the m1e seat never polled: the steward runner skips its duties under the helm (`internal/steward/runner.go:366`, before the channel duty at line 442), so no match ran. (The old `cursor.json` is not evidence of the last poll: polling neither reads nor writes it, `channel_test.go:1166`; corrected after Astra round 2, RRS-6.) `question wait Q` does poll and match (`cmd/metasystem/channel_verbs.go:344` calls `channel.Poll`), but nobody waits on a question whose act already happened, and `question list` and `question show`, the verbs a person runs to look, do not poll (Astra round 1, RRS-2). The question stayed open until it was withdrawn by hand at 11:18.

## Decisions

D1. Matching a committed reply to this seat's open question is the person's act arriving, not a machine decision. One shared operation, "settle replies", runs the match (`matchInbox` over the ledger tip) and also resumes every unfinished answer: a question already persisted `State="answered"`, `Phase="matched"` whose publication failed (`internal/channel/inbox.go:604` persists before publishing) is published to completion, never skipped as "not open" (Astra round 1, RRS-1; the recovery fixture at `internal/channel/channel_test.go:1247` is extended through every new entry point: interrupt after matching, retry, answer published once). Settle-replies never closes a question that asks for a person's act: landing action requests are created without a goal or `UnitStop` binding (`internal/landing/plain/policy_question.go:347`) and today a goal-less answer is recorded locally and closed (`internal/channel/poll.go:187`, `:249`); settle-replies leaves every action request outstanding (it records the reply against it, renders "replied 'yes' at 09:13; the act is still needed: …") and only the request's own effect-reconciliation owner closes it when the act succeeded; a fixture asserts that a reply leaves the request open and the landing records unchanged (Astra round 3, RRS-7). The helm tick runs settle-replies exactly as the ordinary tick does; it still launches nothing and reports only. `question list`, `question show` and `question answer` run settle-replies before answering, so the person at a terminal sees the reply the moment it is in the ledger; `question wait` keeps its poll.

D2. An unmatched verified reply is told back, once, by the seat that received it, after the host's other seats had one poll interval to match it. "Handled" needs an explicit predicate: a successful match updates the asking seat's question and goal history but not the inbox record's `question` field (`inbox.go:604`, `poll.go:210`), and status replies are marked only in the receiving seat's `inbox-handled.json` (`inbox.go:544`); U2 therefore adds one shared handled-reply marker (the reply's message id written into the ledger the moment the reply is recorded against a question, matched or answered, independent of the action's completion, and the status-reply path writing the same marker) and the notice fires only when no marker exists after the interval (Astra round 2, RRS-4). Cutover: notices are eligible only for replies received after the unit's activation (the activation time is recorded with the seat's channel state); replies from before it are never noticed, whatever their inbox record says, because existing handled evidence lives only in asking seats' closed answers whose phase is `closed` and which recovery skips (`poll.go:93`; e.g. inbox `telegram-1055` verified-unmatched at c701317a9 while its asking seat's question is answered); a fixture holds that closed-answer/unmatched-inbox pair (Astra round 3, RRS-8). The notice reads: "I could not match 'yes' (09:13) to an open question; the questions open now are: …" with the open questions of every seat on the host (each seat's `artifacts/agents/channel/questions` is readable by the others; the host board lists the seats). Until then the receiving seat is silent, as today, so a reply matched by its asking seat is never second-guessed.

D3. A settled answer is shown as an answer. When settle-replies matches and publishes a reply, `advanceAnswerWithEndpoint` closes both phases (`internal/channel/poll.go:249`) and today's `questionView` (`cmd/metasystem/intent_questions.go:135`) then renders the question as withdrawn or drops it. U1 distinguishes an answered closure from a withdrawal and renders the reply the command just settled ("answered 'yes' at 09:13"); its entry-point tests assert the rendered line, not only the publication (Astra round 2, RRS-3). `question list` shows a reply that awaits this seat's match ("a reply 'yes' at 09:13 awaits matching; run metasystem question list again or metasystem question wait Q"), and a question whose act already happened closes on the act: today the build question is generic (`Kind: "other"`, the command kept in prose, `cmd/metasystem/intent_work.go:1528`) with no action binding that `ActCommand` (`internal/channel/unit_stop.go:33`) recognises, and an ordinary reply closes it on the reply (`poll.go:249`). U3 binds a person-act question to the act it asks for (the exact verb, goal and work) so the act's own success closes the question and says so ("the build this asked about started at 08:59 at your terminal"); a reply to an already-closed question is acknowledged, never re-applied (Astra round 2, RRS-5; finding 305).

D4. UX (Wido 2026-10-14: intent, not internal structure): no record paths, ledger terms ("inbox record", "unmatched", "verified") or seat names in anything the person reads; the person reads "your reply reached the question" or "I could not match your reply to a question", and the one thing to do next.

## Units

- U1 (D1 + D3 rendering): settle-replies as one operation (match + resume unfinished answers); action requests are left outstanding with the reply recorded (RRS-7 fixture); the helm tick and the question verbs run it; an answered closure renders as the answer, never as a withdrawal; tests: a reply committed by another seat matched by this seat under the helm and by `question list` without a tick, with the rendered line asserted; the recovery fixture (interrupt after matching, retry) through each new entry point. ≤200 lines.
- U2 (D2 + D4): the shared handled-reply marker written by answered closures and by the status-reply path; the activation cutover (notices only for replies received after it; the closed-answer/unmatched-inbox fixture); the one notice for an unmatched reply after the interval, listing the host's open questions in plain words; test with two seats' question stores, a reply handled by the other seat, and a matched action reply whose request is still outstanding (no notice, landing records unchanged; Astra round 4, RRS-9). ≤180 lines.
- U3 (D3 closes-on-act): the action binding of a person-act question and its closure by the act's success; the awaiting-match line; a reply to a closed question acknowledged. ≤180 lines.

Delivery boundary (2026-10-15 10:58): replies-reach-the-asking-seat delivers U1 (with its correction 1: settling never blocks a person's question verbs) and is concluded at its landing; U2 and U3 are delivered by goal replies-reach-the-asking-seat-2, so each goal integrates once (Astra's critique of the 24-hour plan, F1).
