## Problem

`landing status` composes its first line in `cmd/metasystem/intent_landing.go:420-460` from `Describe` (`internal/landing/state/describe.go:124-140`, last matching priority wins, then the overrides at `:200-264`) and always appends "N waiting, M returned in the queue" (`:462, :514-525`). The fragment "batch sealed HH:MM: N members" is added whenever the batch has a `SealedAt` (`describe.go:278-280`); `ShadowOf` keeps a closed batch that still has members (`plain/shadow.go:107-146`) and the lifecycle retires only a closed batch without members (`state/lifecycle.go:54-56`), so the batch that landed at 18:47 on 2026-10-14 was in the headline at 07:18 the next day. The "Queue" block (`intent_landing.go:583-631`) lists every hand-in in queue.jsonl (`plain/status.go:44-49`, `:446-451`); the submission lifecycle row that retires a landed or returned entry after 24 hours (`state/lifecycle.go:20,45-47`) applies only to the shadow's hand-ins (`shadow.go:155`), never to the Queue table or the headline's words, so entries from 2026-10-02 still show. The to-do comes from `plain.LaneStatusRemedy` (`plain/remedy.go:22-41`) and the `Words` table (`describe.go:47-91`); the audit `TestLaneRemediesNameIntentVerbs` (`plain/remedy_test.go:49`) checks that a hinted verb is on a list, not that it changes the state that produced the hint — `landing resume` (`plain/resume.go:33-221`) reads nothing of `Main.Flakes`, so the unstable-checks headline (`describe.go:203-208`) hints a verb that cannot act on it. The seat's `goal list` banner (`cmd/metasystem/goal_list_view.go:59-74`) reads `trunk-red.json` from the seat's accepted tree (`internal/goal/trunkred.go:23`; staleness banner after 30 min, `goal/project.go:197-202`; only `--fetch` advances it) while the lane reads the register from freshly fetched main (`plain/hold.go:32-51`) and judges main from its own results (`shadow.go:217-231`): on 2026-10-15 07:24 the seat said "main is red in adopt since Tue 12:33" about a flake fixed 17 hours earlier. At every proof start the describer shows "failed" for about 15 seconds before the first observation (finding 303).

## Decisions

D1. The headline is the lane's current state and nothing else. Every batch closed by the existing batch owner leaves both the headline and the "Selected batch" block — closure through a confirmed push, a recorded return, external landing, supersession or deferral alike (`internal/landing/plain/batch.go:590-612`, `:383-429`); its historical records remain available (Astra LS-9); the words after the headline count current work only. Startup is represented at the launch owner and the shared observation boundary: "starting the check" is shown only while launch ownership is live (`plain/prove.go:565-589` persists a pending launch with no process identity before the launcher runs; `plain/status.go:781-795` classifies it as failed today); an explicit launch failure and a dead pending owner stay failures (the distinctions of `proof_admission_correction_test.go:13-53` and `verb_guard_test.go:74-91` are kept) (Astra LS-1).

D2. The current Queue is the work that can still land, partitioned from History by outcome, independently of the 24-hour retention window (`state/lifecycle.go:45-46` needs a landing time that `status.go:534, 694-706` only derives from pushes of the last day; external landings have none, `status_test.go:198-199`): landed and superseded entries leave the Queue at once (as the default view already hides them, `intent_landing.go:587-590`); the Queue holds only the latest hand-in of each goal, keeps an unresolved return whatever its age, and moves an earlier return to History when a new hand-in of that goal arrives (today queue reduction supersedes only waiting entries, `plain/queue.go:238-242`); `--verbose` shows History under its own heading with the recorded event time or "time not recorded". Withdrawal is not specified here (deferred until its command and recorded outcome are) (LS-5, LS-6).

D3. A hint has its stated effect. Every rendered hint — from the `Words` table, the describer's overrides (`describe.go:195-208, 261-263`) and headline parsing (`plain/remedy.go:26-37`) — is executed by one audit as its exact command on a fixture in the state that produced it, and the audit asserts the hint's named observable result (an inspection hint may leave state unchanged; a mutation in which `resume` clears a pause but does not start the requested check must fail the audit, `plain/resume.go:228-247`) (LS-7). An unstable-check request is rendered only when the outstanding-action owner (goal asks-clear-on-their-effect) reports that re-judging is still required, and then hints `metasystem landing prove --trunk --fresh` (`--fresh` is what keeps a trunk check from inheriting unaffected results once a-records-change-runs-its-affected-checks lands, `cmd/metasystem/intent_landing_prove.go:219`; the audit supplies a reusable baseline and a records-only change whose affected checks exclude the unstable test, asserts that the test executes again, and fails when `--fresh` is removed; Astra LS-12); otherwise retained flakes are shown as information, never as the headline or the to-do, and never replace the current activity (the amended lane-clock D3; LS-4). For an engine incident the corrective hint is `metasystem landing incident clear ID` after inspection through `metasystem landing incident list` (the existing wording still asks for `resume`, `describe.go:90`; clearing is `ClearEngineIncidents`, `plain/engine_incident.go:81`; the audit asserts the named incident disappears, unrelated incidents remain, and substituting `landing resume` fails — the correction one-person-one-truth D3 transfers to this design; LS-13). The goal-list hint `metasystem incident list` is valid and stays (`intent_planning.go:428-434`); finding 322's `landing incident list` becomes a read-only view of ENGINE incidents (they live under the landing installation, `plain/engine_incident.go:64-75`, apart from the goal incident register), admitted before the clear-only authority check (`landing_engine_incident.go:8-21`), from an agent invocation and an enrolled terminal alike, clearing nothing (LS-3).

D4. One truth about main. `landing status` and the seat's `goal list` banner consume one shared current-main projection taken from a coherent observation — the applicable commit, its verdict, the actual proof time (not the push time that `shadow.go:237-240` can substitute), and any observation failure — keeping the applicable-result and non-run rules of lane-proof-finds-its-own-engine (neither `Status.Shadow`, the previously captured record, `plain/observation.go:195-197`, nor `TrunkProof`, the latest result without a current-commit check, `status.go:503-507`, is that projection) (LS-2). Both consumers identify a lane-backed verdict as applying to the lane's last fetched commit and show its refresh time when known or "refresh time not recorded"; neither presents an observation time or a proof time as evidence that remote main was freshly checked (the observation's coherence check, `plain/observation.go:123-148, :246`, proves only that local references did not change during the observation, and `plain/hold.go:32-51` reads the supplied commit: a registered but stopped lane would otherwise give both consumers the same obsolete verdict) (LS-10). Without a lane, the banner names its source and age ("as of this seat's tree, 4 h old; goal list --fetch refreshes it"), and in every case it says "red" only for entries of a red class: the register also holds flakes, pending and known flakes, hangs and quality entries (`goal/trunkred.go:27-36`), which the incident-list renderer already distinguishes (`intent_planning.go:2452-2457`) while the banner labels every open entry red (`goal_list_view.go:60-73`) (LS-8).

D5. UX: every line a person reads says what the lane does now and the one thing to do next; no internal state words, record paths or seat names.

## Amendment: the headline names the admitted scope; times and durations are for people (2026-10-16, accepted after Astra 2 → 3, stop rule)

### The evidence

- 347: during the scoped check of main c0487ef65 (07:01:45–07:06:05, "main moved by records only; 5 checks run, the rest holds from the 06:08 check") the headline read "proving (0 of counting units, 4.0 min); proving main (0 of counting units, 4.0 min); the lane proves main" for the whole run: the unit count never resolved and the scope was never named; the scope reason exists only in the result afterwards (results.jsonl `scopeReason`). A scoped 5-check run and a 235-unit full proof look the same to a person until they end.
- 345: `landing health` ends "steward: prove main before asking anything 874.5796184666667 min ago" (`internal/landing/plain/health.go:565`, `measured(elapsedMinutes(...))` rendered with %s); the same line says "lane running since 07:54" for a start 22 hours earlier, without the day.

### The amendment

D1 (the headline is the lane's current state) gains: **the headline names the admitted scope and its progress.** While a check runs, the headline reads the scope pinned at admission (records-scope U1's `admittedScope`: full or scoped, with the selected checks and the reason) and the progress of THAT scope: "proving main (scoped: 5 affected checks of a records-only move, 2 of 5 done, 2 min)" or "proving main (full: 118 of 235 units, 12 min)". "counting" appears at most in the first tick before the plan is known, never after the plan exists; when the plan is known and no unit has finished, the headline says "0 of 5 done", not "counting". The same scope words appear in the result line when the check ends ("main green in scope: 5 affected checks, the rest holds from the 06:08 full check").

D5 (every line a person reads says what the lane does now) gains: **times and durations are for people.** A duration is rendered as whole minutes under an hour, as hours and minutes above ("14 h 34 min"), never as an unrounded number; a time of day older than today carries its day ("since Wed 07:54"); one renderer owns both, used by `landing status`, `landing health` and the describer, with an audit that scans every rendered line of the status, health and describe outputs in the existing fixtures for a bare float or a bare time older than the fixture's day.

U1's scope grows by these two items (its brief is amended accordingly; the brief's inventory sets the bound). Tests: a scoped check fixture whose headline is read at the first tick, mid-run and at the end (the scope words and the progress of the admitted plan, no "counting" after the plan); a full check fixture (the unit progress as today); the health line with a steward row aged 874.58 minutes ("14 h 35 min ago") and a lane start on the previous day ("since Wed 07:54"); mutation: drop the scope words from the running headline (the scoped fixture fails); render the raw float (the audit fails).
