## What it delivers

`work brief G --work U --out FILE` produces one unit's specification from its accepted design and retained review decisions. The builder receives the exact Decision text, including folded acceptance changes, the public test and mutation, actual reference matches, current recurring finding classes with examples, and the run's declared check. The same composer serves build and correction admission, so hand-written scaffolding cannot bypass it. Invalid code citations identify the path and line that failed against the build base.

**Step 1 is B1:** selecting one unit copies its Decision and public test without including another unit's work. This is useful as a reviewable brief immediately; citation checking, reference matches, record-derived advice and automatic admission follow in B2–B5. The design's four must-deliver requirements remain here. No partial implementation lands or switches on the wider machinery.

The threat model is mistakes, incomplete input, concurrent edits and stale observations by trusted participants. Text from a review is evidence, never authority. Composition performs no model call, test execution, goal mutation or deletion. Existing design admission, round stops, person authority and check execution remain their owners' decisions.

## Failure, cutover and deferred homes

Preparation is synchronous and performs bounded local work over the requested base and retained store, with cancellation propagated through existing Git/process adapters; it launches no retries or jobs. A cancellation or required-input failure leaves the output unwritten. The existing atomic file writer preserves a previous output; after successful publication, repeating identical input is unchanged. Blob, authority, required specification and write failures are distinct from unavailable advisory history/reference coverage. Existing goal 2 locks and frozen request identity own races; no lock spans a scan of the whole history. Re-read required binding at admission and retain the completed projection once.

**Use of the wait.** This composer adds no wait. During a pending run, a caller can inspect its immutable brief and evidence without changing the worktree. Preparing a future brief is ordinary synchronous work; a base, design or decision change invalidates that preparation. No speculative model or proof job is launched and no fleet boundary is emitted.

B5 removes the obsolete executable template check while retaining all unrelated template behavior. Existing admitted runs keep their frozen briefs/checks; new runs and corrections use the composer. A no-design build explicitly permitted by the existing design gate carries the supplied specification, marked as such, through the same path/reference/check composition and cannot claim accepted items. The composer does not turn a warning-only design gate into a refusal. Both build and correction `--brief` prose is retained supplementary text, never a replacement for the bound Decision, dispositions or owned Check; matching a preview is not required. A literal command quoted as historical evidence or specified as a mutation is not another Check; no shell-language classifier is introduced.

Deferred homes: scope/roster enforcement is in the follow-up brief; cheap-check budgets/assertion audits remain process-interventions; convergence remains the accepted-body/item publisher; the runner owns twice-size holds; fleet and runs-advance own boundaries, queues and next-unit scheduling.
