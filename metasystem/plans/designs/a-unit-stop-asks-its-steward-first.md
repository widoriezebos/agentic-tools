## 1. Problem

A unit stop goes straight to the person's phone. No steward sees it first. Three stops in fourteen hours were mechanical, and each asked Wido.

- 2026-11-02 10:15, question H5XMS…: "The steward acts: the build of unit U3a stopped at 10:15 (build: exit-1). ★ Continue … Stop here … If you do nothing: it waits". The Codex builder was killed when the provider token was invalidated by Wido's account switch at 09:55 (finding 490). The engine recorded build-failed, cause unclassified (run record, round 1). The right remedy was a retry of the build step with the worktree kept. The question closed at 10:20 as "work-revise: the retained build hold was continued" after the pane's correction; Wido's Continue came after it.
- 07:01, question 67K69…, still open: a-red U1a2a round 6, `proof:unit-check: exit-1`. The red was `TestPendingWaitFromChildShell` under load, a test the unit does not touch (journal 07:03). The only act offered is a correction round.
- 2026-11-01 21:27, question MS98PT…: a-red round 2, the same words and buttons (finding 479). Round 5 of the same unit was a gap after a no-op correction (journal 06:17).

By Wido's rules a stop inside the goal's box is the steward's decision. Decision 61 (the answer is the act); the 2026-10-24 rule (a read answers its own questions; a value question goes to the person in his name with the rejected alternative); the grant classes of the-steward-decides D2 (class 40: mechanical and recovery acts). The person is asked at the box's edge or on a value question.

What the code does:

- One composer asks. `syncBuildHolds` (`cmd/metasystem/intent_unit_questions.go:150-223`) runs in `unitOutcome`, the result of every unit verb (`cmd/metasystem/intent_work.go:1551`, the call `:1633`). For the newest held round it picks the act (`:169-193`) and calls `channel.Ask` at once (`:211-217`). `correctionChoice` makes it a contract question with Continue or Retry or Accept the size, and Stop here (`:228-259`).
- Only a failed round gets a prepared brief. `failureAsk` writes `correction-brief.md` for a failure whose act is a correction (`:208-209`, `:273-279`). A gap's act is the continuation (`:171-173`), which keeps `FILE` (`cmd/metasystem/intent_selection.go:470`, `:482`) (finding 4).
- The stop is the machine's record. `holdFailedStep` writes `Stop{Loop: "unit-build", Decision: "stop", Handoff: "stopped <cause>"}` with the class words "the failed step needs a person's decision" and the time from the runner's clock (`internal/launch/unit_step_failure.go:139-156`). The gap and the size hold write the same loop with "stopped (gap)" and "hold size" (`internal/launch/unit_build_outcome.go:60-75`).
- The size is not measured in production lines. `countBuildLines` sums all changed lines of the builder's diff (`unit_build_outcome.go:84-104`); a failed build is counted the same way, and a count that fails leaves zeros (`:107-120`). The test-path rule exists only in the branch package (`internal/goal/branch/attest.go:225-233`), which imports `internal/launch`, so launch cannot read it from there.
- The machine already retries some steps once. A failed step with cause environment, or a unit check at `proof.deadline`, is retried once (`unit_step_failure.go:92-97`); a provider refusal waits its probe interval first (`:101-119`). Anything else holds.
- An agent cannot act on a held round. `allowCorrection` admits a gap (`internal/launch/unit_stop.go:281-283`) and refuses every other stopped round (`:290-292`). A step retry needs a proven person (`unit_step_failure.go:178-180`) and cause environment or deadline (`:192-194`; the verb's gate `cmd/metasystem/intent_unit_review.go:48`).
- A person's reasoned revise skips the box. With `Person` set, `Revise` skips `allowCorrection`, the round limit, the counted cap, the divergence check and the decided-findings check (`internal/launch/unit_revise.go:178-198`). The goal's budget still holds at the launch: attempts, reserved job minutes and elapsed are checked for every unit launch (`internal/dispatch/unit_launch.go:44-73`).
- The grant cannot carry a unit act. The class table admits ordering, acks, `goal approve` of a flake fix, `work stop` and a stale `goal release` (`cmd/metasystem/attorney_class.go:30-71`). `work revise` and `work review` are in no class, so `decision record` refuses them (`cmd/metasystem/intent_decision.go:146-157`).
- A question asks once per stop. `AskOrFind` finds an earlier unit-stop question by goal, loop, subject, attempt and finding (`internal/channel/question.go:351-353`) over all questions, open and closed (`listQuestions`, `:166-180`). A successful act closes it through the act record (`internal/channel/unit_stop.go:95-163`); its writers are the step retry (`intent_unit_questions.go:155`), a continued hold (`:195`), a person's revise (`intent_selection.go:948-958`), a drop (`cmd/metasystem/intent_unit_drop.go:332`) and a clean read (`cmd/metasystem/intent_unit_stop.go:78`).
- A reply in a closed question's thread applies nothing. `contractReply` answers "This was settled …" or "Already done at HH:MM" (`internal/channel/inbox.go:616-650`). The inbox skips matching altogether when no question is open and none acted in the last day (`:490-498`); a-tap-is-answered-at-once D8 (its U3, not landed) changes that and puts the actor on the act record (finding 5).
- The steward's page has no unit stop. Its first thing is an ask, a red, the claim to continue, a goal to claim, or wait (`internal/steward/situation_compose.go:102-125`). A stopped unit shows only inside the goal's facts, as a stage line (`internal/steward/situation.go:564-585`, `cmd/metasystem/intent_selection.go:147-211`).
- The resident steward runner ticks on its own, apart from any session, and exits on its stop mark (`internal/steward/runner.go:338-346`).

## 2. Decisions

- D1 One table says whose a unit-build stop is. New `launch.StopRow(record UnitRunRecord) (UnitStopRow, bool)` in `internal/launch/unit_stop_row.go` reads the newest round of a run whose stop has loop `unit-build`. `UnitStopRow` holds the subject, the occurrence, the finding, the steward's acts, the person's reason, the round's production lines and the stop's time (`Stop.At`). The finding is the one `syncBuildHolds` names (`intent_unit_questions.go:160-189`). The rows are read from the record's fields, never from a goal or a case:

  | The stop | The steward's acts | The person's at once when |
  |---|---|---|
  | `stopped environment`, `stopped deadline` | retry the failed step in the same round; correction | the failed step has already run twice (the machine's retry, or an earlier retry) |
  | `stopped unclassified` | correction; retry where the retry gate admits the cause | the same |
  | `stopped own`, `other`, `main` | correction; retry where the gate admits the cause; for main, a wait whose trigger is main's fix | the same |
  | `stopped (gap)` | correction from the prepared brief | the round before was a gap too |
  | `hold size` | accept the size | |
  | every row | | the round's production lines are over 250, or unavailable; the rounds are spent: the run is at `MaxRounds`, or its counted rounds are at `CountedCap` (`internal/launch/coded.go:43-48`, `unit_run.go:1091-1100`) |

  The production lines are measured, not inferred. `countBuildLines` also sums the blocks of the same diff whose file is not a test path into `UnitRound.ProductionLines` (`*int64`). The test-path rule moves into launch as `launch.TestPath`, and the branch's `isExistingTestPath` (`attest.go:225-233`) keeps its blob check and calls it, so there is one rule. A count that fails, a failed build whose count failed (`unit_build_outcome.go:117-119`), and a round written by an older engine leave it nil: unavailable. The row then says "the production count is unavailable" and is the person's. The boundary applies to every act the steward is admitted to, not only to the size hold. "Where the retry gate admits the cause" is one predicate, `retryableCause`, taken out of `RetryFailedStep`'s gate (`unit_step_failure.go:192`) and read by both. Today it admits environment and deadline. a-red-is-classified-once widens it (its D4, U2); this row widens with it and owns no second gate. A value question the steward raises is its own `ask` disposition (D3). Rejected: a case per cause seen today (a credential row, a load row); the boundary on all changed lines (a unit with 240 lines of tests and 60 of production would go to the person for nothing).
- D2 The steward acts in his name, class recovery, inside the box. `attorneyActClass` (`attorney_class.go:30-38`) gains `work revise` and `work review`. Both take the flags `work`, `after`, `brief`, `reason`, `by`, `json`, `verbose`. Either is `recovery` only when the named unit's `StopRow` is a steward row, the act is one of the row's acts (a revise is a correction; a review with `--reason` on a failed step is a retry; on a size hold it accepts the size), and the occurrence has no person's choice (D5). The admitter reads the row through a new owner `unitRow`, whose default reads the run through `goalWork` (`cmd/metasystem/intent_selection.go:31`) at the checkout, as the steward's unit reader does (`cmd/metasystem/steward_seat.go:246-258`). The steward acts with `decision record --row unit-stop --subject S --occurrence O --dispose act --class recovery --text … --rejected … --undo "metasystem work stop G" -- VERB …`. The child runs as Wido's act under the grant (`cmd/metasystem/intent_planning.go:525`, the attorney seam `internal/humanauthority/authority.go:893`). It writes the act record that closes any question, as a person's act does today. The class rule carries the box because the person path skips it (`unit_revise.go:178-198`): the round limit, the counted cap and the production boundary are in `StopRow`. The divergence and decided-findings checks guard corrections after a read; a `unit-build` hold adds no read, so they have nothing new to judge, and a run whose reads diverge stops on the read side, which stays the person's (section 3). The goal's budget still refuses at the launch (`unit_launch.go:44-73`). No live grant, or a refused child, leaves the stop unhandled, and D3 asks the person. Rejected: a new verb for the steward (verb naming is Wido's; the existing verbs carry the acts); the agent's own authority (`allowCorrection` refuses it on every held round but a gap).
- D3 The stop is routed, not asked. `syncBuildHolds` keeps its act choice. Before `channel.Ask` (`intent_unit_questions.go:211-217`) it reads `StopRow`. A person's row asks at once, as today. A steward row asks the person only when the occurrence's newest disposition is an ask, or when it is unhandled 30 minutes after `Stop.At`. Handled is an act disposition whose child is confirmed, partial or unchanged (`humanauthority.ActHandled`, `internal/humanauthority/decisions.go:53-56`), or a wait whose time has not passed. Anything else is unhandled. The rule is one new function beside it, `humanauthority.OccurrenceState(decisions, row, subject, occurrence, now)`, over `ReadDecisions` of the board home (`decisions.go:74`). The lane's cannot-heal reading (the-steward-acts U4a, built inline in `internal/landing/plain/steward.go`) moves onto it, so the rule has one owner. The 30 minutes are the-steward-acts D3's. The clock's owner is the resident steward runner (`runner.go:338-346`): its tick runs a new pass, `TickConfig.UnitHolds`, on both tick paths beside the report pass (`internal/steward/tick.go:393`, `:445`). It is wired from the command layer as the unit stages are (`steward_seat.go:189`): an invocation at the checkout, the tick's clock as its command clock, `syncBuildHolds` for each held run of a goal this machine claims. Every unit verb's result (`unitOutcome`) runs the same gate. A dead seat session changes nothing: the runner still ticks. A stopped runner asks nothing until it runs again; its first tick then asks at once for every occurrence past its 30 minutes. No other watchdog. `AskOrFind`'s key keeps it to one question (R-129-ui). Rejected: ask at once and withdraw on a disposition (the phone still buzzes, which is what Wido objected to); a pass that decides by itself (the steward is the seat session, the-steward-acts D1; the runner only keeps the fail-safe).
- D4 The question says why it is his. When D3 asks, the question opens with the frame of finding 488 in the existing contract form. The decision line names the goal as "goal <id>", so it is never read as an actor. It says what stopped and why it is the person's: the steward did not decide by HH:MM; the steward's act was refused, with the verb's words; a retried step failed again; the production change is over 250 lines or unmeasured; or the rounds are spent. It then says what was spent: round n of `MaxRounds`, the production lines, and the goal's attempts and job minutes from `ProjectBudget` (`internal/dispatch/budget.go:276`). The options keep what yes buys and what no costs (`intent_unit_questions.go:237-257`); the starred option is the recommendation. Rejected: engineering first (finding 488: "I cannot judge by this text").
- D5 The person's say stands. The occurrence's human decision is the latest person's choice on its record: an outstanding `Answer` (matched or recorded), or the newest attempt with outcome chosen, acted, settled or accepted. A refused attempt is no choice. Stop here is such an attempt, outcome chosen, with `Answer` cleared (`poll.go:337-384`). The reader is `channel.UnitStopChoice(root, goal, key)` over `listQuestions`; it returns the label and its time, and the latest one stands across repeat taps and every later read. Any person's choice ends the steward's part for that occurrence: the class rule refuses the steward's act, and the page shows the row as answered, not as a first thing. The machine never undoes a person's answer (R-142-m1e). Rejected: reading `Answer` alone (a Stop here would then admit the steward); the steward re-deciding after a person's choice.
- D6 The steward sees the stop first. `SituationSources` gains `UnitStops`, read for the seat's goals (`seatGoals`, `situation.go:557-562`), and `Situation` keeps the rows with their `OccurrenceState`. An unhandled steward row of a seat goal is a first thing of kind `row` (the kind the-steward-acts U4a adds), after the lane's rows and before the asks. Its why line is "unit U of goal G stopped at HH:MM (class); asks the person at HH:MM unless disposed". Its command is composed by the command layer from the row's first act: `work review G --work U --reason TEXT` for a retry or a size hold, `work revise G --work U --after N --brief FILE --reason TEXT` for a correction, inside D2's `decision record` line. The other acts follow on the same line. Every correction row has a real brief: `syncBuildHolds` prepares `correction-brief.md` for a gap as for a failure (`writeCorrectionBrief`, `intent_unit_questions.go:291-304`, the builder's last words and its open items), so no command carries `FILE`. The wake line is `routed|unit-stop|S|O|state`, with a deadline at `Stop.At` plus 30 minutes. The-steward-acts U3a's stewardship selection then starts the seat session when no main holds the seat. A pane main reads the same page. Rejected: a board ask to the seat (a second record of one stop; the stop record is the route, as the lane's rows are in U4a); one command for every row (a size hold cannot be revised).
- D7 One occurrence identity. Row `unit-stop`; subject `goal/unit/run`, the stop's own subject (`unit_step_failure.go:141`); occurrence `<round>:<finding>`. These are the question's key fields (`UnitStopQuestion.sameKey`, `internal/channel/unit_stop.go:28-30`). The question, the notice, the disposition and the page's row name the same occurrence, and they live on one record, so a stop asks once. A new round is a new occurrence. Rejected: a key of its own (two identities for one stop would let a disposition miss its question).
- D8 Each act of the steward is one line in the stop's thread, and Stop here undoes it (decision 174). Wido's rule is "Telegram only for acts and delivered goals"; a steward's unit-stop act in his name is his act, class `act` of the channel page (a-persons-channel-carries-only-acts-and-deliveries D1 (a)). After a handled child of a `unit-stop` act disposition, `decision record` calls `channel.ActNotice(root, goal, key, line, undo)`. It finds the occurrence's record (D7) or writes one, closed by the act. It sets `Notice{Text, Undo, Posted}` and makes the one option "Stop here", whose act is the undo (`metasystem work stop G`). The line is the goal and unit, the steward's `--text`, and "Reply Stop here to undo.", for example "U3a stopped: the builder was killed by a credential change; retried in the same round. Reply Stop here to undo." The poll's delivery loop (`poll.go:96-117`) posts an unposted notice once: in the record's thread when it has one, else as a new message whose reference becomes the thread. A reply in that thread: an explicit Stop here is a choice on a closed record with a notice (`contractReply`, `inbox.go:616-650`), so `settleChoice` runs the undo and records the attempt (`poll.go:341-384`); `work stop` takes `--answer` as the contract acts do. A second Stop here meets the acted attempt and gets "Already done at HH:MM (Stop here)" (`inbox.go:624-625`). A stale Continue, or any other text, applies nothing and gets a-tap D8's settled reply. The undo is offered for one day, as `recentlyActed` counts a day (`inbox.go:655-657`); later the reply names `metasystem work stop G`. The steward's funnel (`notify.go:175-197`) is not involved. Rejected: no notice (revision 1's answer 1, overruled by decision 174); a notice without an undo (the person could not overrule in the thread, finding 1).

## 5. Questions answered in Wido's name (he overrules with one line)

1. The phone gets one line per steward act, in the stop's thread, with Stop here as the undo (decision 174, overruling revision 1's answer).
2. 30 minutes? The same as the-steward-acts D3: one number for both routes.
3. May the steward retry an own red? Only where the retry gate admits it (a-red U2), and only once per step. A flake is a-red U1's and never stops.
4. With no live recovery grant? The steward's act is refused before the child runs. The stop stays unhandled, and the person is asked at 30 minutes. That is the generic fail-safe.
5. The boundary is 250 measured production lines for every act the steward takes; past it, or unmeasured, it is a stop-line question, outside every class (the-steward-decides D2).
6. A Stop here a day after the act? It is answered with the command, not run: `work stop G` stops every running job of the goal, and a day later that is other work.
