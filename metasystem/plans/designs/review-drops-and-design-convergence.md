The size rule is at most **250 production lines per unit**, tests excluded, and **five units per goal**, counted across every design of that goal. The settings are `design.unit-lines-max=250` and `design.goal-units-max=5`, committed declarations an adopter can change. The parent plan's design row replaces the critical-only second round: at most four completed examinations; whole-page material must fall; a fixed class recurring in its section stops; zero accepts. At a stop, concrete changes become mandatory unit acceptance items and only unresolved choices and dependent sections move. A buildable remainder is accepted as well as split. Convergence does not ask a person to choose that exit, except when the person holds the applicable policy. It grants no build approval. That entire mechanism remains in the follow-up below; this goal does not write `Status: accepted`.

The impact-before-override rule, R-143-m1e, requires the owning verb to print and retain what passes, remaining risk, consequences for later work, and how to undo the act, with who, when, what and why, **before** its effect. It adds no confirmation step. A person's identity comes from the existing authority boundary, never from `--by` alone.
