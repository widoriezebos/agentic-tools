## 1. Intent

When the landing lane lands a goal whose holder declared it finished and said what it delivers, the goal concludes by itself once the work is live. The delivered sentence is the conclusion. The landed line and the conclusion are one ledger act. Nobody repeats `work land` to record the landing, and nobody types `goal done`. A person can reopen, and can always conclude by hand.

## 2. Problem

The lane lands. Then a seat must come back twice: once to record the landing, once to conclude.

## 3. Decisions

- **D1 The holder's declaration, recorded at hand-in, and only from the holder.** `plain.Line` and `plain.Entry` gain `Holder string` (`json:"holder,omitempty"`) and `HolderClaimedAt string` (`json:"holderClaimedAt,omitempty"`), beside `WholeBy` (`queue.go:63`, `:95`), copied where a hand-in starts an entry (`queue.go:250`, `:439`). `handIn` (`landing_plain.go:202`) records them, the claim's pair and its `Claimed.At`, only when all three hold:
  - Authority. The caller is the session holding the claim (the same test as `--whole`: `inv.heldGoals` contains the goal, `intent_delivery.go:1907-1920`), or a person who declared the end with `--whole --by` and proof (`inv.wholeBy` set by `actingAs`, `:1921-1923`). Any other caller's hand-in, and its sentence, records no declaration: it lands as today and the answer says it will not conclude by itself.
  - Eligibility, explicit. The hand-in passed the end check and carries built work: it is not a records hand-in, and `handLandingSubject` returned a unit count above zero (`intent_delivery.go:2609-2612`), which it does only after a complete Units table, a `--last` unit or `--whole`. `landGoalRoute` keeps that count (`:2190`, today discarded as `_`) and passes it to `handIn` (`:2234`). A planning landing (count zero) is lawful and never concludes, `--whole` or not.
  - A claim. The goal is claimed when the hand-in is written.
  Lines from older engines have no `Holder`: they never conclude by themselves. That is the mixed-version boundary.
  Rejected: "who hands in does not matter" (revision 1). A caller who does not hold the claim would turn its own sentence into the holder's declaration (Astra finding 2). Rejected: derive completion from "not records" (revision 1). A planning landing would conclude an unfinished goal (Astra finding 3). Rejected: a new `--conclude` flag. The holder already declares the end by handing in finished work.

- **D2 The lane concludes, in one ledger act, when the declared commit is live.** One pass, `concludeLanded`, in `metasystem/cmd/metasystem/landing_conclude.go`, run in the lane installation, in two halves.
  - Selection (part U1b1). From `plain.Entries` (`queue.go:206-212`), per goal, only the newest code hand-in (not `Records`), as `intent_landing_push.go:80-86` keys it. It qualifies when it is not returned or superseded, has `Holder`, `HolderClaimedAt` and `Delivered` (after any `Say`), and the goal is live and claimed by exactly that pair with exactly that `Claimed.At`. A superseded hand-in never concludes, whatever its words; a replacement without words concludes nothing. The selection reads the lane installation's projection fresh (`projectionWithFetch(true)`, `intent_goals.go:35`).
  - Live (part U1b2). The pass checks containment against the selected hand-in's own commit, never a ledger snapshot: main contains `entry.SHA` (`plain.ContainedIn`, against the pushed or the deployed commit), and when the project declares a deploy, the active deploy contains `entry.SHA` and no newer failure does. `goalDeployReady` is split at `intent_goals.go:1113`: the lookup stays, and its tail (`:1116-1141`) becomes `deployReadyAt(id, sha)`, which the seat's check and the lane's pass both call. One wording: "on main but not deployed yet", "its deploy failed". With no deploy contract main suffices (`:1091-1093`).
  - When. At the end of `landing push` (after `deployAfterPush`, `intent_landing_push.go:234`), and at the end of every `deploy now` whose outcome is deployed or current (`runIntentDeployNow`, `intent_deploy.go:436-441`). Both are acts of the landing.
  - Dispatch (part U1b2). Through the sync owner every `goal done` uses (`intent_goals.go:1048-1052`): `done --root <lane installation> --id G --conclude <Delivered> --lineage landing-agent --lane-holder <pair> --lane-claimed-at <At>`. The done case (`goalsync_mutations.go:1951`) reads the two flags into `VerbRequest.LaneHolder Actor` and `VerbRequest.LaneClaimedAt string` (`verbs.go:263`). So the sweep, the released card and the retro debt run as for any conclusion.
  - The act (part U1a). In `doneRequest` (`verbs.go:2828`), with `LaneHolder` set:
    - refused unless the live goal is claimed by `LaneHolder` with `Claimed.At == LaneClaimedAt`: "goal G's claim is not the one its hand-in spoke for; the lane does not conclude it". This is the goal's own state: a release, a takeover, a done or a reopen ends that claim. A history row, of this goal or another's priority move, plays no part;
    - an archived done goal is the existing repeat, nothing written (`:2838-2848`);
    - the landed row is written in the same transaction when `landedSinceLanding` is false (`landgate.go:215-222`), reason "landed under the landing lane's push; " plus `LandedUnder` (`landgate.go:557-568`);
    - the two holder-only rows are passed: another pair's claim (`verbs.go:2889-2894`) and a human-origin goal (`:2901-2906`). The parked or split row (`:2895-2900`) is unreachable: a parked goal is not claimed, so the claim check refuses first. Read items, obligations, carry words and blockers refuse as for the seat (`:2853-2882`, `:2907-2922`);
    - `Conclude` is the delivered sentence (`:2929`).
  - Who. The lane records the conclusion as itself, `machine+landing-agent`, never as the holder, never as the registrant. The done row's reason names the holder ("concluded by the landing lane for ui+steward-seat"). The displacement marker (`verbs.go:2925-2927`) stays; Astra, from source: it drives the holder's automatic acknowledgment on a later publication, not a notice (`verbs.go:712-798`).
  - The answer is the confirmed ledger state. After the sync call the pass reads the goal back. Done: it is concluded, whatever error came with it; the pass closes the goal's unit-stop questions with the conclusion, as `runIntentDone` does (`intent_goals.go:1058-1068`), and reports a sweep or retro failure on its own line ("goal G is concluded; its branch was not swept: ...; goal done G repeats the sweep"), left to the sweep's owner. Live: not concluded; one line names the refusal and the seat's path, and the next push or deploy tries again. A done goal is never selected again.
  Rejected: the agent runs `goal done` after landing, as today. It asks the seat to watch main and come back twice; the evidence is that it does so by hand. Rejected: the steward concludes at its tick. A tick is not an act of the landing; it concludes from a reading, later, under an identity that landed nothing. Rejected: fix the seat's Stop step (write `Landing` at hand-in, let the lane write the landed line, let the holder's next Stop conclude). It still needs a seat turn after the landing, and two writers share one goal's end. Rejected: conclude at the push and ignore the deploy (the-second-half-is-the-seats D8: a goal would read done while its change is not live). Rejected: call `goalDeployReady` as it is (revision 1). A snapshot that has not fetched the landing passes the check (Astra finding 1). Rejected: a done row after the hand-in time as "already concluded" (revision 1). Another goal's conclusion writes such rows (Astra finding 5).

- **D3 Two acts, two messages, as decision 19b keeps them.** The lane's landing post stays as it is: at the push, the delivered sentences (`landedMessage`, `intent_landing_push.go:155-171`; `postLanded`, `metasystem/cmd/metasystem/channel_verbs.go:68-75`). The conclusion is the second act. Its "goal is done" line is a-delivered-goal-and-a-needed-act-reach-the-person's notice on every transition to done, whoever concluded (decision 19). This page posts nothing new and suppresses nothing. The pending decision for Wido (section 4) does not touch this.
  Rejected: one message at the conclusion, the push's post left out (revision 1). It reverses decision 19b (Astra finding 7). Rejected: a conclusion post of this page's own. Its owner exists.

- **D4 No sentence: the hand-in says so once, and the goal lands without concluding.** A code hand-in by the holder without `--delivered` answers, beside today's channel words (`deliveredHint`, `intent_delivery.go:2092-2105`): "goal G at X handed to the lane; it will not be concluded when it lands, because no sentence says what it delivers", next `metasystem work land G --delivered "WHAT IT DELIVERS, IN ONE PLAIN SENTENCE"`. That repeat, while the hand-in waits, adds the sentence (`plain.Say`), and the goal concludes when it is live. `Say` takes the caller's clock (`queue.go:447`, `:455`). Once it landed without one, the seat concludes as today. No channel question: the sentence is the holder's to write. A sentence `goal.Done` refuses (`verbs.go:2440-2453`, left-over work without a `goal:` link) is refused at the conclusion and the seat concludes by hand; Astra, from source, that suffices.
  Rejected: refuse a hand-in without a sentence (a person's hand-in is never refused for words, R-142-m1e). Rejected: the lane writes a sentence (a machine summary is not the holder's word).

- **D5 Reopen and repeats.** Reopen is `goal reopen G --next TEXT`, unchanged; concluding by hand, by a person or the seat, is unchanged. After a reopen, automatic completion is deferred: the old declaration is bound to the claim that the done ended (D2), and land-once refuses a second code hand-in of a landed goal (`intent_delivery.go:2185-2189`), so no new declaration can be written. A later goal that lifts land-once decides automatic completion after a reopen. Repeats change nothing and say so: the pass on a done goal (`verbs.go:2838-2848`); `goal done G` after the lane concluded (the same); `work land G` on a goal the lane concluded answers "goal G at X landed on main and is concluded: <conclusion>", outcome unchanged, no next step (`landing_plain.go:108-112`), and `noteLanded` writes no landed line for a done goal (`landing_gate.go:218-227`).
  Rejected: promise a second automatic conclusion after a reopen (revision 1; land-once forbids its hand-in, Astra finding 8). Rejected: a `goal undo-done` verb.

- **D6 The sweep is handed off, not owned.** The lane's conclusion is a `goal.Done` through the done case, with the same `SweepBranch` (`goalsync_mutations.go:1955-1987`). A sweep or retro failure after a confirmed conclusion is a separate line (D2) and the conclusion stands. Whatever a-done-goals-branch-is-swept changes in that closure and in `goal done`'s repeat applies here unchanged.
  Rejected: a lane-side sweep closure (a second copy the sweep goal is about to change).

- **D7 The recovery limit, stated.** A lane conclusion interrupted between its publication intent and its confirmation is replayed by recovery as a plain done (`verbs.go:2972-2980`, `recover.go:591-593`) without `LaneHolder`. That replay is refused as another pair's conclusion (`verbs.go:2889-2894`): the goal stays live and unchanged, and the next push or deploy concludes it afresh. Safe, not complete. When it is fixed, `doneArgs` carries the holder pair and claim time and replay rebuilds `LaneHolder` through the same journal machinery. Not built here.

## 4. Decision for Wido

This changes who concludes a goal. Decision 38 says the second half of a seat-claimed goal is the seat's: it hands in whole and it concludes. The recommendation: the seat still declares the end, at hand-in, by handing in finished work with its sentence, and only the seat holding the claim, or a person with his proof, can make that declaration; the lane, the only party present when the work goes live, carries it out as its own recorded act, `machine+landing-agent`, with the holder named in the reason, and refuses when the claim it spoke for has ended. The person's power is unchanged: he can conclude, withhold the sentence, or reopen at any time. Rejected: the lane acts as the person who registered it (`lane.Record.RegisteredBy`, `metasystem/internal/landing/lane/lane.go:47-56`): a person's act without his proof, and an act is recorded under who made it (`landgate.go:453-454`). Rejected: the lane writes as the holder's pair, for the same reason. Rejected: keep the seat concluding; today it does so by hand, twice per goal. a1 decided (c) in Wido's name: the lane records the conclusion as itself. The question left for a1 under Wido's standing rule is the shift from decision 38's "the seat concludes" to "the seat declares, the lane concludes"; it does not reopen decision 19b. Wido overrules.

## 5. What does not change

- The end checks of a hand-in (`intent_delivery.go:2532-2612`), `--whole`, `WholeBy`, land-once.
- The lane's merge, proof and push; its landing post; the deploy runner and its records.
- A person's `goal done G --reason TEXT --by NAME`; the seat's `goal done G` and its deploy wait (now through `deployReadyAt`, same words).
- The holder's Stop step and the seat ladder, which stay for `--queue-only` landings.
- `RecordLanded` for the seat's repeat.
- The parked or split row: concluding such a goal stays a person's act.
