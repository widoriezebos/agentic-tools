## Step 1

After U1 (the restart reader, the tick's settle and the tick's words): a tick that holds the seat names the one record that holds it and the one command that clears it, in plain English; a missing record, or a job record the reaper cannot close, never turns the tick degraded; the session-main health row shows the hold instead of "no seat step is due"; the hold question offers a Revive button only where revive acts, and that button says what it forgets, what it permits and how to undo it. After U2 (revive closes the restart episode and starts): `machine revive SEAT` from any of the eight states below either starts the seat (or reports the session that already runs) or refuses with exactly the command the tick names, writing nothing; after it the tick never names revive for the same state; the cycle test passes on all eight. U3 (a launch without a process ends with its starter) is independent: a seat launch whose starter died before any process was recorded fails by itself, so the tick, `work status` and revive agree.

## 4. Why no state cycles

After U1 and U2 the tick names revive only on the seat path: in rows 1, 3a, 3b and 5a and for the restart policy and unknown seat-start holds, and there only after `PlanSeat` selected (`metasystem/internal/steward/seat_start.go:484-489`). It no longer names revive in rows 3c, 4, 6c, 7a and 7b, nor anywhere on the continuation path, where the claim is another lineage's and the remedy is that claim's own line. In each row that names revive, revive's checks (D5 steps 3 to 6) refuse only on a seat launch that runs or starts, a census in doubt or not free, a continuation that runs or starts, or an unreadable record; in each of those the tick does not name revive either (it reports the run, the start, the census, `work stop`, or the file), because both read one reader and one census. The selection (step 7) cannot refuse in those rows: it is the tick's own `PlanSeat` over the same seat world with the restart caps lifted, so it selects at least what the tick selected, and the busy check is the same reader the tick reads first (a busy seat is in-flight work, `metasystem/internal/steward/openwork.go:69-73`, which never reaches the restart judgment). Past them revive closes the episode together with the start (D3), after which no restart hold can name revive (nothing named, no history, a zero count). A refused revive writes nothing, so it cannot make a new state: the next tick reads what it read before, and its line is the refusal's line, with the same command. One table-driven test checks this for every row (section 6).
