## 1. The problem

On 2026-09-14 seat m1b's Bash tool ran a descendants walk that never ends under zsh; the tool's timeout moved it to the background and it burned 65 to 70 percent of a core for twelve hours (`records/misc/leaked-processes-diagnosis-2026-09-15.md:72-80`, class C). The captured row (`records/misc/leaked-processes-evidence-2026-09-15.txt:3`) shows pid 53724 with a live parent 90295, 11 h 56 min of its own CPU at 68.6 percent, and the argv `/bin/zsh -c source /Users/wido/.claude/shell-snapshots/snapshot-zsh-...sh 2>/dev/null || true && ...`. Each pass forked `pgrep`, `tr` and a command substitution, so at any instant it may have had a child; the CPU was the shell's own.

Critique r1 of this goal (`artifacts/reports/bss-critique-r1.md`, verdict revise, seven material findings) is folded in section 11.

## 2. What the engine can prove

**The kernel keeps the CPU proof.** Every process carries a monotone accumulator of the CPU its own threads consumed; a child's time is charged to the child and folded into the parent's separate children figure only at exit. Two readings of the shell's own accumulator, any time apart, give the exact CPU the shell itself burned over the interval. A parent blocked in `wait` on a long `go test` or `shasum` gains almost no self time.

**The shell carries its own owner.** Live fact, this machine, 2026-09-16 16:10: this delegate's tool shell is `/bin/zsh -c source /Users/wido/.claude/shell-snapshots/snapshot-zsh-1789567609586-w0q3ue.sh ...`, parent 37773 (`claude -p ... --name design-bss-r2`), and its environment carries `CLAUDE_CODE_SESSION_ID=638767a1-1133-4958-82d0-4c0f033aaf62` and `CLAUDE_PID=37773`. The checkout's announcement `artifacts/agents/mains/638767a1-...-37773.json` has `runtimeSession` equal to that id, `pid` 37773 and `pidStartedAt` 1789567600 (whole seconds, the announcement's own precision, `internal/lease/classify.go:22-45`). The identity package already reads a process's environment: `KernelProber.Probe` fills `Exact.Environ` and `EnvironKnown` on Darwin through `kern.procargs2` (`internal/identity/identity_darwin.go:86-102,112-113`) and on Linux through `/proc/<pid>/environ` (`identity_linux.go:98-110,118-132`; fields at `identity.go:40,49`); it survives the parent's death. `identity.ProcessCwd` reads the working directory natively (`internal/identity/enumerate_darwin.go:76`). An announcement is removed only by `session end` (`internal/lease/verbs.go:276-292`).

## 3. Rules

Package: new `internal/busyshell` (with `testmain_test.go`). Injected seams, per R-104-m1e: `Clock`, `Sleeper`, `Prober` (`identity.Prober`, `identity.go:174-181`), `CPUReader`, `Children` (pid lister plus parent reader), `Announcements`, `LockOwner` and `Signal` (`identity.SignalFunc`, `ref.go:129`). Every witness runs on these fakes; only P2 reads a real process, its own.

- **P1 (shape).** A process is a seat tool shell when argv[0]'s base is `zsh`, `bash` or `sh`, argv[1] is `-c`, argv[2] begins with `source ` followed by a path whose parent directory is `<home>/.claude/shell-snapshots/`, and its environment is known and carries `CLAUDE_CODE_SESSION_ID` and a numeric `CLAUDE_PID`. An unreadable argv or environment (`identity.go:42-49`) never matches. A process whose parent is alive and is not `CLAUDE_PID` is a subshell or another runtime's child, not a candidate; a candidate's parent is `CLAUDE_PID`, 1, or dead. A shell that `exec`s its last command leaves the shape and is dropped. Witness: `TestToolShellShape` (the evidence argv with the session environment matches; a live parent other than `CLAUDE_PID`, a `/bin/sh -c` hook, a `zsh -c` without the prefix, an argv-unknown and an environment-unknown row do not). Mutation: drop the parent-directory check and the hook row matches; drop the environment requirement and the environment-unknown row matches.
- **P2 (the CPU reader).** `identity.CPUTime(pid) (time.Duration, bool)`. Darwin: `SYS_proc_info` (336), `PROC_INFO_CALL_PIDINFO` (2), flavor `PROC_PIDTASKINFO` (4), a 96 byte buffer with the return size checked equal to 96; `pti_total_user` at offset 16 plus `pti_total_system` at offset 24, mach absolute units, divided by `hw.tbfrequency` read once through `unix.SysctlUint64` (sysctl is already used at `enumerate_darwin.go:47,116`; live value 24000000). Linux: fields 14 and 15 of `/proc/<pid>/stat` after the last `)`, the split `parseProcStat` uses (`identity_linux.go:140-146`), over `userHZ` (`:44-48`). Elsewhere and on any error: `false`. Witnesses: `TestCPUTimeOfSelfAgreesWithRusage` (own pid against `Getrusage(RUSAGE_SELF)` read at the same instant, within one second, no waiting); `TestCPUTimeIsMonotoneAcrossASpin` (spin until own rusage shows 100 ms more, then the reader shows at least 50 ms more; the loop ends on accrued CPU, never on wall time, so load lengthens it and cannot fail it); `TestCPUTimeOfAGonePidIsUnknown` (pid 2147483647). Mutations: wrong timebase and the rusage witness fails by a factor of 24; offset 8 and the spin witness fails. Revision 1's sleeping-child witness is dropped: it needed a real child.
- **P3 (owner).** At a candidate's first sighting the pass reads `S` = `CLAUDE_CODE_SESSION_ID` and `M` = `CLAUDE_PID` from its environment and looks for an announcement in this checkout's `artifacts/agents/mains/` (reader `readAnnouncements`, `classify.go:157-158`, exported as `lease.ReadAnnouncements` in unit 2) with `EffectiveRuntimeSession() == S` and `Pid == M`. A match writes the owner into the entry once: `runtimeSession` S, `mainId`, `ownerRoot` (the acting root), provenance `environment+announcement`; later sightings never overwrite it, so the entry keeps its owner when the main dies or the announcement is removed at `session end`. No match makes the candidate `unowned`: counted in the pass result and `steward status`, never stored, never signalled. Witness: `TestOwnerFromEnvironmentAndAnnouncement` (fixture mains directory, fake prober: `sessionId="session-123"` with `runtimeSession="real-session"` resolves to `real-session`; an announcement removed after the first sighting leaves the owner in place; a session with no announcement here is unowned). Mutation: read `SessionId` and the placeholder row records `session-123`; resolve afresh each tick and the removed-announcement row loses its owner.
- **P4 (the registry).** One JSON document per checkout, `artifacts/agents/steward/busy-shells.json`, written through `atomicfile.WriteText` (`internal/atomicfile/atomicfile.go:76`), read, reduced and replaced inside the tick, which holds the tick arbitration lock (`internal/steward/tick.go:110-114`); no second lock. Key: the full `identity.Ref` (`identity.go:25-31`), so a reused pid is a new entry. Fields: `ref`, `owner{runtimeSession,mainId,ownerRoot,provenance}`, `command` (argv[2] whole, capped at 4096 bytes, `truncated` flag), `cwd`, `startedAt`, `firstSeen`, `sightings[{at,cpu}]`, `state` (`sighted`, `kept-live-child`, `kept-cpu-fell`, `kept-protected`, `ended`, `survived`, `gone`), `lastAction{at,outcome,detail}`. Reduce: append this tick's sighting; drop sightings older than twice the bound, never the newest; a Dead identity becomes `gone` and is dropped next tick; an entry unseen for twice the bound is dropped; a failed reading appends nothing; an unreadable file becomes an empty registry with one log line, never a failed tick. Witnesses: `TestRegistryRetainsTheBaselineAcrossTicks` (seven ten-minute sightings on an artificial clock retain the four spanning the bound), `TestRegistryRecoversFromACorruptFile`, `TestRegistryKeysOnExactIdentity`, `TestRegistryKeepsTheWholeCommandAndCwd` (3000 bytes whole; 5000 capped and flagged). Mutation: keep the last two sightings and the baseline witness fails; cap at 200 bytes and the command witness fails.
- **P5 (the predicate).** Busy when retained sightings i and n (n newest) exist with `at_n - at_i >= bound` and `cpu_n - cpu_i >= fraction × (at_n - at_i)`; bound 30 minutes, fraction 25 percent. The CPU is the shell's own by the kernel's accounting, so a shell whose work is in its children cannot satisfy it. Witness: `TestBusyPredicateOverSightings`: 31 minutes at 100 percent, busy; 40 minutes at 0.5 percent beside a hot child, not busy; 20 minutes at 100 percent, not busy; idle 3 hours then busy 40 minutes, busy at the first qualifying pair; readings that go down never qualify. Mutation: compare against the oldest sighting only and the idle-then-busy row fails; drop the span check and the 20 minute row fails.
- **P6 (the guard, beside each signal).** Immediately before TERM, and again before KILL, in order: (a) the exact identity is re-proved by the guard's own probe (a mismatch records `gone`; `identity.SignalExact`, `ref.go:132-151`, proves it once more inside the send); (b) a fresh CPU reading is appended and P5 re-evaluated over the retained baseline; if it no longer holds the action is refused, state `kept-cpu-fell`; (c) P11's protected-pid check; (d) children: the pass lists every pid whose parent is the shell (`identity.AllPids`, `enumerate_darwin.go:25`, plus `ParentPid`, `:112-116`); an empty list sends the signal in that same step; a non-empty list sleeps `guard-seconds` (default 2) through `Sleeper` and observes again, up to `guard-attempts` (default 5) observations; if every observation shows a child the action is refused this tick, state `kept-live-child`, the last child's pid and argv head logged, one notice (P7). A child is never signalled; a refusal does not reset the baseline. The incident loop's children live milliseconds each, so a childless observation comes within a few attempts; a persistent, new or replaced child shows at every observation and the shell is kept. Witnesses: `TestGuardRefusesAnyChildAtTheFinalObservation` (fake children rows: empty then a new `go test` child, refused; a persistent child, refused; a different identity each observation, refused; children at one and two and none at three, signalled at three after exactly two sleeper calls); `TestGuardReprovesThePredicateBesideEachSignal` (a fresh reading below the fraction: `kept-cpu-fell`, zero signals); `TestGuardRunsBeforeKillToo` (the second guard's refusal leaves the KILL unsent). Mutations: refuse only a child seen twice and the new-child row proceeds; skip (b) and the fallen-CPU row signals; drop the KILL-side guard and the third witness sees a KILL.
- **P7 (the action).** For a busy entry that passed the guard: TERM to the pid alone, never to a group (the shell's group can be its main's), through `identity.SignalExact(prober, ref, TERM, signal)` (landed, 400218350); poll `AliveRef` (`identity.go:191`) every `Poll` (250 ms) through `Sleeper` until `Clock` shows `TermGrace` (5 s) elapsed; if still Alive, the guard again, then KILL, then `KillGrace` (1 s). Outcome `ended`, `survived` (Alive after KILL: a defect, health dead, P8) or `gone` (mismatch at any step: nothing sent). Every outcome appends a JSON line to `artifacts/agents/steward/busy-shells.log` with the whole entry, span, self CPU seconds and outcome, and queues `steward.QueueNotification(ownerRoot, {Nonce: "busy-shell-<pid>-<startMicro>-<outcome>", Message})` (`internal/steward/intervene.go:320-348`). Wording is per outcome. `ended`: "the steward ended a busy seat shell of session <S>: pid N burned M CPU seconds over T minutes with no child; cwd <cwd>; command: <first 200 characters>; whole record: metasystem steward status --repo <ownerRoot>". `survived`: "did not die after KILL and is still running; a human must look". `gone`: "was gone before the steward acted; nothing was sent". `kept-live-child`: "kept because a child was present: <pid, argv head>". Delivery is unchanged: the owner checkout's channel on the next `DeliverPending` (`internal/steward/notify.go:115`; the fixture log for fixture enrollments, `notify.go:41-49`), `steward pending` and `steward status` (`cmd/metasystem/steward_verbs.go:778,803`), and the Stop hook's pending line (`supervision-hook.sh:2507-2508`), read by every session stopping on the owner checkout. Resume: the record's `command` and `cwd` let the owner re-issue the command by hand or abandon it; nothing is resumed automatically. Witness: `TestActionSignalsByIdentityLogsAndNotifies` (all seams fake; two enrolled checkouts in one fixture home: the sequence is guard, TERM, poll, guard, KILL; the pending file exists under the owner checkout only; each outcome has its own wording; the poll loop makes exactly `TermGrace/Poll` sleeper calls with the prober Alive throughout). Mutations: signal the negative pgid and the target assertion fails; queue on the acting root when it differs from `ownerRoot` and the two-checkout assertion fails; take the deadline from `time.Now` and the sleeper-count assertion fails, because the fake sleeper returns at once and the loop runs five real seconds.
- **P8 (the health role).** `busy-shells` joins `healthRoleOrder` and `KnownHealthRole` (`internal/steward/health.go:66-92`): dead when any entry is `survived`, or `kept-live-child` or `kept-cpu-fell` with a first refusal older than twice the bound; unknown when the registry is unreadable; alive otherwise. The verdict's `Reason` (`RoleVerdict`, `health.go:99-109`) always lists the entries acted on within retention ("ended pid N of session S at T"), so the line carries the event when the role is alive; `RoleVerdict.Line()` prints it (`health.go:263-276`), `health.json` carries it (`health.go:219-220`) and the Stop report writes one line per role (`internal/report/stoppresentation.go:1287-1291`). The preview refuses unknown fields (`stoppresentation.go:275`), so the role lands in steward and hook together. Witness: `TestBusyShellsHealthRole` (empty: alive; one `ended` yesterday: alive, reason names it; `kept-live-child` for 90 minutes: dead; `survived`: dead). Mutation: drop the survived branch and a shell that outlived KILL reads alive; clear the reason on alive and the ended row loses the event.
- **P9 (wiring).** `RunTick` (`tick.go:104`) runs the pass after `ReapContinuations` (`tick.go:170`) and before the decision; a pass error is carried in `TickResult.BusyShells` (`tick.go:46-58`) and the tick log, never through `degradedTick` (`tick.go:323`): a leak census must not stop supervision. The pass enumerates the table itself (`identity.AllPids` and `KernelProber.Probe`, as `census.EnumerateProcesses` does, `internal/census/production.go:45-76`) and reads parent and CPU only for P1 matches. Witness: `TestTickRunsTheBusyShellPassAndSurvivesItsFailure` (registry path unwritable: the tick completes with the error in its result, no degraded verdict). Mutation: return the error and the witness sees a degraded tick.
- **P10 (knobs and the fixture scale).** `metasystem.steward.busy-shell-minutes` (git config, read like `TickSeconds`, `runner.go:97-98`; default 30; outside 5 to 1440 refused with a logged reason), `metasystem.steward.busy-shell-guard-seconds` (default 2, 1 to 120), `metasystem.steward.busy-shell-guard-attempts` (default 5, 1 to 10). In a fixture-enrolled checkout only (`EnrollmentFixture`, as `notify.go:49` checks) the bound, `TermGrace` and `KillGrace` are multiplied by `METASYSTEM_FIXTURE_CAP_SCALE_MILLI/1000`; guard interval and attempts are never scaled; a production root ignores the variable, unlike `runnerStopWait` (`runner.go:901-915`), which is not copied. Witnesses: `TestBusyShellKnobsValidate`, `TestBusyShellScaleAppliesOnlyToFixtureRoots`. Mutation: scale unconditionally and the production row shrinks its bound.
- **P11 (authority).** A checkout's pass signals only an entry whose `ownerRoot` is the acting root: the seat that started the shell is the seat whose engine ends it; no checkout ends another's shell (R-79-m2). `unowned` candidates are counted, never signalled. Protected pids: `LockOwner` reads `/tmp/metasystem-testrun-lock/owner` when present; every decimal token naming a live pid is protected; a candidate that is a protected pid, or whose ancestor chain (`ParentPid` up to 1, at most 64 hops) contains one, is refused with state `kept-protected` and no signal; a protected descendant is refused by P6(d). Witness: `TestPassNeverSignalsAForeignOrProtectedShell` (two fixture checkouts in one home: a busy entry owned by B is not stored by A's pass; an owner file naming the candidate's pid, then its parent's, refuses with zero fake-signal calls; an owner file with no live pid protects nothing). Mutation: act regardless of `ownerRoot` and the first row sees a signal; skip the ancestor walk and the parent row signals.

## 7. Proof of DONE

The Go witnesses prove each rule on artificial clocks and fake readings; the end-to-end proof runs real shells once, by the seat, under the test-run lock (Wido, 2026-09-12: artificial clocks in tests, never load-fragile tests; wiring proofs wait for facts on a tenfold bound).

`scripts/agents/busy-shell-fixtures.sh` arms a fixture-enrolled root with `metasystem.steward.tick-seconds=5` and `METASYSTEM_FIXTURE_CAP_SCALE_MILLI=10` (bound 18 s, TermGrace 50 ms, KillGrace 10 ms), announces the bed's shell as the root's main with a session id (`lease announce`, `lease associate-session`, `cmd/metasystem/main.go:602-603`), exports `CLAUDE_CODE_SESSION_ID` and `CLAUDE_PID` into the planted shells, writes a snapshot under a fixture `HOME/.claude/shell-snapshots/`, and starts three tool-shaped children, each recorded through m1c's bed helpers, which reap them at the end and on abort:

1. the busy loop, the incident's own shape bounded by a deadline: `zsh -c 'source <snapshot> 2>/dev/null || true && pids=" "; end=$((SECONDS+600)); while [ -n "$pids" ] && (( SECONDS < end )); do next=""; for p in $pids; do c=$(pgrep -P $p | tr "\n" " "); next="$next $c"; done; pids=$(echo $next); done'`;
2. the long checksum: `zsh -c 'source ... && shasum -a 256 < <fifo>'`, the bed feeding zeros into the FIFO in one-megabyte writes until step (c), then closing it, so the checksum is alive with its child working exactly as long as the proof needs and ends normally;
