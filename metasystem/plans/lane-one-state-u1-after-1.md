A coherent observation without the installation lock (LOS-11 rounds 6-7, LOS-13 round 7): the shadow's inputs are ONE list in one place, `shadowInputs(home) []string` — every raw record file the projection reads (queue.jsonl, batch.json, running.json, results.jsonl, pushes.jsonl, stops.jsonl, agent-step.json, agent-idle.json, the fix records, the question files, the engine stamp, and in the host dir landing-lane.json, landing-lane-paused.json, landing-lane-resumed.json) — and `recordsFingerprint(home) string` hashes each input's content (they are small) in that order. A capture is `Captured{Shadow *state.Record; Absent bool; Version int; Fingerprint string}`. An observation is COHERENT when the fingerprint taken before `BuildView` equals the fingerprint taken after `observe` returns (no raw record changed under the read) and the shadow version did not change; only a coherent observation is compared (health) or published (refresh). An incoherent one is re-observed, at most three times; after that health prints one row "the lane's records kept changing during this read (3 tries); compared again on the next call" and the refresh appends one `observation-unstable` event and publishes nothing. Publication checks freshness under `state.lock`: `Replace(dir, now, next, by, expectVersion, expectFingerprint)` re-reads the record's version and recomputes the fingerprint and refuses (a typed `*Stale`, no write, no event) when either moved, so an older refresh can never overwrite a newer record (LOS-13); the caller re-observes. Every write of the record increments `Version`. Tests: (i) refresh A observes paused, stalls; a resume removes the pause; refresh B publishes running; A's publication is refused Stale and A re-observes running — the record never says paused again; (ii) an unpause between BuildView and the status read (the pause record removed with the shadow NOT yet refreshed by the verb) → the fingerprints differ, the observation is re-taken, no drift row, no mixed facts (a stopped owner next to Paused=false never reaches a comparison); (iii) a lane with nothing moving → one observation, coherent, compared.
