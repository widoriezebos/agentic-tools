## Vision

In the paper's own words (`paper/index.md` at the repository root): "Engineering after the shift in software: governing its production, not building it" — "moving human attention from building the application to building the system that builds the application (and about refusing to carry human ceremony into a workforce that does not need it)." The principles come first: "serve revisable intent, prefer evidence to trust, keep durable records, enforce important rules, spend in proportion to risk and reserve named decisions for humans." What the machinery must keep, in the concepts document's words (`docs/concepts.md`): "the properties a careful human team would keep: provable identity, single ownership of every truth, spending that cannot run away, evidence that cannot be quietly lost, and human authority at exactly the boundaries where judgment is sovereign."

## Boundaries

From the paper: "I consider neither the software nor this paper 'finished'" — where the paper "went beyond what the software currently does", it "shows the direction to take the software next", and the software's discoveries revise the paper in turn. "Humans govern values and exceptions through clearly bounded authority"; the machinery does not decide which human value prevails. And self-application "cannot show by itself that the approach works beyond the system that ran it."
