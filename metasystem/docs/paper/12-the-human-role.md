# 11. The Human Role

Thesis: the engineer does not disappear; the engineer moves into the
three seats machines must not occupy.

## Legislator: writing the laws
Humans define the invariants, the budgets, the projections of what
matters. Law-writing is the new senior engineering: precise intent,
enforceable constraints, and deliberate freedoms.

## Judge: ruling on the raised
The system escalates genuine dilemmas — blown appetites, conflicting
invariants, irreversible acts — as crisp decision asks with evidence
attached. Human attention becomes a scarce, protected resource spent
only where judgment is genuinely human: values, risk acceptance,
direction.

## Intent-holder: mission command
Orders state intent, constraints, and freedoms — not task lists. The
system reports in plain language: what was accomplished, highlights
and lowlights, the weakest item first. Trust is calibrated by proofs
and honest reporting, and the vocabulary of oversight shifts from
activity ("what are you doing") to progress ("is intent advancing").

## What the human must not do
Review everything (caps the system at human speed); rescue silently
(destroys the evidence loop); rule by exception so often that law
stops meaning anything. The chapter closes with the emotional truth:
letting go of the builder's seat is hard, and the system must earn it
with years of honest envelopes.
