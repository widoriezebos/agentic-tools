# 16. What Engineering Becomes

Thesis: where reuse and risk justify the investment, delivery systems
become durable assets and engineering shifts toward rules, evidence,
care, and judgment.

## The organization follows the shift

Teams may form around intent domains and the delivery systems that
serve them rather than around a permanent queue of construction tasks.
The chapter will explore how careers move toward clearer intent,
stronger verification, safer authority design, and better judgment
without claiming that all organizations or applications should adopt
one structure.

## New scarcities, new skills

The scarce skills become writing checkable intent, designing tests that
distinguish failure, pricing verification and judgment, learning from
incidents, and explaining machine action in plain language. Human
attention remains scarce, so the craft includes deciding where it is
mandatory and where reliable machinery should protect it.

## What must not be lost

Accountability must terminate in identifiable humans; affected people
must have a route to challenge consequential decisions; records must
make those decisions understandable; and reversibility should exist
where the world permits it. Efficiency is not an excuse to erase
responsibility, appeal, or evidence of harm.

## Open problems

The paper will leave open federation across organizations, portable
evidence standards, liability and audit, regulation of runaway spend,
representation of affected people, and assistance to human lawmakers
that does not surrender their authority. These questions mark the
limits of the concept rather than future features promised by a hidden
implementation.

## Closing

The final section will return to the sequence: state and revise intent,
refuse inherited ceremony unless it still protects something real,
build machinery for the limits of the new medium, and hold that
machinery to rules it cannot evade. The aim is not software without
humans, but human attention spent where purpose, responsibility, and
judgment require it.
