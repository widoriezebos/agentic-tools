# 10. A System That Learns

Thesis: learning is compiled into machinery, or it is lost.

## Lessons become guards
The retrospective's output is not minutes but machinery: every
incident ends as a refusing gate, a ratchet, or a recorded precedent.
The test of having learned is that the same mistake is now impossible,
not that it is remembered.

## Ratchets: progress that cannot silently regress
Monotone guards — coverage floors, dependency allowlists, quality
thresholds — that any change must maintain. Ratchets catch what
reviews miss, including regressions introduced by the newest, most
scrutinized work: the system catching the system.

## Near-misses are evidence
Borrowed from aviation: events that harmed nothing still get recorded,
classified, and answered, because they are the cheapest warnings that
exist. Silent tolerance of a known weakness is itself an incident.

## Fix forward, with records
Perfection at the gate is not the goal; convergence is. Defects that
escape are met with immediate forward fixes and a record of the
escape path — and dropping work is cheap because records make
reopening cheap.

## The learning loop closed
Evidence envelopes from every run, near-miss registers, precedent
ledgers, and ratchets form one loop: experience becomes record, record
becomes law, law becomes machinery, machinery produces better
evidence.
