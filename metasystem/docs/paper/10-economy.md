# 9. The Economy of Machine Engineering

Thesis: when labor is metered in tokens, engineering management
becomes an economics problem the system itself must solve.

## Value before process
The standing question — "what is this process paying for, this week?"
— asked mechanically. Processes that cannot answer are dropped with a
record; nothing runs on tradition.

## Appetites as stop-laws
Work is authorized with a budget (time, rounds, spend) and the budget
is enforced: exhaustion stops the work and raises a decision, rather
than quietly doubling the bill. Sunk cost is countered by machinery
because it cannot be countered by discipline.

## Weight: when expensive proof is worth it
Cheap checks run always; the expensive global proof runs when
accumulated change crosses a declared threshold. The system weighs its
own backlog of risk and calls for the proof itself — cadence by
measured accumulation, not by calendar or anxiety.

## Parallelism changes the shape of spend
Many independent attempts plus judging often beats one careful
attempt; breadth is bought with tokens and paid back in verification
signal (agreement, divergence, and refutation between attempts carry
information). Design spend portfolios, not single bets.

## Measuring the system, not the activity
Outcome benchmarks over activity metrics; reconciliation of spend
against delivered intent, in the spirit of financial controls; the
delivery system earns its keep in measured deliveries.
