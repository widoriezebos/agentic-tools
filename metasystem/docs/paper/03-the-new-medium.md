# 3. The New Medium

Thesis: agentic labor is a different material with different failure
modes, so the delivery system must be derived from its real limits.

## What falls or changes shape

The cost of copying context, starting parallel attempts, working
without fatigue, and supplying a recorded skill briefing falls
sharply but remains real. Selecting enough relevant context,
coordinating attempts, judging their results, and paying for
computation remain real costs, so cheap copying is not free knowledge
and cheap generation is not free delivery.

## What actually goes away

Some familiar constraints can be removed from a single unit of work:
working hours, fatigue, ego, career incentives, and the need to wait
for one person to finish before another fresh worker can try. Later
role design will rely only on these removed limits, while preserving
separations required by authority, independent judgment, and
continuity.

## What appears

The chapter will introduce statelessness, silent death, plausible
wrongness, agreement-seeking, drift, uncertain process identity, and
metered computation. Each is a concrete failure mode: memory ends with
a session, absent output can look like patience, fluent work can be
false, and every extra attempt creates both spend and judging work.

## The asymmetry that organizes everything

Generation becomes cheap faster than trustworthy judgment does.
Verification — discriminating tests, independent review, and evidence
grounded in observable behavior — therefore becomes the bottleneck
around which the system is organized.

## Consequences in one view

The full paper will include a table mapping each limit reduced, each
limit introduced, and the system property that follows. This closes
the chapter with a design brief rather than the false premise that all
human constraints have vanished.
