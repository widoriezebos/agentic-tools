## HAE-CC-01 [medium|material=True]

The claim-time over-norm check the design requires is absent, so an approved goal whose reserved job minutes exceed the configured norm claims without a covering norm-approval record. The design's section 3 says claim, arc claim, the set-arc join, and steal must refuse with GOAL_NORM_REFUSED when the stored norm approval is missing or too small. The single gate returns the stored budget with no norm check, the tree validator only checks coverage when a norm-approval record exists, and the grandfather sweep approves any valid budget without a norm check. On the live backlog the queued goal counselor reserves 2400 minutes against the 1440-minute default with no norm-approval line; after the sweep it would claim without the strict-form human token the old claim path demanded. The test that certified that refusal was rewritten to drop the refusal case, so nothing proves the new rule. Fix: add the norm check inside requireApprovedForClaim in internal/goal/approval.go (or at its four callers plus resume) and restore a refusal assertion for an approved over-norm goal without a covering record. A mitigating reading exists: the sweep listing shows the budget and the empty norm-approval column, so the human confirming it has seen the number. The design nonetheless keeps the strict-form token as a separate requirement, and a later reduction of the norm would silently admit every approved goal above it.

## HAE-CC-02 [low|material=False]

A relayed approve rewrites a standing proven approval as relayed. The design says an approve on a claimed goal only re-ratifies a relayed record to proven, and that an unchanged proven record is nothing-to-do. The implementation's nothing-to-do shortcut also requires the caller to be proven, so a relayed word on a proven record replaces it with a relayed one that then expires at the review date or the fleet enrollment, after which steal and resume of that running work refuse. The command-layer test enshrines this downgrade. The exposure sits inside the conceded unauthenticated-relay limit (the same relay could unapprove outright), so this is a named follow-up: treat a proven, unexpired, unchanged record as nothing-to-do for a relayed caller.

Evidence: metasystem/internal/goal/approval.go:205-210 (skip only when f.Approved.Authority == proven AND authority == proven); metasystem/cmd/metasystem/goalsync_mutations_test.go TestGoalApproveAndUnapproveTemporarySurfacesRecordProofs at patch lines 489-510 approves the proven-approved claimed fixture with a relayed word and asserts authority=relayed; plans/human-approval-for-execution-design.md section 4 item 5 and section 5 'Re-approval, the specified path'.
