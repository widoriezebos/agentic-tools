1. **CRITICAL — STRUCTURAL — Delegated deletion still escapes the operation journal.**

   The draft requires a durable journal before every destructive action, but applies the discipline to janitor-owned classes while merely collecting delegated owners’ outcomes afterward ([disk-hygiene-design.md:83](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/plans/disk-hygiene-design.md:83), [disk-hygiene-design.md:138](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/plans/disk-hygiene-design.md:138)). These are concrete destructive actions outside the promised journal.

   The design should require every deletion owner to enlist through one journal transaction interface: durably prepare the exact action, delete only after receiving the prepared token, then complete that token. Standalone evidence GC and combined sweeps must use the same protocol; post-hoc outcomes are not write-ahead evidence.

3. **CRITICAL — STRUCTURAL — The suite writer set has a spawn race and no safe closing transition.**

   “Every detached writer enrolls at spawn” does not say whether enrollment precedes process creation or how the root capability reaches transitive spawners ([disk-hygiene-design.md:113](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/plans/disk-hygiene-design.md:113)). A crash between spawn and enrollment can therefore expose an apparently empty set while a writer is alive.

   Stage three compounds this by recognizing any root with a new ownership record, not a root whose enrollment epoch is proven closed ([disk-hygiene-design.md:70](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/plans/disk-hygiene-design.md:70), [disk-hygiene-design.md:76](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/plans/disk-hygiene-design.md:76)).

   The design should require a root-scoped capability inherited by every transitive spawner, a synced pending reservation before spawn, PID/start binding before the child’s first write, conservative retention of unresolved reservations, and an atomic `enrollment-closed` transition that refuses future spawns. Destructive migration eligibility must require that closed protocol version, not merely an ownership record.

6. **HIGH — STRUCTURAL — Journal and report retirement cannot satisfy the universal journal rule.**

   The draft says there is one journal, that each class places its journal on the source device, and that journals and reports are themselves compacted ([disk-hygiene-design.md:83](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/plans/disk-hygiene-design.md:83), [disk-hygiene-design.md:87](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/plans/disk-hygiene-design.md:87), [disk-hygiene-design.md:106](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/plans/disk-hygiene-design.md:106)). One physical journal cannot reside on several source devices. Retiring that journal or evicting an old report is itself destructive and therefore requires a surviving prior journal entry; otherwise the protocol either recurses forever or violates its own rule. “New reports record old-report evictions” does not distinguish planned from completed eviction ([disk-hygiene-design.md:138](/Users/wido/LocalStorage/GitHub/agentic-tools/metasystem/plans/disk-hygiene-design.md:138)).

   The design should define one logical journal protocol consisting of generation-numbered, per-device segments. A durable successor records retirement of a sealed predecessor. Reports must name the exact completed generations they cover, and report eviction must be prepared and completed in a still-live successor generation.
