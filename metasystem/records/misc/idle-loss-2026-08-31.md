1. NO DELIVERY: a blocked-on-human state and an idle-seat escalation
   both terminate in a git-landed log. The human learns nothing unless
   he polls. Owner: alert-escalation-channel (existing goal, Tier 5 —

2. NO WAITING DISCIPLINE: m3 ended a turn waiting on a peer with no
   deadline and no fallback in flight, violating the never-idle
   standing order (docs/orchestration.md). Conduct failure, now also a
   named gap (WAITING DISCIPLINE) in seat-mutual-awareness.
3. DETECTION BLIND SPOT: the stalled-idle detector went quiet during
   the deepest idle window; repository motion is not goal progress.

   the steward evidence semantics — needs the verification above

4. PEER OPACITY: neither seat could see the other was blocked; each
   waited on the other's silence. Owner: seat-mutual-awareness
   (discovery + response protocol + waiting discipline).
