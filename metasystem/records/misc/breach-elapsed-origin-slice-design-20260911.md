## Deliverable and observable contract

For an existing revision-bound claim, an approved `goal set-budget` may change the budget and claim revision, but must not move the elapsed start in either direction. A lawful revisionless migrated claim has no measurable structured episode: its first human-approved complete budget initializes the episode at that transaction, preserving the current repair behavior. At the same observation time immediately before and after a raise, `ProjectBudget.StartedAt`, `Elapsed`, and any consumed-discharge `WeightEpoch` remain equal; limits and their resulting admission verdict may change. Five raises cannot buy five fresh elapsed periods.
An ownership episode begins on a genuine fresh claim or successful human resume. Release/reclaim and steal start fresh episodes through existing lawful paths. The episode provides the stable origin for later work; this slice still measures wall-clock elapsed time.

## Persisted fields and transitions

Add `EpisodeAt string`, `EpisodeRevision uint64`, and `EpisodeObligationRevision uint64` to `ClaimRecord`.
The closed `Claimed:` grammar admits optional `episodeAt` and `episodeRevision` together; `episodeObligationRevision` is optional only beside that pair. Presence, including an empty value, determines validation. Present revisions must be positive integers and the timestamp must be RFC3339. Render the pair when `EpisodeRevision > 0`, then the obligation key only when positive. Continue rendering `accountingRevision` exactly as today.
No episode keys means a legacy claim: derive `effectiveAccountingRevision` from positive `Claimed.AccountingRevision`, otherwise `Claimed.Revision`; the fallback is `(Claimed.At, effectiveAccountingRevision, 0)`. Use this pair for elapsed projection and the consumed-proof lower bound. On the first effective set-budget, materialize the pair from the pre-raise binding and require its exact history pin. Do not search history, migrate records or change the spending interval. Revisionless claims remain readable only where currently lawful and remain unknown for structured budget admission.

`ValidateClaimRevision` retains its current claim-history checks, including its explicit risk-raise exception. In addition, an explicit episode must have both fields, a positive revision no later than the claim revision or available history, an exact timestamp match to `History[EpisodeRevision-1].At`, and a parsed timestamp no later than `Claimed.At`. A positive inherited obligation revision requires the episode pair and the strict ordering `EpisodeRevision < EpisodeObligationRevision < Claimed.Revision`. Reject values at either endpoint or outside that interval before projection; because the claim is history-bound, this also excludes a future history revision. An impossible inherited identity must return unknown evidence, never silently discard a valid consumed discharge and produce a known earlier clock. Reject invalid input before indexing history; never infer a valid origin from malformed explicit keys.
Retain revision 6a's exact diagnostics: `Claimed episode binding is incomplete (episodeAt and episodeRevision travel together)`; `Claimed episodeObligationRevision requires the episode binding (episodeAt and episodeRevision)`; `Claimed episodeObligationRevision=%q is not a positive integer`; `claimed episodeRevision=%d is later than claim revision=%d`; `claimed episodeAt=%s contradicts History revision=%d at=%s`; `claimed episodeAt=%s is later than claimed at=%s`; `claimed episodeObligationRevision=%d has no episode binding`. Use existing positive-revision/history-bound diagnostics for corresponding episode failures. History contradictions continue through `BUDGET_UNKNOWN`; malformed episode time uses `the claim episode timestamp is malformed` in projection.

## Elapsed origin and consumed discharge proof

Derive the episode pair once in `ProjectBudget`; pass both to `obligationBudgetStart`. With no live obligation and inherited obligation revision zero, return the episode origin without reading the consumed-proof ledger. Otherwise use the live obligation revision when present, or the inherited revision when absent.
A consumed proof is eligible only for this goal, with `EpisodeRevision <= proof.GoalRevision <= Claimed.Revision`, consumption at or after the episode origin, and an obligation revision equal to that selected obligation identity. Select the latest consumption timestamp, breaking ties by the higher weight generation exactly as today.
Retain the selected proof's **own** goal/obligation revision pair. The durable green attempt must match that exact pair, run identifier and weight generation, be green, have a closed breaker and not be exhausted. Do not substitute the current claim, accounting revision, or a merely in-range durable state for this exact pair. Keep malformed ledger, unauthorized consequence, duplicate-proof and missing-durable-proof refusals; no new authority to consume a proof is granted.
The result is `StartedAt`; elapsed is observation minus that start. Observation before it returns `BUDGET_UNKNOWN` with `CLOCK_REGRESSED: the claim episode origin is later than the observation`, including when the later origin came from discharge. Unknown evidence never becomes zero spending or an admitted request.
Change the existing post-discharge time comparisons to use the episode origin as their base. Preserve all current accounting-revision range filters for jobs, retained proof reservations, live governed runs and durable terminal attempts. Keep observed-minute settlement, open caps, setup-refusal handling, exact proof reservation-owner joins, duplicate/overflow checks and weight-epoch filtering. The expanded discharge lookup must not pull pre-budget reservations into the new accounting interval.
The inherited key preserves only a consumed clock movement, not permission to run a cleared obligation. Governed admission still requires a live obligation; installing a new one applies the supersession rule below. Existing run snapshots are not rewritten.
