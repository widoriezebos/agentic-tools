## Gaps the critic declared

- The resident steward lineage gap stands. metasystem/internal/lease/lease.go proves the value exists and metasystem/internal/up/up.go proves it is announced, but the worktree contains no dependency-safe public seam that returns it to metasystem/internal/steward, no runner snapshot of it, and no ruling on whether a later lease succession should change the presence lineage. ## Findings

### SMA-C-01 (material: True, severity: high)

Claim: Raw seat-authored words cross onto a human-visible narrator surface that the design says the human never sees. The proposed digest copies the question, the asker's silence plan, answer text, and close reason verbatim at metasystem/plans/seat-mutual-awareness-design.md:354-366, then claims at lines 368-371 that none reaches the human through the tick. The Stop hook always includes that digest in its system message, and the repository's communication law explicitly calls digest and stop messages human-reaching channels. The landed approval readers still will not accept this text as an approval, but the design failed its own human-channel isolation claim and omitted this path from the refusal table and residual list. The digest contract must either remove peer-authored text from the human-visible surface or specify an unmistakable non-authoritative envelope and prove every consumer treats it that way.

Evidence: metasystem/scripts/agents/supervision-hook.sh:445-470 and 648-662 always carry the pending digest into the returned message; metasystem/docs/seat-communication.md:1-9 says digest lines and stop messages reach the human. The contrary design claim and raw formats are at metasystem/plans/seat-mutual-awareness-design.md:354-371.

### SMA-C-02 (material: True, severity: high)

Claim: The sibling-directory choice is right for old validator compatibility, but the claim that the directory is harmless to every older engine is wrong. Before the new manifest row is installed, an old engine classifies metasystem/plans/seats as an ordinary plan record, its pre-commit guard does not fence it, and ordinary landing permits creation of a previously absent plan record. An old checkout can therefore introduce a new malformed ask or presence path that its old fetch validator accepts; upgraded machines then refuse the shared tip. This is materially worse than the design's comparison to a malformed goal file, because old engines already fence and validate goal files but do neither for seat files. The design needs a mixed-version deployment fence, capability gate, or tolerant migration rule before the first writer.

Evidence: The old reader compatibility is established by metasystem/internal/goal/channel.go:137-171, metasystem/internal/goal/channel_inbox.go:18-69, metasystem/internal/goal/validate.go:402-495, and metasystem/internal/goal/fetchadvance.go:55-81. The old write hole is established by the generic plan-record row at metasystem/scripts/agents/path-classes.txt:28-38, longest-prefix resolution at metasystem/internal/pathclass/pathclass.go:191-213, the guard regexp at metasystem/scripts/agents/pre-commit-guard.sh:70-85, and creation permission at metasystem/internal/landing/observe.go:672-693. The design nevertheless says no fleet action is required at metasystem/plans/seat-mutual-awareness-design.md:504-510.

### SMA-C-03 (material: True, severity: high)

Claim: The 30-minute seat-facing age and 180-minute dead age are justified for a dead role, but the proposed unknown state bypasses that policy. The design marks the seat role unknown whenever the last presence publication was refused. Landed health logic alerts after two consecutive unknown observations, so a persistent pushed journal entry normally reaches the human after about two ten-minute ticks rather than after 180 minutes, even if no seat question is that old. The design must decide whether presence publication failure belongs in this role, whether its unknown counter may alert, and which age actually governs the external notification.

Evidence: The design assigns presence refusal to unknown at metasystem/plans/seat-mutual-awareness-design.md:338-340 and states that 180 minutes governs human notification at lines 342-349. Two unknown observations set ShouldAlert at metasystem/internal/steward/health.go:461-470; UpdateAlertEpisodes submits that alert at metasystem/internal/steward/alert_episode.go:323-381, called every tick at metasystem/internal/steward/tick.go:267-299. The default tick period is ten minutes at metasystem/internal/steward/runner.go:53-60.

### SMA-C-04 (material: True, severity: high)

Claim: Seat publication cannot be recovered from the journal contract the design specifies. A transport-unknown publication blocks all later goal and seat mutations in that clone. Recovery can confirm it if its operation identifier landed, but if the operation is absent and must be rebuilt, the journal lacks the record body, facts, question, silence consequence, answer text, and close reason; the recovery dispatcher also has no seat cases. The documented remedy, “run goal recover,” would eventually reject and clear an absent seat operation instead of completing it, losing an ask or answer after the original process dies. The design must specify complete normalized intents, recovery constructors, and named recovery outcomes for all three seat verbs.

Evidence: The complete-intent requirement is metasystem/internal/goal/journal.go:57-66. Clone-wide pushed blocking and transport-unknown behavior are metasystem/internal/goal/txn.go:503-516 and 650-723. Recovery's absent-operation path and missing seat cases are metasystem/internal/goal/recover.go:58-199 and 202-403. The incomplete proposed intents are metasystem/plans/seat-mutual-awareness-design.md:142-160, 239-252, and 258-282.

### SMA-C-05 (material: True, severity: high)

Claim: The design has no durable fleet-membership or seat-capability record, so it cannot distinguish an existing machine whose steward never runs from a machine that does not exist. A real idle machine with no presence, current claim, or open ask is omitted by seat fleet and is refused by seat ask as unknown; the design's example showing paper as “never seen” cannot be produced from its stated enumeration rule. Conversely, a machine known only through a claim can receive an ask even when it runs an old steward that will never surface it. The goal's named nine-machine fleet therefore has no exact discovery or unsupported-target outcome. The design must add an enrollment/capability source or explicitly define and gate unreachable and nonexistent targets.

Evidence: The brief names the nine-machine fleet at metasystem/plans/seat-mutual-awareness-design-brief.md:21-26. The design enumerates only presence, claim, or open-ask machines but prints an otherwise undiscoverable paper example at metasystem/plans/seat-mutual-awareness-design.md:176-192. It refuses a target with neither presence nor claim at lines 239-252, while old engines deliberately ignore seat records at lines 47-64 and the surfacing path requires the new tick at lines 312-371.

### SMA-C-06 (material: True, severity: high)

Claim: No-answer and late-answer semantics are incomplete. The timeout flag has no default, a timeout writes no state transition, and ifSilent is prose rather than a durable deadline. Because seat-late is defined only when the record is already non-open, an answer arriving after the asker timed out and acted on its silence plan is still accepted and changes the record to answered. A target that never runs a steward can also leave the ask open forever when timeout is omitted. The design must name whether timeout closes or merely observes, define the omitted-timeout outcome, and give post-timeout answers exactly one binding or late outcome.

Evidence: ifSilent is only a string at metasystem/plans/seat-mutual-awareness-design.md:221-228. Answering is late only when state is not open at lines 263-282. seat wait has an optional timeout, returns locally on expiry, and performs no ledger write at lines 299-306.

### SMA-C-07 (material: True, severity: medium)

Claim: The at-rest state validator is not total enough for an implementer to build one deterministic record language. Its seat-state row does not say that states outside open, answered, and closed are refused; does not forbid close fields on open or answered records; and does not require non-empty answer text or close reasons. It also validates goal existence but not whether the target is a known machine, despite that restriction being enforced by the live ask verb. Hand-written records can therefore create states and phantom targets whose fleet, health, wait, and digest behavior the design never defines. These conditions and their named refusals must be added to the validator and malformed-tree tests.

Evidence: The record schema describes the intended state and payload shapes at metasystem/plans/seat-mutual-awareness-design.md:211-228, while the purportedly exhaustive refusal table at lines 410-419 omits the listed conditions. Target existence appears only in the live verb refusal at lines 239-252.

### SMA-C-08 (material: True, severity: high)

Claim: The design settles narrator quietness but not the operational cost or transport policy of making every steward a ledger writer. Nine machines at one presence commit per thirty minutes create up to 432 idle commits per day. Every other machine fetches them and projects every intervening commit even though no narrator event is emitted, and every upgraded validation reads an ask directory that the design says never rotates. Goal publication pushes only the configured canonical remote; it does not invoke the legacy transport mirror, so an implementer must guess between leaving that fallback stale and mirroring hundreds of heartbeat commits. The design must state the accepted load bound, history-growth policy, and legacy-transport behavior; SMA-F-QUIET proves only absence of attention and digest events.

Evidence: The cadence is metasystem/plans/seat-mutual-awareness-design.md:142-156 and the nine-machine count is metasystem/plans/seat-mutual-awareness-design-brief.md:21-26. Per-commit projection is metasystem/internal/steward/ledgerattention.go:226-268, while the no-event mechanism is lines 141-179 and 264-266. No rotation is explicit at metasystem/plans/seat-mutual-awareness-design.md:531-540. Publication and separate mirroring are metasystem/internal/goal/txn.go:343-372 and metasystem/scripts/agents/sync-transport.sh:4-35; the fallback's unresolved status is metasystem/plans/goals/transport-sync-law.md:1-6.

### SMA-C-09 (material: True, severity: high)

Claim: The lineage data exists, but the traced gap still blocks implementation because no owned steward API exposes the right value. The lease and announcement contain ownerLineage, yet the lease loader is private, its public current-holder view omits lineage, and the lease package already imports steward, preventing steward from importing lease without a package cycle. The runner record also omits lineage. Moreover, the current lease lineage can change after the long-lived steward was armed, while the proposed record claims to describe the arming session. The design must name the owner of a new dependency-safe read seam and decide whether presence reports the arming lineage or the current lease-holder lineage.

Evidence: OwnerLineage and the private loader are at metasystem/internal/lease/lease.go:24-44 and 71-102. Announcement carriage is at metasystem/internal/lease/classify.go:16-40 and metasystem/internal/up/up.go:428-447. CurrentHolder omits the field at metasystem/internal/lease/verbs.go:255-283, whose package imports steward at lines 1-17. The steward runner record omits it at metasystem/internal/steward/runner.go:40-51. The unresolved instruction is metasystem/plans/seat-mutual-awareness-design.md:199-205.

### SMA-C-10 (material: True, severity: high)

Claim: The proposed fixtures and unit tests cannot certify the design's highest-risk behavior. They omit mixed old/new engine fetch and write behavior, concurrent and transport-unknown recovery for every seat verb, absent and old stewards, nonexistent-target distinction, timeout followed by a late answer, persistent-unknown human alerts, the raw narrator-to-human path, fleet-scale commit churn, legacy transport synchronization, and the lineage source. These omissions would let every preceding defect ship while all named tests pass. The box estimate is candid that the full build exceeds 240 worker-minutes, but it understates the proof work because these scenarios are absent; the proof matrix and estimate must change before implementation.

Evidence: The eight fixtures and thirteen named unit-test functions are listed at metasystem/plans/seat-mutual-awareness-design.md:442-498 and contain none of the named cases. The design estimates 195 to 285 worker-minutes and defers a raise until after slice two at lines 500-529, against the approved 240-worker-minute box at metasystem/plans/goals/seat-mutual-awareness.md:11.
