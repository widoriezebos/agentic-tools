- **Superseded:** the toolchain-pinning proposal explicitly says not to approve or claim it. Its number preserves its place in this discussion inventory; its disposition is closure review, not implementation.

Recent setup work also repaired several fixture and root-routing defects. Some older backlog entries may now be wholly or partly covered. Their rows flag examples; this pass has not claimed a repository-wide proof of every old defect's closure.
