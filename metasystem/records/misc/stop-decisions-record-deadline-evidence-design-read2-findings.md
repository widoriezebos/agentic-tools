## SDE-01 (critical)

The critical lost-refusal race remains. Mutual exclusion prevents two stored completions when either actor acquires and releases the record lock normally: a deadline completion makes the worker replay without spending, while a completed worker block makes the parent relay that block. The timeout interleaving is still unsafe. A worker can acquire the record lock, observe no marker, and then wait for the verdict-state lock or perform later writes. The parent can subsequently exhaust its 2.5-second wait, create the marker outside the lock, and emit an allowance. Because the worker checks the marker only once, it can then spend and commit a block that the seat never received. Conversely, if the marker precedes the worker's check, the worker spends nothing but the page does not say who completes the still-pending episode. The claimed maximum worker hold is not established: the two seconds bound only goal-lock acquisition, not later state writes, record publication, or log append, and the worker can acquire after the parent's wait began. Independently, today's command writes the open-work seen marker before it even calls the verdict owner, while the landing section omits metasystem/cmd/metasystem/goal.go and metasystem/internal/report/openwork.go. An implementer following the listed boundary can therefore preserve an invisible spend even without the marker interleaving.

Evidence the reader cited: metasystem/plans/stop-decisions-record-deadline-evidence-design.md:107-122 puts one marker check before spending and nested state work; lines 130-139 let the parent publish the marker and emit outside the record lock; lines 206-213 assert the unsafe race is covered. metasystem/cmd/metasystem/goal.go:657-680 calls MarkOpenWorkSeen before TurnVerdict, and metasystem/internal/report/openwork.go:269-322 durably writes that marker. Idle counts are mutated at metasystem/internal/goal/turnverdict.go:501-516 and 548-556. The nested goal lock may wait two seconds at metasystem/internal/goal/goalverbs.go:99-125. The incomplete landing boundary is metasystem/plans/stop-decisions-record-deadline-evidence-design.md:232-236.

## SDE-11 (high)

The 2.5-second lock wait can itself consume the deadline parent's response budget. The page allocates 2.5 of the final three seconds to lock acquisition but gives no absolute deadline or budget for reading or preparing the episode, terminating the worker, recording hook expiry, completing the record, appending the line, composing JSON, and emitting it. Today's parent already performs child and resolver shutdown plus hook-expiry bookkeeping before constructing its fallback. An implementer must guess where the 2.5 seconds begins and can legally build a path that reaches the runtime's sixty-second cutoff without emitting anything, recreating the umbrella failure.

Evidence the reader cited: metasystem/plans/stop-decisions-record-deadline-evidence-design.md:130-136 specifies a relative 2.5-second wait, while lines 175-177 merely state that time must remain. metasystem/scripts/agents/supervision-hook.sh:40-48 reserves three seconds; lines 306-351 stop and wait for processes and invoke hook-expire before the existing fallback is composed at lines 362-405. No design step assigns an absolute cutoff to all of those operations.

## SDE-02 (high)

The ordinary episode key now distinguishes same-second retries, but the uncertain identity is still not buildable or demonstrably collision-free. Prepare accepts generation, attempt and deadline plus a boolean uncertain flag; it accepts neither the parent process identifier nor the parent start used by the declared uncertain key, nor a complete episode identity. The phrase “parent start” also has no unit or source. The current handoff exposes a parent process identifier and a second-resolution invocation time, not an exact birth identity; using those apparent values permits a recycled process identifier in the same second to reuse an old uncertain key. The page also still conflicts with the umbrella, which explicitly includes checkout, holder session, lease epoch and condition fingerprint in the episode key. An implementer must choose which contract and coordinate source to implement.

Evidence the reader cited: metasystem/plans/stop-decisions-record-deadline-evidence-design.md:41-49 declares both key forms, but the prepare interface at line 68 cannot carry the uncertain suffix. metasystem/scripts/agents/supervision-hook.sh:49 and 72 expose only epoch-second invocation time and the shell process identifier. metasystem/plans/stop-hook-never-forces-an-empty-turn-design.md:135-140 still declares the longer key, although line 153 separately requires attempt comparison. The goal Intent names generation and deadline end. The certain generation and attempt sequence are independently advanced at metasystem/internal/steward/component_evidence.go:226-251.

## SDE-03 (high)

The episode-file handoff is connected and correctly ordered before prepare, but it is not crash-safe. The page says only to write one identity line. A direct shell redirection can create an empty file and then be killed before writing or can expose malformed content. The parent defines behavior for no file, a file naming an unprepared episode, and a pending episode, but not an existing file that names no valid episode. Implementers can choose direct replacement, atomic rename, or uncertain fallback and produce different episode counts.

Evidence the reader cited: metasystem/plans/stop-decisions-record-deadline-evidence-design.md:101-105 specifies publication before prepare without an atomic publication operation. Lines 124-129 enumerate only three parent cases and omit empty or malformed publication. The repository already has an all-or-nothing publication owner at metasystem/internal/atomicfile/atomicfile.go:67-113, but the page does not assign the episode file to it. A death between file creation and the one-line write can therefore escape all three cases.

## SDE-05 (high)

Parent incidents remain ambiguous when the worker has already committed. A repeated complete returns the worker's decision without changing it, while the page says the parent's cause is recorded in the same write as the decision. When expiry or unreadable output finds an existing worker decision, there is no decision write in which to add the parent incident. The implementation may return unchanged and lose the deadline condition, or may mutate incidents during a nominally repeated completion. Those choices change what the later steward drain receives.

Evidence the reader cited: metasystem/plans/stop-decisions-record-deadline-evidence-design.md:80-83 defines repeated completion as returning the committed decision; lines 130-134 require the parent to relay that decision. Lines 141-145 say parent causes become incidents in the same write as the decision but do not define the already-completed case. The umbrella requires retained incidents to be drained at metasystem/plans/stop-hook-never-forces-an-empty-turn-design.md:169-175.

## SDE-06 (high)

The verb interfaces still omit required inputs and an arming representation. The episode schema requires a checkout field, but prepare supplies no checkout or root. The turn-verdict interface supplies a record path, identity and arming file but no deadline-directory or marker path, so the verdict cannot locate the marker it must check without reading an undocumented environment variable. Complete requires an arming file even when the deadline parent completes before arming produced one. Finally, a raw rendered arming file cannot by itself establish complete versus incomplete or split exact component lines from the aggregate while also “parsing nothing”; nevertheless the decision log requires the aggregate separately. Implementers must invent flags, environment coupling, sentinels, or a parser, changing metasystem/cmd/metasystem/report.go, metasystem/cmd/metasystem/goal.go and the persisted schema differently.

Evidence the reader cited: metasystem/plans/stop-decisions-record-deadline-evidence-design.md:47-59 declares checkout, arming completeness and a separate aggregate. The interfaces at lines 68, 78 and 92 omit checkout, marker location and an absent-arming form. Lines 101-112 place the marker in the deadline directory but pass only record path, identity and arming path to turn-verdict. Lines 147-152 require a separately populated aggregate. The current arming owner only renders typed results to lines at metasystem/internal/up/up.go:79-113; the page selects no inverse format or parser.

## SDE-07 (medium)

The fold assigns one decision-line owner but still lacks an exactly-once crash protocol. The worker completes the record and then appends the decision line while holding the lock; a process death between those two file operations leaves a completed episode with no decision line. A later deadline parent sees the worker completion and is expressly limited to a relay line, so it cannot repair the missing decision line without violating the page. If it cannot tell whether the worker appended before dying, repairing unconditionally risks duplication. This changes the permanent stream used for the daily count and incident recovery.

Evidence the reader cited: metasystem/plans/stop-decisions-record-deadline-evidence-design.md:115-117 orders completion before the decision append. Lines 147-157 require one decision line and only a stop-relay line from a relaying parent. The fixture at lines 220-226 covers ordinary relay and append failure but not death immediately before or after append. The umbrella requires one permanent decision line per stop at metasystem/plans/stop-hook-never-forces-an-empty-turn-design.md:242 and 269.

## SDE-08 (medium)

The new retention bound can delete undelivered incidents, contradicting the delivery contract. Prepare prunes every episode older than thirty days without exempting an episode whose incidents remain unacknowledged; because the readable record then simply lacks the incident, the later steward cannot use its unreadable-record fallback. The rule for dropping version-one causes also measures thirty days from “the record's upgrade” without adding an upgrade timestamp to the schema. Implementers must invent both the undelivered exception and the timestamp source.

Evidence the reader cited: metasystem/plans/stop-decisions-record-deadline-evidence-design.md:60-62 says delivery remains undelivered until acknowledgment, but lines 70-76 prune all thirty-day episodes and version-one history. The umbrella requires undelivered incidents to remain until acknowledgment at metasystem/plans/stop-hook-never-forces-an-empty-turn-design.md:142 and uses the hook log only when the version-two record is unreadable at lines 169-175. No upgrade timestamp is listed among the record fields at metasystem/plans/stop-decisions-record-deadline-evidence-design.md:47-60.
