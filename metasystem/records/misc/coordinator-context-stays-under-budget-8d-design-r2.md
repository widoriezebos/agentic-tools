## 2. The shape in one paragraph

Two configuration keys give the trigger, and the margin is chosen so the DONE's numbers hold by arithmetic (D1). The Stop gate samples the last call from the transcript the hook passes, blocks over the trigger with one sentence and keeps blocking until a successor is confirmed; the verb is the only recorder (D2). A PreToolUse hook, one engine process under an injected 100 ms deadline, denies every call over the trigger but the handoff, a delegate launch, a landing, a registered wait, a job watch and the memory note, allowing on any doubt (D3). The handoff verb ends the session's waits and records each row's selector and target, records every in-flight task by a state machine over the transcript, and refuses without a fresh memory note that names every declared output (D4). The verb launches the successor itself under a durable launch record, retries a failed launch under the dry cap, and waits up to 30 seconds for the running signal (D5). The successor is a steward continuation in the delegate class for as long as its predecessor lives: no lease moves; its Stop hook runs the over-trigger gate against the installation, so the chain continues (D6). Its first act, `context resume`, verifies, registers every open-work row afresh against its recorded target, and writes the dead time (D7). The predecessor's later Stops are allowed with one console line; when the successor dies, the reaper returns the claim once and relaunches (D8). Claims move to the successor by one authenticated leg, and the handed-off runtime session's ledger mutations refuse (D9).

## 3. Decisions

### D1. The trigger is configuration; the proof numbers are the DONE's (U3a-1; CC8D-103, 114; SR4)

- D1.1 Keys in `internal/config`: `context.ceiling.tokens` (default 250000, R-114-m1e item 3) and `context.handoff.margin.tokens` (default 115000); the trigger is ceiling minus margin (135000 by default). Both keys are registered with the numeric knobs in internal/config/validate.go (:496-512) and documented in metasystem.conf beside the spend keys (:27-35). Load refuses `CONTEXT_CONFIG_INVALID key=<k> reason=<r>` for a malformed value (not an integer), a non-positive ceiling or margin, a margin at or above the ceiling (inverted), and a trigger above `ProofP95Tokens - HandoffHeadroomTokens` (150000 - 15000 = 135000; D1.4); an unknown `context.` key is refused as validate.go refuses any unknown key today. `ContextBoundTokens` and `ContextCeilingTokens` (steward/context.go:23-24) become the accessor `config.ContextBudget(root) (Budget{Ceiling, Margin, Trigger int64}, error)`; its readers are `contextVerdict`, the health line (`context-budget: <N>K of <T>K trigger, ceiling <C>K`), `context status`, D2's block and D3's gate.
- D1.2 The sample is the last main-thread call's prompt tokens from the Stop payload's `transcript_path` when the hook passes it (D2.1), else from the registered session as today. An unreadable sample never blocks: the verdict prints `CONTEXT: unknown (<reason>)` (P1).
- D1.3 The proof's thresholds are the DONE's numbers as constants in contextreport.go: `ProofP95Tokens = 150000` and `ProofMaxTokens = 200000` (the maximum rule at :566-569 keeps its number). They are not configuration; the accessor never reaches the report; the goal's DONE text is unchanged (SR4). Nothing changes what the proof measures or excludes (R-115-m1e item 6).
- D1.4 The arithmetic (the diagnosis: boot near 110K, growth near 4K a call; the proof week measures both). A call is sampled after it returns, so the first call at or over the trigger is under 139K; the tool gate then admits only D3.3's calls, and the handoff is two of them (the note edit, the verb): under 143K and 147K. In the normal path no call reaches 150K, so the p95 line holds with every call of the session under it. Calls over 150K arise only from allowlisted in-flight work at the trigger (a landing driver run, a registered wait, a job watch, a delegate launch: one call each) and from a seat that ignores the first block; the worst case the design admits, the handoff plus a landing, a wait and a delegate launch, is five calls under 159K, 41K under the DONE's maximum. The ceiling (250K) is where even delegate launches are denied (D3.4); a call over 200K is the proof's finding, never a stop. The price of the numbers is about 25K of working context per session between boot and trigger; question 1 puts that to Wido.

### D2. The over-trigger block (U3a-2; CC8D-109; SR7)

- D2.1 The hook passes `--transcript`, `--runtime` and (B1 of registered-wait-matches-the-runtime-session) `--runtime-session` to `goal turn-verdict`; the payload already carries `session_id` (supervision-hook.sh:1534). A missing field is not an error (D1.2).
- D2.2 A branch `context-over-trigger` in `TurnVerdict` runs after the handoff allowance (turnverdict.go:393) and before the open-work, unwatched, uncertainty and idle-backlog branches: when the sample is at or over the trigger and D8.1's marker for the runtime session is `none`, `failed` or `ended-without-handoff`, the Stop blocks with exactly `CONTEXT AT <N>K OF <C>K (trigger <T>K): run metasystem context handoff --root <installation> alone in your next message` (`BlockSource` = `context-over-trigger`; not counted in `IdleBlocks` nor in the ten-refusal threshold, P3). A `launching` marker blocks with `handoff <nonce> recorded; successor not yet confirmed; run metasystem context handoff --await <nonce> --root <installation>`.
- D2.3 The gate never records: the verb is the only recorder, so one caller holds the fence's entry (CC8D-101) and S7's note is the seat's own act. Every later over-trigger Stop without a confirmed successor blocks with the same sentence; `ContextBlocks` on `sessionState` counts them and raises the attention item `context-handoff-overdue` once per session at the third block (a notice to the human, never a wait on him, R-114-m1e item 6); there is no bound on the blocks (a bound is a stop). Over the trigger the tool gate leaves the seat only D3.3's calls, so a block costs one short turn.
- D2.4 Faults (CC8D-109). Interactive seat main: a capture fault (record unwritable or unreadable) allows the Stop through `infrastructureVerdict("handoff-record")` as today (turnverdict.go:396) with the attention item `context-handoff-capture-fault`; a launch failure after the record (marker `failed`, D5.2) allows the Stop with `systemMessage` = `handoff <nonce> recorded, successor not started: <reason>; run metasystem context handoff --retry <nonce>`; the pane stays alive, so no session ends (P2), and the seat retries at its next turn. Headless continuation (`Headless` on `TurnVerdictOptions`, set by U3c-2): both cases block (fail closed) with `HANDOFF FAULT <code>: <reason>; fix and record again` or `...; run metasystem context handoff --retry <nonce>`; when the attempts are exhausted the sentence is `run metasystem context handoff --await <nonce> --seconds 600 --root <installation>`, an allowlisted call that waits inside one tool call for the running signal (D5.3), so each block costs one call per ten minutes and no session ends without a successor started.
- D2.5 The idle-backlog bound stays at three here; P3's change to two is U2c's.

### D3. The in-turn bound: a PreToolUse hook (U3e; R-114-m1e item 5; CC8D-107, 108, 112; SR6)

- D3.1 Installation (CC8D-108). scripts/enforcement/claude-code-hooks.json gains `PreToolUse` (no matcher: every tool) running `bash scripts/agents/supervision-hook.sh claude tool`, timeout 5; host setup merges it into .claude/settings.json (setup.go:153-185) and `hooks check` reports it when missing (hooks.go:40-49 requires every shipped event; the structural check at :57-74 applies because the command invokes the supervision hook). U3e edits the shipped file and re-derives the checkout's .claude/settings.json through `metasystem up`, never by hand. Job settings (adapter/claude.go:122-131) are unchanged; a delegate job that is not a continuation gets an immediate allow from the verb (D3.2).
- D3.2 The path and the bound (CC8D-112). `claude tool` is the first case of the script's dispatch, before engine resolution and `lease hook-delegate` (:1464-1476, :1581-1589): it reads the engine path `claude start` caches at `artifacts/agents/context/engine-path` (absent: print the allow JSON with cause `engine-unresolved`, exit 0) and execs one process, `<engine> adapter claude-tool-gate --root <installation> --deadline-ms 100`, payload on stdin (`session_id`, `transcript_path`, `tool_name`, `tool_input`, `cwd`). The verb starts its clock at entry (injected `Now`), classifies from the custody in-process (`lease.HookDelegate`, hook_delegate.go:22-23; a non-continuation delegate allows with cause `delegate`), reads the budget (D1.1) and samples through `usage.LatestCall` with two new `ReadOptions` fields, `MaxBytes` (262144) and `Deadline`: the reader seeks to max(cursor, size minus MaxBytes), drops a partial first line, skips any line longer than the window (the 32 MB cap of cursor.go:19 is never reached), returns `Reading{Reason: "deadline"}` when `Now()` passes `Deadline` between lines, and never writes the cursor (the Stop-time reader keeps that). Under the trigger, no sample, any read error or the deadline passed: allow with `tool-gate-allow: <cause>`. What the harness timeout skips: a hook that exceeds its timeout or exits without JSON yields no decision and the call proceeds, so every slow path allows. Decisions are counted in `artifacts/agents/context/tool-gate.jsonl` (session, at, tokens, tool, decision, cause, elapsedMs), one row per deny or non-trivial allow. No witness asserts wall time: the deadline is the injected clock's.
- D3.3 The grammar (CC8D-107). The `Bash` command is split into simple commands on `&&`, `||`, `;`, `|` and newlines; each is shell-split; a leading `cd <path>`, `K=V` assignments and a `bash` or `sh` wrapper are stripped; the command word is matched by its basename after any path prefix. Allowed at the trigger: `metasystem context handoff|resume|status|verify`, `metasystem landing <verb>`, `metasystem goal land-ready`, `metasystem wait`, `metasystem job watch`, `metasystem delegate`, `metasystem steward revive|status`, and `<path>scripts/agents/land.sh`; a trailing output filter (`head`, `tail`, `grep`, `tee`, `wc`, `cut`, `sed`) is neutral. Also allowed: the `Agent` tool; `Write` and `Edit` whose path is under the seat's memory directory (D4.4). A compound command with any other simple command, and every other call, is denied with `{"hookSpecificOutput":{"hookEventName":"PreToolUse","permissionDecision":"deny","permissionDecisionReason":"CONTEXT AT <N>K (trigger <T>K): this call is denied; run metasystem context handoff --root <installation> alone, or launch a delegate"}}`. A landing, a land-ready, a wait or a job watch is never denied whatever the sample (R-114-m1e item 5).
- D3.4 The allowlist is one table in internal/adapter/toolgate.go with two columns, `trigger` and `ceiling`, read by the gate and its test. At or over the ceiling `metasystem delegate`, `steward revive` and `Agent` are denied too (reason `... run metasystem context handoff --root <installation> alone`); what stays allowed at the ceiling is the never-denied set, the handoff verbs and the memory note.
- D3.5 What DONE's "under a stated context size" means after U3e is D1.4's arithmetic: every call after the trigger is one of D3.3's, and the proof's maximum rule reports any call over 200K.

### D4. The open-work record (U3b; R-115-m1e item 2; S7; CC8D-104, 111, 113, 115; SR5, SR8)

- D4.1 Waits. The handoff ends every `registering` or `pending` row whose `MainId` is the caller's (handoff_capture.go:374-427 no longer refuses): it signals the row through the waiter's own interrupt path (the row turns `interrupted`, `interruptedBy: handoff <nonce>`), then records the row under `openWork` with the complete `WaitSelector` (nine fields, waiter.go:57-68), the complete `WaiterTarget` (waiter.go:45-53, marshalled whole so a later field travels without a design change), the deadline left in seconds, and for a `local` row the child's pid and birth. A `registering` row younger than 5 seconds is waited for up to 5 seconds (injected clock). A row that cannot be ended refuses `HANDOFF_WAIT_UNENDABLE row=<id>` and nothing is recorded. The successor never runs `wait --resume` (D7.2).
- D4.2 Engine jobs stay as today: running jobs of the held goal under `openJobs`, a pending one refuses `HANDOFF_LAUNCH_IN_FLIGHT`.
- D4.3 In-flight tasks (CC8D-104, SR5). `usage.InFlightTasks(transcript, opts) ([]Task, error)` in internal/usage/tasks.go walks the records in order with one state per task id: launch = an assistant `tool_use` named `Agent`, or `Bash` with `tool_input.run_in_background` true, whose user `tool_result` carries `agentId: <a>` (Agent) or the background task id (Bash); resume = a later `tool_use` whose result carries the same id; terminal = a user record holding `<task-notification>` with `<task-id>` equal to the id (any `<status>` word ends the flight). A task is in flight iff its last event is a launch or resume. The verb: `--no-delegates` refuses `HANDOFF_TASKS_IN_FLIGHT ids=<...>` when any task is in flight; `--delegate id=<a>,asked=<text or path>,output=<path>` for an id the transcript never launched refuses `HANDOFF_DELEGATE_UNKNOWN id=<a>`; an in-flight id not declared refuses `HANDOFF_DELEGATE_UNDECLARED id=<a>`; a declared terminal id is recorded with `terminal: true` (its output is still owed to the successor); neither flag refuses `HANDOFF_DELEGATES_UNDECLARED`. Each declaration is recorded under `delegates` (id, toolUseId, kind agent|bash, asked, output, declaredAt, terminal). A runtime with no transcript takes the declaration as the record. So S1's quiet point reads: no task in flight, or every in-flight task declared. Fixtures under internal/usage/testdata/tasks/: running, completed, failed, missing-notification, mismatched-declaration, resumed-then-completed, background-bash.
- D4.4 S7 mechanically (CC8D-113 narrowed). `--note <path>` is required (`HANDOFF_NOTE_MISSING`); the path must lie under the seat's memory directory, the `memory/` sibling of the transcript's project directory that internal/usage already resolves (else `HANDOFF_NOTE_OUTSIDE_MEMORY path=<p>`); the note is read once before anything is written: it must contain every declared delegate's `output` path (else `HANDOFF_NOTE_LACKS_OUTPUT path=<p>`); its modification time must follow `Announcement.PidStartedAt` (whole seconds): a later second passes; the same second passes only when the note's sha256 differs from the `lessonsNote.sha256` of the seat's previous handoff state (none: passes); earlier refuses `HANDOFF_NOTE_STALE note=<p> modified=<t> session=<t0>`. The state records `lessonsNote {path, modifiedAt, sha256}` (one object) and `VerifyHandoffState` checks `lessonsNote.modifiedAt < writtenAt` (S7's check, unchanged). No machine judgement of the lessons text. A headless continuation names its own note the same way.
- D4.5 Engine identity and the schema (CC8D-115). The state records `engine {path, sha256, installGen}` from `os.Executable()`; D5.7 and D7.4 read it. `HandoffSchemaVersion = 2`; the state adds `openWork []HandoffOpenWork`, `delegates []HandoffDelegate`, `lessonsNote *HandoffNote`, `engine *HandoffEngine`. The reader (:139-151 callers, the version check at :304-307) accepts 1 and 2: a version-1 document decodes into the same struct with the new fields absent and the verifier skips the note check for it; the writer always writes 2; any other version refuses as today. `splitHandoffStateLists` (:736-749) moves `openWork` and `delegates` to the manifest on overflow beside the three lists it moves today; scalars never overflow; the 32 KB bound holds.

### D5. The successor starts from the verb, within seconds, by identity (U3c-0, U3c-1; CC8D-101, 102, 105; SR2, SR7)

- D5.1 Authority (CC8D-101, SR2). The successor is a steward continuation in the delegate class, custody-based, for the whole life of its predecessor: it never announces as a seat main, the lease never moves while the predecessor lives (claim.go:117-122 gains no exception), and its own handoff is the delegate-class path that exists (handoff_capture.go:322-340, context_verbs.go:223-233). Main-ness returns to whoever announces after the predecessor has ended, as today. Holds in `decideForHandoff`: `handoffPredecessorLiveness` goes into the reason only (no hold at handoff.go:93-101); the census hold (:102-114) becomes identity-based: `Workers` gains `SeatMainRefs []identity.Ref` from `workersFromVerdict`, and a live seat main counts only when it is neither `binding.Predecessor` nor a main whose runtime session's marker (D8.1) is `running` or `ended-with-handoff`; `others` (revive.go:276-285) passes the intents and excludes a consumed intent whose `JobId` equals `binding.PredecessorJob`, so a continuation is succeeded by the next; `CensusComplete`, `Untracked`, `Unprovable`, the dry cap and the outage hold are unchanged.
- D5.2 Launch lifecycle (CC8D-105, SR7). `steward.HandoffAndLaunch(stateRoot, caller, args, now, launch LaunchSeam, confirm ConfirmSeam)` in internal/steward/handoff_launch.go: `Handoff` as today (record, supersede, stage), then `decideForHandoff`; on launch it writes the intent's durable `launch {attempt, startedAt, jobId, stampedAt, failedAt, reason}` with attempt n and `startedAt` before dispatch (consumption and `RecordRevival` as at revive.go:189-206), dispatches through the verb's seam of steward_verbs.go:493-509 (`<engine> delegate --revive <nonce>`, `Setsid`), then `StampLaunch` writes `jobId` and `stampedAt`. A dispatch error writes `failedAt` and `reason`: the intent stays consumed and restageable; `context handoff --retry <nonce>` (same caller admission) launches attempt n+1 while `attempt < cfg.MaxRevivals`; at the cap `HANDOFF_LAUNCH_ATTEMPTS_EXHAUSTED nonce=<n> attempts=<k>` and the steward's `ActNotify` escalation `handoff-launch-failed` names the human, as the dry cap does today (handoff.go:118); the tick never relaunches. A stamp failure is distinct: `jobId` set, `stampedAt` empty; `--retry` and `--await` reconcile it from the job record (running: stamp now, idempotent; missing: a launch failure). No duplicate: before any dispatch the launcher reads `launch.jobId` and dispatches only when no job record for it exists or that record is terminal.
- D5.3 The bound: the successor is running within 30 seconds of the record (`HandoffConfirmWaitSeconds = 30`, injected clock, poll every 500 ms; the record time is `state.writtenAt`, the launch time `launch.stampedAt`). `context handoff --await <nonce> --seconds <n>` (n at most 3600) runs the same confirm seam and prints D5.5's lines; both wait inside one process.

- D5.5 The running signal: `launch.stampedAt` set and the job record `<jobId>` at `running` with a pid the identity prober confirms. `steward status` gains `continuation <jobId> <running|launching|failed|held|missing> for handoff <nonce> since <t>`; `context handoff` prints `handoff recorded: <nonce> state=<path> sha256=<digest>` then `successor running: job=<jobId> pid=<pid> after <n>s` and exits 0; without the signal inside the bound it prints `successor not confirmed: <reason>` and exits 1, and D2.4 takes over. The seat is not handed off before the signal.
- D5.6 The staged brief (stage.go:153-164) adds: run `metasystem context resume --root <installation> --nonce <nonce>` first, then `metasystem goal claim --id <goal> --continue-handoff <nonce>`, use the engine at `<engine.path>`. The role's rule 2 (scripts/agents/roles/steward-continuation.md:20-22) gains: under a handoff nonce the predecessor is expected alive and idle; the continuation never yields to it, never announces as a main, and claims only through the continuation leg.
- D5.7 Stale engine. Before staging, the verb runs `<engine> context resume --help` under a 2-second injected deadline; a non-zero exit refuses `HANDOFF_ENGINE_LACKS_RESUME engine=<path>`. The seat rebuilds and records again.

### D6. The continuation keeps its class and gains a Stop gate (U3c-2; SSTB-301; CC8D-109; SR2)

The continuation stays in the delegate class throughout (D5.1); `context handoff` from it is the delegate-class record of fact 1.
- D6.2 An allowed Stop under a confirmed successor ends the process; the reaper closes the chain (reap.go:33-57) and `others` falls; D5.1's `PredecessorJob` exclusion lets the next launch precede the reap. No other path ends it: D2.4's headless rules block every other Stop (P2).
- D6.3 The continuation runs in the worktree dispatch gives it (dispatch.sh:1482); every ledger verb and the gate use `--root <installation>` from the custody; landings follow the role contract's covenant as today. Whether a `SendMessage` reaches a headless session stays unverified; U7's inbox is the relay (R-114-m1e item 4).

### D7. `context resume`: verify, register afresh, record dead time (U3c-3; S6; SSTB-308; CC8D-111)

- D7.1 `metasystem context resume --root <installation> --nonce <nonce>` (main.go `families()`, a router line): refuses unless the caller is the main process of the job the consumed intent for `<nonce>` names (`HANDOFF_RESUME_NOT_SUCCESSOR`, the classification of context_verbs.go:223-233); runs `VerifyHandoffState`; prints the held goal, its next step's first line, the `openWork`, `delegates` and `openJobs` counts, the `context-budget` line, and `next: metasystem goal claim --id <goal> --continue-handoff <nonce>`.
- D7.2 It registers every `openWork` row as a fresh wait through the wait verb with every selector field, the deadline left, the local pid and birth under U1's authenticated registration (members A to C), and the recorded target through a new `--target <json>`: wait_verb.go:180-223 resolves the current target and compares every `WaiterTarget` field with the recorded one, refusing `WAIT_TARGET_CHANGED row=<id> field=<f>` when any differs (a reused id bound to a later lifecycle). A refused row prints `open-work row <id> not registered: <reason>`, the verb still exits 0, and the row stays in the state for the seat to act on. It never runs `wait --resume`.
- D7.3 Dead time. The verb writes once, exclusively, `artifacts/agents/context/handoffs/<nonce>/resume.json` (schema 1): `recordedAt` (= state.writtenAt), `launchStampedAt`, `resumedAt`, `successorJob`, `successorSession`, `successorMainId`, `launchSeconds`, `deadTimeSeconds`, `openWorkRegistered`, `openWorkFailed`. `state.json` is untouched, so the nonce directory stays immutable for `VerifyHandoffState`. `context status` prints `last handoff <nonce>: dead time <n>s`; `context report` lists every handoff of the week with its dead time; U5a reads the same file (R-115-m1e item 3). The expectation is under 120 seconds; a longer one is a proof-week finding, never a stop.
- D7.4 The verb compares its own executable's sha256 with `state.engine.sha256` and prints `engine differs: recorded=<sha> running=<sha>` as a line, not a refusal.
- D7.5 It is S6's first act; the second is the claim leg (D9.1); the third is `goal next` on the held goal as the role says.

### D8. The handed-off predecessor: one console line per Stop, no turn; recovery when the successor dies (U3c-4; SSTB-305; R-115-m1e item 2; CC8D-106)

- D8.1 The marker is derived from durable records, never stored in the session store: `steward.HandoffMarker(stateRoot, runtimeSession, now) (Marker{Nonce, JobId, State, Since})` reads live and consumed intents whose `PredecessorRuntimeSession` is the caller's (`LiveHandoffForSession` gains the consumed intents; the allowance survives consumption and the reap) and the successor's job record. `State` is `none`, `launching` (no `stampedAt`), `failed` (`launch.failedAt`, attempts not exhausted or exhausted), `running` (stamped, job running), `ended-with-handoff` (job terminal and a later intent names it as predecessor), `ended-without-handoff` (job terminal, `successorEnded` written by D8.4) or `missing`. The gate, the census (D5.1) and the fence (D9.3) treat `running` and `ended-with-handoff` as handed off; `launching` and `failed` follow D2.
- D8.2 Every Stop of a handed-off session is allowed whatever the backlog: the open-work, unwatched, uncertainty, goal-revision and idle-backlog branches are skipped, and the ten-refusal threshold does not count it. The output is never empty (SSTB-305): `systemMessage` carries `handed off <nonce>: successor <jobId> <running|ended-with-handoff> since <t>, dead time <n>s; goal <id>: <next step, first 120 characters>; <the successor's context-budget line>`. An allowed Stop with a `systemMessage` costs no model turn; the hook's empty-display rule (supervision-hook.sh:2368) is untouched.
- D8.3 The line's sources: the intent and the job record, `resume.json` (D7.3), the goal ledger, and `ContextBudgetLine` with the holder = the successor's runtime session (recorded by the job's SessionStart, adapter/claude.go:268). A source that cannot be read prints `unknown` in its slot; it never blocks.
- D8.4 Recovery (CC8D-106). Owner: the reaper, at the event it already observes, the continuation job's close (reap.go:42-58). For a closed job named by a consumed handoff intent with no later intent naming it as predecessor, it writes `successorEnded {at, reason, progress}` on the intent (progress = a landing under the successor's lineage since `launch.stampedAt`), performs D9.4's exactly-once return when `claimChain` ends in the successor, and, when no progress landed and the dry cap allows, relaunches under the same nonce and state (a new `launch` attempt; the state is still the resume point); with progress it relaunches nothing (the state is stale). The predecessor's next Stop, active or idle, reads the marker: a relaunch keeps it handed off and the line names the new job; no relaunch prints `successor <jobId> ended <with|without> progress; claim returned; this session resumes under S1` and the gate is the ordinary gate again. An idle predecessor needs no Stop for the return or the relaunch; the background waiters were ended by D4.1.
- D8.5 A human message to the pane costs one turn; the predecessor answers in one line naming the successor's job id and relays through U7 when built; until then it says the relay is not built. Nothing here depends on U7.

### D9. Claims move by one authenticated leg; the handed-off session is fenced (U3c-5; SSTB-303; CC8D-101, 106, 110; SR2)

- D9.1 `metasystem goal claim --id <goal> --continue-handoff <nonce>` admits only when the caller is the main process of the job the consumed intent for `<nonce>` names (the delegate-class classification), the intent's goal is `<goal>`, and the ledger's claimant lineage is in the intent's `claimChain` (`PredecessorLineage` at the record, each continuation appended by this leg); it rewrites the claimant's machine, lineage and main id to the caller's, keeps epoch and revision, appends the caller's lineage to `claimChain`, and writes the history row `claim continued from <lineage> under handoff <nonce>`. Any other caller refuses `CLAIM_CONTINUE_NOT_SUCCESSOR`.
- D9.2 Withdrawn: U3c-5 builds before U3c-3 (section 6), so resume never runs without the leg.
- D9.3 The fence (CC8D-110). `goal.VerbRequest` gains `RuntimeSession string`; `runSyncOnly` (goalsync_mutations.go:2260-2292, the one assembly point for claim, release, steal, land-ready, edit and the other mutations) fills it from the caller's announcement `RuntimeSession` (member A) through the parent-pid classification `context handoff` uses (context_verbs.go:213-247); `context handoff` (:139) fills it the same way. Before any write, `goal` consults an injected `SessionHandedOff(runtimeSession) (nonce, jobId string, handedOff bool)` (cmd wires `steward.HandoffMarker`): `running` or `ended-with-handoff` refuses `SESSION_HANDED_OFF nonce=<n> successor=<jobId>`. The refusal is scoped to that runtime session: the predecessor's delegates, other seats and the successor are untouched. An empty token (a runtime without member A) is not fenced; that gap is stated, not hidden.
- D9.4 The return (CC8D-106). `goal.ReturnClaim(req, nonce, chain)` is called in-process by the reaper with `CallerClass: steward`: if the claimant's lineage is the chain's last (the dead successor), it rewrites it to the chain's first (`PredecessorLineage`) with the history row `claim returned to <lineage> after successor <jobId> ended`; if it already is the first, no-op. The intent's `claimReturnedAt` is written after the ledger row; the ledger state is the idempotency, so a crash between the two writes re-runs safely.

### D10. Where 8c.7 plugs in (not decided here; SR9)
