# Instruction Ledger

Standing ledger of instruction changes adopted by retros (`skills/retro/SKILL.md`). Rows enter as `ADOPTED` with `Review by` naming the next retro; that retro replaces the status with a verdict — `KEPT`, `AMENDED`, or `REVERTED`, with reversion the default after two unsupported reviews.
